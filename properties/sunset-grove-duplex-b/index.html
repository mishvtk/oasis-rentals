Property Description

This nearly new duplex, in excellent condition, features an open floor plan with vaulted ceilings, offering 3 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, and a 1-car garage on each side. The kitchen boasts granite counters and stainless steel appliances, overlooking a covered lanai and a large family room. The entire home is tiled with large porcelain tiles. The spacious master suite includes a luxurious bathroom with a large tiled walk-in shower. Additional features include generous-sized bedrooms, a large lanai overlooking a beautiful, private backyard, and hurricane shutters. Perfect for extended families or investors, the property is located in the Seminole Woods subdivision of Palm Coast. The adjacent mirror duplex is also available, with current tenants on a month-to-month basis, allowing the new owner to re-lease at market rates or take possession vacant. Each unit has central electric heating and cooling with a heat pump, and appliances include a dishwasher, disposal, microwave, refrigerator, and electric stove. The property sits on a 0.23-acre lot with a 1-car garage and driveway parking, offering easy access to I-95 and SRD100. Built in 2019 by SEAGATE, this 3,355 sq ft ranch-style duplex is constructed with block and stucco, featuring an architectural shingle roof and underground utilities. No HOA fees.
