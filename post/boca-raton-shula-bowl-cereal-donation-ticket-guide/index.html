Boca Raton Shula Bowl: Cereal Donation Ticket Guide

The FAU Owls host the FIU Panthers in Boca Raton on Saturday, September 19, 2026. Kickoff is scheduled for 6 p.m. at Flagler Credit Union Stadium. This year, the Tackle Hunger drive adds a cereal donation offer: bring an unopened box of cereal to Gate 1 and receive one ticket to a future FAU football game. The offer does not include admission to Saturday’s Shula Bowl, according to the published promotion details.

Thunderstorms are in the forecast, and the exchange takes place outside the stadium entrance. Keep the cereal dry, arrive early and have a separate ticket for Saturday’s game ready on your phone.

Saturday’s Shula Bowl at a glance

Time, place and broadcast

The matchup is at Flagler Credit Union Stadium on the Florida Atlantic University campus in Boca Raton. Kickoff is set for 6 p.m. Saturday, and the game will stream on ESPN+, as confirmed in FIU Athletics’ game preview.

General admission gates normally open an hour and a half before kickoff, putting the standard opening time at 4:30 p.m. Premium gates normally open earlier. Event-specific instructions can override FAU’s regular times, so check the stadium A-to-Z guide and the athletics feed before leaving home.

Read the cereal promotion carefully

The free-ticket headline is easy to misread. Donating cereal at the Shula Bowl earns a ticket for a future FAU football game. It does not replace the ticket needed to enter Saturday’s game.

If you are mainly going for the food drive, you still need lawful access to the Gate 1 collection area and should check with FAU for any late operational update. If you plan to attend the rivalry game, buy or secure Saturday’s ticket separately.

How the Cereal4All ticket exchange works

Bring an unopened box to Gate 1

Cereal4All and Boca Helping Hands are partnering with FAU Athletics for the fifth annual Tackle Hunger drive. The published instructions ask donors to bring an unopened box of cereal to Gate 1 at Flagler Credit Union Stadium during Saturday’s event. Each donated box earns one ticket to a future FAU football game.

  1. Buy boxed cereal and leave its factory packaging sealed.
  2. Protect the box inside a reusable waterproof tote while walking from your car or rideshare stop.
  3. Take the donation to Gate 1 before entering the stadium.
  4. Complete the exchange with event staff and keep any ticket instructions they provide.
  5. Return the reusable tote to your vehicle if it does not meet the stadium bag policy.

What the announcement does not promise

The promotion notice does not name a required brand, flavor or box size. It also does not publish a household limit beyond saying that donors receive one future-game ticket per cereal box. The stated offer specifically calls for an unopened box, so do not assume that a bag of cereal, an opened family-size box or another breakfast item will qualify.

No separate advance redemption deadline is listed. The exchange is described as taking place at Saturday’s Shula Bowl. Arriving after the game or dropping cereal at an unrelated location should not be treated as another way to claim the ticket. If a clarification could affect your purchase, contact the organizers through the Boca Helping Hands contact page or the FAU ticket office before game day.

A separate option for registered volunteers

Boca Helping Hands volunteers may request a discount code for a free ticket to Saturday’s game by emailing erika@bocahelpinghands.org, according to the promotion announcement. This volunteer benefit is separate from the cereal-box exchange. Allow time to receive the code before kickoff.

FAU versus FIU: what first-time attendees should know

A South Florida rivalry with a shared football connection

The Shula Bowl is named for former Miami Dolphins coach Don Shula. Its football connection comes through the schools’ founding coaches: FAU’s Howard Schnellenberger worked with Shula, while FIU’s Don Strock played for him. The schools are 46 miles apart, and Saturday’s game is the rivalry’s twenty-fourth meeting, according to FIU Athletics.

FAU leads the series 17-6, although FIU won the previous meeting 38-28. Both teams enter Saturday at 1-1. For a newcomer, the evening is best understood as a close-to-home rivalry between two South Florida teams.

What the stadium atmosphere is like

Expect student tailgating, school colors and a noticeable group of visiting fans coming north from Miami. FAU’s traditional Owl Walk takes place approximately two and a half hours before kickoff. The team arrives at Lot 5 and enters through Gate 5. With the scheduled kickoff, the usual walk would be near 3:30 p.m., but that is a planning estimate. Confirm it in the game-day announcement.

Wear breathable clothing and shoes that can handle a long campus walk. Stadium lights may use blackout or pulsating effects. FAU directs guests who are sensitive to those effects to the first-aid staff near Section 112 by Gate 6.

Check these stadium rules before leaving home

Have your ticket ready on your phone

FAU Athletics uses mobile tickets. Add each ticket to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet before reaching campus instead of depending on cellular service at the gate. Turn up the screen brightness for scanning, and make sure every person in the party can present an individual barcode.

The stadium does not allow ordinary re-entry after a ticket has been scanned. If an emergency requires you to leave, FAU directs guests to the ticket office at Gate 1. Before entering, make one last check for medication, phone battery and rain gear.

Follow the clear-bag measurements

Permitted clear plastic, vinyl or PVC bags may be no larger than 12 by 6 by 12 inches. The stadium also accepts a one-gallon clear freezer bag or a small clutch no larger than 6.5 by 4.5 inches. Medically necessary items are allowed after inspection.

Backpacks, purses, fanny packs, coolers, computer bags and drawstring bags are prohibited. Umbrellas are also on FAU’s prohibited-items list, an important detail with rain in the forecast. A compact poncho in an approved clear bag is a practical choice.

Leave these items in the rental

  • Umbrellas, coolers and outside drinks
  • Backpacks, purses and noncompliant totes
  • Camera cases, tripods and lenses longer than five inches
  • Seatbacks, although a cushion without a chair back is allowed
  • Noisemakers, poles, sticks and weapons

FAU makes exceptions for outside food or drink needed for medical or childcare reasons. If that applies to your household, leave extra time for inspection at the gate.

Parking, rideshare and campus traffic

Driving from Boca Raton

The stadium is on FAU’s Boca Raton campus, west of Interstate 95 and north of Glades Road. Drivers can approach the campus from Glades Road or Spanish River Boulevard, although event controls may restrict internal streets. FAU maintains game-day Waze links for specific lots, which are more useful than entering only the stadium name.

FAU says football parking lots open four hours before kickoff. The university’s general stadium guide lists game-day cash parking at $20 per vehicle, although parking assignments and prices can change by season or event. Confirm the 2026 map and price through the ticket office before using that figure in your budget.

A practical arrival time

For a 6 p.m. kickoff, I would aim to reach the assigned lot by 4 p.m. This is a planning recommendation, not a promised travel time. It leaves time to walk, deliver the cereal at Gate 1, clear security and wait out a passing downpour without missing the opening drive.

Check FL511 before departure for crashes or construction on Interstate 95 and nearby state roads. Glades Road gets backed up by ordinary weekend shopping traffic, and stadium arrivals add more turning traffic at the campus entrances.

Using a rideshare

FAU designates Palm Beach Plaza near the Main Administration Building as the Uber, Lyft and taxi drop-off point. Use “Palm Bch Plz @ Main Admin Bldg” as the destination supplied in the stadium guide instead of choosing an improvised curb beside the stadium.

Save the pickup location before the game. Afterward, walk back to the designated area and expect demand to increase as the crowd leaves. If heavy rain delays play, contact your driver after stadium officials provide instructions and you know when pedestrian routes have reopened.

Saturday’s Boca Raton weather plan

Heat and storms are in the forecast

The National Weather Service forecast for Boca Raton, issued Thursday morning, calls for temperatures in the upper 80s and an 80 percent chance of showers and thunderstorms on Saturday. Conditions can still change before kickoff, so refresh the point forecast on Saturday afternoon.

Late-September humidity can make the walk from a distant lot feel harder than the thermometer suggests. Drink water before leaving home, eat before entering and use sunscreen during the afternoon. Leave the umbrella at home because stadium rules prohibit it.

What to do if there is lightning

A poncho will help in a rain shower. If there is thunder, follow official instructions. Do not wait beneath a lone tree, metal tailgate canopy or light pole when lightning approaches, and do not try to cross a flooded campus drive.

FAU says the public-address announcer will provide instructions for severe weather and game delays. Keep phone alerts enabled and follow stadium staff if a route is closed. Ordinary ticket re-entry is prohibited, so leaving during a delay could end your evening.

Keep the donation usable

Thin cardboard cereal boxes can be ruined on the walk from the parking lot to Gate 1. Put the unopened box inside a waterproof shopping bag, deliver it before security and return the empty shopping bag to the car if it is opaque or oversize. A soaked box will not help the pantry or make the exchange easier.

A renter-friendly game-day checklist

Bring

  • Your separate mobile ticket for Saturday, downloaded to the phone wallet
  • An unopened cereal box for the future-game ticket exchange
  • A charged phone and compact battery pack
  • A lightweight poncho inside an approved clear bag
  • Sunscreen, required medication and a hand-size clutch if needed
  • Your parking pass, lot directions or saved rideshare destination

Confirm before you lock the door

  1. Recheck the kickoff and weather pages.
  2. Verify that every ticket barcode opens without a data connection.
  3. Measure the clear bag instead of judging it by sight.
  4. Look at FL511 and the FAU parking map.
  5. Photograph your parking row or pin the rideshare point.
  6. Make sure the cereal seal and cardboard box are intact.

Why the cereal drive matters locally

A game-day donation for an everyday need

Boca Helping Hands lists cereal, pasta and rice among the items that are always needed in its community FAQ. Cereal is shelf-stable, familiar to families and easy for game guests to donate without a complicated collection process.

Action on the field will also produce more support. Robes Law has pledged $26 to Boca Helping Hands each time FAU tackles FIU for a loss, commemorating FAU’s twenty-sixth football season, according to the event announcement. A portion of tickets purchased through the promotion’s designated sales link also benefits the organization.

Supporting the organization after Saturday

If you cannot attend, an unrelated drop-off will not necessarily qualify for the ticket exchange. Visit Boca Helping Hands’ community FAQ for current donation guidance, commonly needed items and ways to contact the organization before delivering food. The donation can still be useful even when no ticket is attached.

FAQ

When and where is the 2026 Shula Bowl?

FAU hosts FIU at Flagler Credit Union Stadium in Boca Raton on Saturday, September 19, 2026. Kickoff is scheduled for 6 p.m.

Does one cereal box get me into Saturday’s game?

No. Each unopened cereal box donated at Gate 1 earns one ticket to a future FAU football game under the published offer. Saturday’s Shula Bowl requires a separate ticket.

What cereal qualifies for the ticket exchange?

The announcement calls for an unopened box of cereal. It does not list required brands, flavors or sizes. Keep the factory seal intact and ask the organizers in advance if your item differs from that description.

Where do I take the cereal donation?

Take it to Gate 1 at Flagler Credit Union Stadium during Saturday’s event. Protect the cardboard from rain while walking from the parking or rideshare area.

What bags are allowed inside the stadium?

FAU permits clear plastic, vinyl or PVC bags up to 12 by 6 by 12 inches, one-gallon clear freezer bags and small clutches up to 6.5 by 4.5 inches. Medical exceptions are inspected at the gate.

What should I do about Saturday’s storm forecast?

Refresh the National Weather Service forecast before leaving, pack a poncho instead of an umbrella, hydrate early and follow stadium announcements if lightning delays entry or play.
