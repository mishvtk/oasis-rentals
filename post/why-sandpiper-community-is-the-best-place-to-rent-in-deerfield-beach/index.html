Why Sandpiper Community is the Best Place to Rent in Deerfield Beach

Sandpiper Pointe in Deerfield Beach, Florida, is a premier gated community offering luxury townhomes that combine modern living with coastal charm. Developed by Toll Brothers, this community features 201 two-story townhomes, each with three bedrooms and spacious layouts ranging from approximately 1,831 to 1,933 square feet.


Sandpiper Community is the Best Place to Rent

Situated just minutes from the pristine beaches of Deerfield Beach, Sandpiper Pointe provides residents with easy access to coastal recreation. Its strategic location near major highways ensures convenient commutes to neighboring cities and attractions, enhancing its appeal for both professionals and families.



Modern Amenities

Residents of Sandpiper Pointe enjoy a range of amenities designed for comfort and leisure, including:

  • Community Pool: A resort-style swimming pool for relaxation and socializing.

  • Fitness Center: A well-equipped gym catering to various workout routines.

  • Clubhouse: A communal space for events and gatherings.

  • Playground: A safe area for children to play and interact.

These facilities contribute to a vibrant community atmosphere, promoting an active and engaging lifestyle.



Contemporary Home Features

Each townhome in Sandpiper Pointe is thoughtfully designed with:

  • Open-Concept Layouts: Enhancing space and natural light.

  • Gourmet Kitchens: Equipped with stainless steel appliances and quartz countertops.

  • Private Patios: Ideal for outdoor relaxation and entertainment.

  • Energy-Efficient Systems: Ensuring sustainability and reduced utility costs.

These features provide residents with a comfortable and modern living environment.



Proximity to Attractions

Beyond the community's amenities, residents benefit from close proximity to:

  • Beaches: Approximately 3.5 miles from Deerfield Beach, offering sun, sand, and surf.

  • Shopping and Dining: Nearby Boca Raton and local Deerfield Beach establishments provide diverse options.

  • Recreational Activities: Access to parks, golf courses, and cultural venues.

This convenient access enhances the quality of life for Sandpiper Pointe residents.


Residents have expressed high satisfaction with the community's design, amenities, and location, noting the quality construction and responsive management as key benefits.
