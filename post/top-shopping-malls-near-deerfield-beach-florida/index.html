Top Shopping Malls Near Deerfield Beach, Florida

Deerfield Beach, Florida, is not just known for its beautiful beaches and vibrant dining scene—it's also conveniently located near some of South Florida’s best shopping mall. Whether you’re in the mood for luxury brands, unique boutiques, or just a leisurely shopping day, the malls near Deerfield Beach offer something for everyone. Here’s a guide to the top shopping malls near Deerfield Beach.


The Galleria at Fort Lauderdale

Located about 30 minutes south of Deerfield Beach, The Galleria at Fort Lauderdale is a premier shopping destination offering a blend of luxury and high-end retail. The mall features major department stores like Macy’s and Dillard’s, as well as designer shops such as Michael Kors, Coach, and Williams-Sonoma. After shopping, you can enjoy a meal at one of the mall's many restaurants, including Seasons 52 and The Capital Grille.


Address: 2414 E Sunrise Blvd, Fort Lauderdale, FL 33304

Highlights: High-end brands, dining options, centrally located.

Source:The Galleria at Fort Lauderdale



Town Center at Boca Raton

Just a short drive from Deerfield Beach, the Town Center at Boca Raton is a luxury shopping mall that offers an impressive selection of stores. It’s home to upscale retailers like Neiman Marcus, Saks Fifth Avenue, and Nordstrom, alongside popular brands such as Apple, Tesla, and Lululemon. The mall also features a variety of dining options, from casual eateries to fine dining, making it a great spot for both shopping and dining experiences. Town Center at Boca Raton is rapidly expanding, with many new shops opening in the past year such as Alo, and Marni and with many luxury store locations scheduled to open in the near future like Moncler, Dolce & Gabanna, and Panerai.


Address: 6000 Glades Rd, Boca Raton, FL 33431

Highlights: Luxury brands, large selection of stores, upscale dining.

Source:Town Center at Boca Raton


Sawgrass Mills

A bit farther out, but worth the drive, is Sawgrass Mills in Sunrise, about 40 minutes west of Deerfield Beach. Sawgrass Mills is the largest outlet and value retail shopping destination in the United States. People travel here from all around the world to get some of the best deals on designer, as well as casual apparel. The mall offers a wide variety of stores, including major outlets like Nike, Adidas, and Calvin Klein, as well as luxury brand outlets such as Prada and Gucci. In addition to its vast selection of stores, Sawgrass Mills features numerous dining options and entertainment venues like an IMAX theater.


Address: 12801 W Sunrise Blvd, Sunrise, FL 33323

Highlights: Largest outlet mall, luxury brand outlets, entertainment options.

Source:Sawgrass Mills



Mizner Park

Mizner Park in Boca Raton is more than just a shopping mall—it’s a cultural and entertainment center. Located just 15 minutes north of Deerfield Beach, Mizner Park offers a mix of retail, dining, and cultural attractions. The outdoor mall features boutique shops, art galleries, and specialty stores alongside restaurants and a cinema. Mizner Park also hosts various events throughout the year, making it a vibrant destination for shopping and entertainment. If you are a fan of art, make sure to visit the art museum in Mizner Park as well, offering a rich selection of modern art!


Address: 327 Plaza Real, Boca Raton, FL 33432

Highlights: Outdoor shopping, cultural attractions, boutique stores.

Source:Mizner Park



Pompano Citi Centre

Closer to Deerfield Beach, Pompano Citi Centre offers a more laid-back shopping experience. This open-air shopping mall features a range of stores, from big-box retailers like Macy’s and JCPenney to smaller specialty shops. It’s a great option for everyday shopping needs or a quick retail therapy session without venturing too far from Deerfield Beach.


Address: 1955 N Federal Hwy, Pompano Beach, FL 33062

Highlights: Convenient location, big-box stores, relaxed atmosphere.

Source:Pompano Citi Centre



Top Shopping Malls Near Deerfield: Conclusion

Whether you’re looking for high-end fashion, outlet deals, or a unique shopping experience, the malls near Deerfield Beach have you covered. From the luxurious Town Center at Boca Raton to the expansive Sawgrass Mills, these shopping destinations offer a variety of retail experiences within a short drive. So, whether you’re a local or a visitor, be sure to check out these top shopping spots for your next retail adventure.
