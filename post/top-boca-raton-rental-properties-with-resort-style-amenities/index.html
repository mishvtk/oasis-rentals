Top Boca Raton Rental Properties with Resort-Style Amenities

Boca Raton, Florida, is renowned for its upscale living and resort-style amenities, making it a sought-after destination for those seeking luxury rental properties. Here are some top rental options in Boca Raton that offer exceptional resort-style amenities:

1. Nuvo Boca

Nuvo Boca stands as a premier luxury rental community in Boca Raton, Florida, offering residents an unparalleled living experience. Nestled between Highway 845 and Military Trail, this beautifully landscaped neighborhood combines the tranquility of suburban life with the conveniences of urban amenities.

Spacious and Modern Residences

Prospective residents can choose from expansive three to five-bedroom homes, with sizes ranging from 1,640 to over 2,700 square feet. Each residence is thoughtfully designed with modern interiors, featuring:

  • Gourmet kitchens equipped with stainless steel appliances, range hoods, custom quartz countertops, ceramic tile backsplashes, and soft-close cabinetry.

  • Wood-grain porcelain tile flooring in main areas, luxury tiled bathrooms with frameless shower enclosures, and Moen Genta faucets.

  • Smart Home technology, impact windows, walk-in closets, full-size washers and dryers, and select homes offering fenced yards and EV charging ports.

Resort-Style Community Amenities

Nuvo Boca's commitment to luxury extends beyond individual homes, providing a wealth of community amenities designed for relaxation, recreation, and social engagement:

  • A sprawling 3+ acre amenity center featuring an expansive resort-style pool with lap lanes, beach entry, and poolside cabanas.

  • A premier fitness center outfitted with top-of-the-line Matrix equipment, complemented by a yoga/meditation room and an outdoor yoga lawn.

  • Outdoor recreational facilities including a putting green, basketball court, pickleball courts, a multi-purpose 40-yard lined field, and walking trails.

  • Additional conveniences such as a dog park, children's playground, hammock and Zen areas, an outdoor grilling pavilion with covered dining and lounge spaces, and a clubhouse ideal for social events.

Prime Location and Accessibility

Situated in a picturesque neighborhood, Nuvo Boca offers residents easy access to fine dining, retail, and entertainment options. The community's proximity to major highways ensures that the vibrant cities of Fort Lauderdale and Miami are just a short drive away, making it an ideal location for those seeking both serenity and connectivity.

Nuvo Boca exemplifies luxury living in Boca Raton, seamlessly blending sophisticated home designs with resort-style amenities and a prime location.

2. 10X Boca Raton

10X Boca Raton redefines luxury apartment living in Boca Raton, Florida, by offering residents an array of resort-style amenities designed for comfort, wellness, and entertainment.

Community Amenities

Residents at 10X Boca Raton enjoy a host of upscale community features, including:

  • Wellness Facilities: A state-of-the-art fitness center equipped with top-of-the-line equipment and a cycling room, complemented by indoor and outdoor yoga spaces, steam rooms, and massage rooms.

  • Resort-Style Pool & Spa: A heated saltwater beach-entry pool and spa, indoor plunge pool, and a blowout bar for a comprehensive relaxation experience.

  • Entertainment Spaces: An active courtyard featuring a fire pit, 9-hole putting green, lounge area, BBQ grill area, and a life-size chess set. Additional amenities include a game room, poker room, and a movie theater.

  • Pet-Friendly Amenities: A dog park and an indoor, air-conditioned pet spa cater to residents' four-legged companions.

  • Convenience Features: A Lavazza coffee lounge, business center, clubhouse with Wi-Fi in common areas, 24-hour air-conditioned Luxor package room, air-conditioned storage units, bike storage, gated access, parking garage, and LEED-certified building standards.

Apartment Features

Each apartment is thoughtfully designed with modern features, including:

  • 100% tile flooring.

  • White subway tile backsplash in the kitchen.

  • White quartz countertops.

  • Golf course views (in select units).

  • Separate glass showers (in select units).

  • Double sink bathroom vanities (in select units).

  • Private yards (in select units).

  • In-unit washer and dryer.

  • Air conditioning and ceiling fans.

  • Nest electronic thermostats.

  • Patio or balcony.

  • Stainless steel appliances.

  • Window coverings.

These features and amenities collectively create an elevated living experience at 10X Boca Raton, making it a premier choice for those seeking luxury and convenience in Boca Raton.

3. Alina Residences

Alina Residences redefines modern luxury living in downtown Boca Raton, Florida, offering residents an exceptional blend of sophisticated design and resort-style amenities. Spanning a nine-acre private community, Alina comprises three distinct towers—ALINA 200, ALINA 210, and ALINA 220—each thoughtfully designed to cater to diverse lifestyles.

Resort-Inspired Amenities

Alina Residences provides an extensive array of amenities focused on wellness, relaxation, and social engagement:

  • Rooftop Pools: Each tower features its own pool area, including a rooftop pool and sun deck with cabanas and shaded lounges, offering panoramic views of the Boca Raton Resort & Club and the city skyline.

  • Elite Performance Fitness Centers: State-of-the-art fitness centers equipped with cardio, strength training, and interactive workout equipment, complemented by private yoga rooms and holistic wellness retreats offering live and on-demand classes.

  • Spa Facilities: Residents can unwind in luxurious spa facilities that include treatment rooms, relaxation areas, salt rooms, steam rooms, saunas, and private locker rooms.

  • Social and Entertainment Spaces: The community boasts indoor-outdoor club rooms with bars, lounges, private dining areas, catering kitchens, game and card rooms, and theater/screening rooms, fostering a vibrant social atmosphere.

  • Outdoor Amenities: Beautifully landscaped meditation gardens, outdoor yoga spaces, dog parks, and entertainment decks with bocce courts, putting greens, and barbecue areas enhance the outdoor living experience.

Prime Location

Situated in the heart of downtown Boca Raton, Alina Residences offers unparalleled access to the city's vibrant cultural scene, upscale dining options, luxury shopping boutiques, and live entertainment venues. The community's strategic location ensures that residents are just steps away from world-class cultural and culinary destinations, making it an ideal choice for those seeking a dynamic urban lifestyle.

Alina Residences epitomizes modern luxury living, seamlessly blending elegant design with resort-style amenities and a prime downtown location, catering to discerning individuals seeking an elevated lifestyle in Boca Raton.

4. Aragon

Aragon is an exclusive oceanfront condominium located on South Ocean Boulevard in Boca Raton, Florida, offering residents a luxurious beachfront lifestyle with a suite of high-end amenities.

Residences and Design

Constructed in 1995, Aragon comprises 41 elegantly appointed condominiums across three distinct towers. Each residence features gourmet kitchens with high-end appliances, floor-to-ceiling windows offering panoramic views of the Atlantic Ocean and Intracoastal Waterway, and spacious layouts ranging from 2,100 to 8,500 square feet.

Amenities

Residents of Aragon enjoy a range of resort-style amenities designed to enhance comfort and leisure:

  • Private Beach Access: Direct access to a pristine private beach allows for serene seaside experiences.

  • Oceanfront Pool and Spa: A beachfront pool area provides a relaxing environment with stunning ocean views.

  • Fitness Center and Sauna: A state-of-the-art fitness facility equipped with modern equipment and sauna facilities supports residents' wellness routines.

  • Concierge and Valet Services: Personalized concierge services and valet parking cater to residents' needs, enhancing convenience and luxury.

  • Private Elevators: Each unit is accessible via private elevator, ensuring privacy and exclusivity.

  • 24-Hour Security: Comprehensive security measures, including 24/7 security personnel, provide peace of mind for residents.

Location

Situated just south of Camino Real, Aragon's prime location offers easy access to Boca Raton's vibrant cultural scene, upscale dining options, and luxury shopping venues. The proximity to parks and recreational facilities further enhances the appeal for those seeking a balanced lifestyle.

Aragon presents a harmonious blend of exclusivity, luxury, and beachfront serenity, making it an ideal choice for discerning individuals seeking a prestigious residence in Boca Raton.

5. Mizner Grand

Mizner Grand is a distinguished luxury condominium complex nestled within the prestigious Mizner Village on the grounds of the renowned Boca Raton Resort and Beach Club in Boca Raton, Florida. This exclusive enclave offers residents expansive residences complemented by an array of resort-style amenities, epitomizing upscale waterfront living.

Residences and Design

Comprising two 12-story towers, Mizner Grand houses 138 residences featuring 3- to 5-bedroom floor plans. These spacious units range from approximately 4,000 to over 8,400 square feet, boasting open layouts, gourmet kitchens, 9-foot ceilings, and private elevators. Expansive balconies provide panoramic views of Lake Boca Raton, the Intracoastal Waterway, the Atlantic Ocean, and the cityscape. Select residences further offer private pools, enhancing the luxurious living experience.

Resort-Style Amenities

Mizner Grand offers an extensive suite of amenities designed to cater to the discerning resident:

  • Waterfront Heated Pools: Two lakefront heated pools provide serene spots for relaxation and leisure.

  • Fitness Centers: State-of-the-art fitness facilities equipped with modern equipment support residents' wellness routines.

  • Steam Rooms and Saunas: These facilities offer spaces for relaxation and rejuvenation.

  • Club Room and Billiard Room: Elegant spaces designed for social gatherings and entertainment.

  • Business Center and Meeting Room: Facilities equipped to accommodate professional needs and conferences.

  • Concierge and Valet Services: Personalized services enhance convenience and luxury for residents.

  • 24-Hour Security: Comprehensive security measures, including 24/7 personnel, ensure a safe living environment.

Proximity to Cultural Attractions and Dining

Situated within Mizner Village, Mizner Grand offers residents easy access to Boca Raton's vibrant cultural scene, including theaters, art galleries, and museums. The area is also renowned for its fine dining establishments, providing a diverse culinary landscape that caters to various palates. This strategic location enhances the luxurious living experience, blending convenience with exclusivity.

Mizner Grand stands as a testament to luxury waterfront living, offering expansive residences, resort-style amenities, and proximity to cultural and dining attractions, making it an ideal choice for those seeking an upscale lifestyle in Boca Raton.
