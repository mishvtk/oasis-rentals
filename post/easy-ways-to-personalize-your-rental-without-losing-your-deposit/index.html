Easy Ways to Personalize Your Rental Without Losing Your Deposit

Renting a home doesn’t mean you have to live in a generic, impersonal space. Even if you can’t tear down walls or paint bold colors, there are plenty of creative, renter-friendly ways to add character and comfort to your space—without risking your security deposit. Here's how to personalize your rental while staying on your landlord’s good side.

Use Removable Wallpaper and Decals to Transform Your Rental

Renting a home doesn’t mean you have to settle for plain, uninspired walls. One of the easiest and most impactful ways to personalize your space is by using removable wallpaper and wall decals. These peel-and-stick options are true game changers for renters who want style without risking their security deposit.

Available in virtually endless patterns, colors, and textures—think modern geometrics, vintage florals, or even realistic faux brick—removable wallpaper allows you to create a statement wall or refresh an entire room without any permanent commitment. Wall decals are equally versatile, perfect for adding small accents, quotes, or playful designs in kids’ rooms.

When it’s time to move out, removable wallpaper and decals peel off cleanly, leaving your walls in their original condition. This makes them ideal for apartments, rental homes, and even dorms.


Tip: Before applying, always test a small hidden area first. This helps ensure the adhesive won’t leave behind sticky residue or pull off paint when removed.

With a little creativity, peel-and-stick wallpaper and decals can instantly elevate your rental’s look—no paintbrush or landlord approval required.

Switch Out Hardware to Instantly Refresh Your Rental

If you’re looking for a simple, renter-friendly way to elevate your kitchen or bathroom, consider switching out the hardware. Swapping cabinet knobs, drawer pulls, and even faucet handles is a fast, affordable upgrade that can dramatically change the look and feel of your space without any permanent alterations.

Stylish hardware options are available in every aesthetic imaginable—sleek matte black for a modern vibe, warm brass for a vintage touch, or classic brushed nickel for a timeless feel. Even small details like coordinating hinges or towel hooks can pull the whole room together.

One of the biggest benefits of updating hardware is that it requires minimal tools and effort. Most knobs and pulls can be replaced with just a screwdriver, making it a perfect weekend project.


Tip: Be sure to keep all the original hardware safely stored in a labeled bag or box. When it’s time to move out, simply reinstall the old pieces to restore everything to its original condition and avoid any deductions from your security deposit.

With just a few simple swaps, you can transform your rental’s standard fixtures into something that feels more personal, polished, and uniquely yours.

Add Rugs and Layer Textiles to Make Your Rental Feel Like Home

One of the most effective ways to personalize a rental is by adding rugs and layering textiles. These elements not only inject color, texture, and personality into a space but also help cover floors that may have seen better days.

Rugs are especially powerful design tools. A large area rug can define open-concept spaces, anchor furniture groupings, and instantly make a room feel more inviting. Choose a bold pattern to make a statement or a neutral tone to create a calming backdrop. If you have wall-to-wall carpeting you don’t love, layering a rug on top can help disguise worn spots or dated colors.

To enhance the cozy factor, incorporate additional textiles throughout your home. Soft throws draped over the sofa, decorative cushions piled high on chairs, and floor-length curtains framing your windows all work together to create a warm, cohesive look. The beauty of these pieces is that they’re completely non-permanent—easy to swap out with the seasons or take with you when you move.


Tip: To tie everything together, stick to a consistent color palette or mix complementary textures like knits, linen, and velvet.

With thoughtfully chosen rugs and layered textiles, you can transform any rental into a stylish retreat that feels like your own—no renovations required.

Upgrade Light Fixtures and Bulbs to Brighten Your Rental Good lighting has the power to completely transform a room—but many rentals come with dated or uninspired fixtures. The good news is, upgrading your light fixtures and bulbs is an easy, renter-friendly project that can instantly elevate your space.

If your lease allows, consider swapping out old ceiling fixtures for something more contemporary—think minimalist flush mounts, mid-century pendants, or elegant chandeliers. You don’t have to rewire the entire room; many modern light fixtures are designed for straightforward installation. For even less commitment, try plug-in pendant lights or wall-mounted sconces with cord covers—these provide stylish illumination without any hardwiring at all.

If replacing fixtures isn’t an option, simply upgrading your light bulbs can make a big impact. Swap harsh, cold bulbs for warmer tones that create a softer, more inviting atmosphere. Or, for maximum convenience and flexibility, install smart bulbs that can be dimmed, brightened, or color-adjusted right from your phone or voice assistant.


Reminder: Keep any original fixtures and bulbs safely stored. Before you move out, reinstall everything exactly as you found it to avoid deductions from your security deposit.

With just a few simple lighting upgrades, you can brighten your rental, showcase your style, and make every room feel like home.

Use Temporary Backsplashes to Instantly Refresh Kitchens and Bathrooms

If you’re tired of bland or outdated surfaces in your rental, temporary backsplashes are a stylish and hassle-free way to upgrade your kitchen or bathroom without making permanent changes.

Peel-and-stick backsplash tiles have come a long way in recent years. Available in an impressive range of styles—from sleek subway tiles to eye-catching mosaics and realistic faux stone—they offer the look of a professionally installed backsplash without the commitment or cost. Many renters are amazed by how realistic these products feel and how quickly they can transform a space.

Installation is simple: just clean your wall surface, measure and cut the tiles to fit, peel off the backing, and press them into place. Most varieties are designed to resist moisture and heat, making them perfect for behind sinks, stoves, or bathroom vanities.

When it’s time to move out, removal is just as easy. Carefully peel the tiles away, and any leftover adhesive can usually be wiped clean without damaging the wall underneath.


Tip: Before starting, always check your lease and test a small area to ensure the adhesive is compatible with your wall paint or finish.

With peel-and-stick backsplash tiles, you can elevate your rental’s look, showcase your style, and enjoy a space that feels fresh and modern—no renovations or permanent changes required.


Hang Art Without Holes to Personalize Your Rental One of the quickest ways to make a rental feel like home is by displaying your favorite artwork, photos, and decorative pieces. But many landlords prohibit holes in the walls, leaving renters worried about losing their deposit. Fortunately, you don’t need nails or screws to create a gallery wall—today’s adhesive products make it simple to hang art without any damage.

Command strips, adhesive hooks, and picture hanging systems are all designed to hold frames, mirrors, and even lightweight shelves securely. These clever solutions come in a variety of sizes and weight capacities, so you can hang everything from a small photo to a large canvas.

Installation is fast and tool-free:

  • Just clean the wall surface to ensure good adhesion.

  • Attach the adhesive strip or hook to your frame or decor.

  • Press firmly against the wall for the recommended time.

When it’s time to remove them, most products peel off cleanly—without leaving residue, damaging paint, or requiring any patching.


Tip: Always check weight ratings before you hang heavier pieces, and consider using multiple strips or hooks for extra security.

With the right products, you can transform blank walls into a personal gallery and create a polished, inviting space—no holes, tools, or worries about repairs when you move out.

Incorporate Freestanding Storage to Maximize Space and Style

Rentals often come with limited built-in storage, making it tricky to keep your home organized without sacrificing style. The solution? Freestanding storage pieces that add function, showcase your personality, and protect your security deposit.

Bookshelves, cube organizers, and standalone wardrobes are some of the most versatile options. A tall bookshelf can act as both storage and a room divider in a studio apartment. Cube organizers are perfect for stashing baskets, displaying decor, or corralling everyday essentials in living rooms and bedrooms. Freestanding wardrobes and garment racks are lifesavers when closet space is lacking.

The best part? Because these pieces don’t attach to the walls, you won’t have to patch holes or worry about damaging finishes when you move out. They can be easily reconfigured as your needs change, or taken with you to your next home.


Tip: Choose storage solutions that blend seamlessly with your decor style—think warm wood finishes for a cozy look, sleek metal frames for modern spaces, or colorful cubbies for a playful vibe. Adding bins, baskets, and decorative boxes can also help keep things organized and visually appealing.

With freestanding storage, you can declutter your rental, create designated zones, and make the most of every inch—without any permanent modifications.

Bring in Greenery to Liven Up Your Rental

Nothing transforms a rental quite like the natural beauty of indoor plants. Whether you have a green thumb or prefer low-maintenance options, bringing in greenery instantly makes a space feel more vibrant, welcoming, and lived-in.

Plants can soften hard lines, purify the air, and add a pop of color to any room. Large potted trees, like fiddle leaf figs or rubber plants, create a striking focal point in living areas. Smaller potted plants—such as succulents, snake plants, or pothos—work beautifully on shelves, countertops, and windowsills.

Trailing plants are especially renter-friendly because you can hang them using removable adhesive hooks or ceiling hooks with tension rods. Let them cascade down bookcases, drape over curtain rods, or fill empty corners with lush greenery.

Stylish planters in ceramic, woven baskets, or modern metal finishes add even more personality. If you’re worried about maintenance, faux plants have come a long way—they look impressively real and require no watering at all.


Tip: Before hanging anything heavy, always check the weight limit on adhesive hooks to avoid damage when it’s time to remove them.

With thoughtfully placed greenery, you can breathe life into your rental, create a calming atmosphere, and enjoy a home that feels fresh and full of character—without making any permanent changes.

Style Your Entryway to Make a Welcoming First Impression

Your entryway sets the tone for your entire home—and even in a rental, you can create a space that feels inviting and pulled together. With just a few thoughtful touches, you can style your entryway beautifully without any permanent changes.

Start with a chic doormat that reflects your personality, whether that’s a playful greeting, a classic stripe, or a minimalist design. A doormat not only keeps dirt out but also makes your entrance feel intentional and welcoming.

Next, add a coat rack or wall hooks. Freestanding coat racks are perfect for renters since they don’t require drilling into the wall. If you’re short on floor space, removable adhesive hooks offer a damage-free solution for hanging jackets, bags, and hats.

Consider incorporating a small bench or a slim console table. A bench provides a convenient spot to sit while putting on shoes, and you can tuck baskets underneath for extra storage. A narrow table creates space to drop keys and mail or display a small lamp and decorative accents.

Finish the look with a mirror to visually expand the area and make last-minute checks before you head out the door.


Tip: Keep clutter at bay by using trays, baskets, or bins to corral everyday essentials in one neat, stylish spot.

With just a few renter-friendly updates, you can transform even the simplest entry into a functional, beautiful space that makes every homecoming feel special.

Personalize With Accessories to Make Your Rental Feel Like Home

When it comes to making a rental truly yours, decorative accessories are the secret ingredient. These finishing touches can transform a plain, neutral space into a warm, personal retreat—without any need for permanent changes.

Start by layering in items that tell your story. Framed photos of family, friends, and favorite places instantly make a room feel more personal and comforting. You can display them on shelves, propped on consoles, or arranged in a gallery on a picture ledge (no nails required).

Candles and diffusers add both beauty and a signature scent that makes your home feel inviting. Cluster candles of varying heights on a coffee table or tuck them onto a bookshelf for a cozy glow in the evenings.

Books and magazines aren’t just for reading—they also make fantastic styling props. Stack a few on side tables or display them in open shelving to showcase your interests.

Finally, don’t forget to sprinkle in your favorite objects and collected treasures—art pieces, travel souvenirs, or vintage finds. These are the details that create a layered, lived-in look and remind you every day that this space is yours.


Tip: Stick to a consistent color palette or theme to keep your accessories feeling cohesive rather than cluttered.

With thoughtful accessorizing, you can easily turn any rental into a space that feels uniquely you—and best of all, everything can come with you when you move.
