Why More Families Are Choosing to Rent in Palm Coast

Palm Coast, Florida, has become an increasingly popular destination for families seeking rental homes. Several factors contribute to this trend, including the area's family-friendly amenities, affordable living costs, and a surge in vacation rental properties.

Family-Friendly Amenities Palm Coast and the Flagler Beaches in Florida offer a wealth of family-friendly amenities, making it an ideal destination for those seeking a blend of relaxation and adventure. With 19 miles of pristine, uncrowded beaches, families have ample space to unwind and play. Notably, Flagler Beach is among the few Florida beaches that welcome dogs, allowing the entire family, including pets, to enjoy the shoreline together.

Beyond the beaches, the area boasts numerous parks and trails suitable for family outings. The Betty Steflik Memorial Preserve offers 217 acres of coastal scrub and marshes, featuring boardwalks where visitors can observe gopher tortoises and migratory birds. For dog owners, the James F. Holland Memorial Park provides a 1.5-acre dog park with separate areas for large and small dogs, along with wash stations to cool off after playtime.

Water activities are plentiful, with options like boogie boarding, body surfing, and kayaking. Local shops such as Z Wave Surf Shop and Flagler Board Shack offer equipment rentals and lessons suitable for all ages. The area's extensive trail system, featuring paths through historic oaks and natural preserves, provides opportunities for biking and hiking adventures.


Dining with the family, including furry friends, is convenient with numerous pet-friendly restaurants. Establishments like Java Joint Beachside Grill and High Tides at Snack Jack offer outdoor seating where families can enjoy meals with ocean views.

Accommodations are diverse, ranging from oceanfront properties like Si Como No Inn to campgrounds such as Beverly Beach Camptown Resort, which offers RV campsites and cabins directly on the beach. Many vacation rentals in Flagler Beach are pet-friendly, catering to families seeking extended stays with all the comforts of home.

Palm Coast and the Flagler Beaches provide a harmonious blend of natural beauty, recreational activities, and family-oriented amenities, making it a top choice for families looking to create lasting memories in a welcoming environment.

Growth in Vacation Rentals

Palm Coast, Florida, has experienced a significant surge in vacation rental properties over the past two years. The number of single-family homes utilized as short-term vacation rentals has increased by 70%, rising from 342 to 581 properties. This growth reflects the area's rising popularity among tourists and the expanding opportunities for property owners in the vacation rental market.

Diverse Rental Options

The expansion of the vacation rental market in Palm Coast offers visitors a wide array of accommodation choices. From single-family homes to condos and townhouses, these properties cater to various preferences and budgets. This diversity allows families, couples, and solo travelers to find suitable lodging that meets their specific needs, enhancing the overall appeal of Palm Coast as a vacation destination.

Economic Impact

The proliferation of vacation rentals has notable economic implications for the local community. Property owners benefit from additional income streams, while local businesses experience increased patronage from the influx of tourists. However, this growth also presents challenges, such as ensuring compliance with local regulations and maintaining the character of residential neighborhoods. The local government is actively addressing these issues to balance the benefits of tourism with community interests.

Market Performance

Vacation rentals in Palm Coast demonstrate strong market performance. A typical short-term rental is booked for approximately 215 nights per year, achieving a median occupancy rate of 59% and an average daily rate of $178. In 2023, the average annual revenue for hosts was around $36,000. As of September 2024, there are 588 active Airbnb listings in the area, indicating a robust and competitive market.

The substantial growth in Palm Coast's vacation rental market underscores the area's attractiveness as a tourist destination and provides a variety of lodging options for visitors. While this expansion brings economic benefits, it also necessitates thoughtful management to ensure sustainable development that aligns with the community's values and long-term goals.
