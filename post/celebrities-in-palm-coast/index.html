Celebrities in Palm Coast

Palm Coast, Florida, may not have the immediate star-studded reputation of Miami or Los Angeles, but its tranquil charm, beautiful coastlines, and luxurious communities have attracted a handful of well-known figures from various industries. Here’s a look at some celebrities who have called Palm Coast their home or have strong connections to the area:


Bobby Thigpen

Former MLB player Bobby Thigpen, best known for his time with the Chicago White Sox, has been linked to the Palm Coast area. Thigpen’s legacy in baseball is notable, holding the record for most saves in a single season for several years. His connection to Palm Coast is rooted in the area’s quiet and private nature, making it a perfect retreat for athletes who want to escape the spotlight.


Ronnie Van Zant (Legacy of Lynyrd Skynyrd)

While the iconic Ronnie Van Zant, the lead singer of Lynyrd Skynyrd, passed away in 1977, his family has maintained strong ties to Florida. Several members of the Van Zant family, including brothers Johnny (current lead singer of Lynyrd Skynyrd) and Donnie (founder of .38 Special), have lived in various parts of Florida. The connection of the Van Zants to the state adds a rock 'n' roll legacy to the cultural backdrop of areas like Palm Coast.


Bill France Jr.

NASCAR fans may recognize the France family name. Bill France Jr., who was a key figure in the development of NASCAR, also had a residence in the Palm Coast area. As the former CEO of NASCAR, France’s influence on the sport is undeniable, and his choice to reside in Palm Coast speaks to the allure of the area for those who seek both luxury and a quieter lifestyle away from the racing tracks.


Reggie Miller (NBA)

Though Reggie Miller is most famous for his NBA career with the Indiana Pacers and his iconic moments on the court, he has also enjoyed Florida’s coastal lifestyle. The Hall of Famer is known to frequent the Palm Coast region, enjoying the area's blend of luxurious living and scenic beauty, perfect for his post-basketball life.


Why Celebrities Choose Palm Coast


Palm Coast’s appeal to celebrities is multifaceted. Its pristine beaches, expansive golf courses, and gated communities offer the perfect blend of privacy and luxury. For stars looking to escape the fast pace of city life, Palm Coast provides a serene environment to relax while still offering easy access to Florida’s major hubs like Orlando and Jacksonville.


Additionally, the availability of high-end rental properties, like those offered by Oasis Rentals, attracts those who seek temporary or seasonal stays in the region. Spacious beachfront estates, private pools, and access to world-class amenities make it an ideal destination for both vacationers and those seeking long-term residences.


Though it may not have the celebrity-laden image of Beverly Hills or Malibu, Palm Coast quietly attracts stars who prefer a more private, luxurious lifestyle. For those looking to follow in the footsteps of athletes, musicians, and influential figures, Palm Coast offers the perfect balance of relaxation and exclusivity.
