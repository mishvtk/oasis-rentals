Top Attractions Near Palm Coast for Renters

Palm Coast, Florida, is a hidden gem along Florida’s east coast, offering the perfect blend of natural beauty, outdoor activities, and a relaxed lifestyle. For renters looking to enjoy everything this growing area has to offer, Palm Coast provides easy access to a variety of attractions, from scenic parks to historic landmarks. Whether you're a new resident or planning an extended stay, here are some top attractions near Palm Coast that are worth exploring.


1. Washington Oaks Gardens State Park

Located just a short drive from Palm Coast, Washington Oaks Gardens State Park is a must-see for nature lovers. Famous for its unique coquina rock formations along the shoreline, this park offers breathtaking views of the Atlantic Ocean. The park’s formal gardens are perfect for a relaxing stroll, while nature trails offer opportunities for hiking and wildlife viewing. For those who love fishing, the Matanzas River on the west side of the park provides excellent spots for casting a line.

- Distance from Palm Coast: 8 miles

- Activities: Hiking, fishing, photography, picnicking


2. Flagler Beach

Just a 15-minute drive from Palm Coast, Flagler Beach is a favorite spot attractions for both locals and tourists. With its uncrowded beaches, charming restaurants, and laid-back atmosphere, it’s the perfect place for a beach day. The Flagler Beach Pier offers opportunities for fishing and stunning views of the coastline. You can also explore the local shops and cafes that line the beachside streets, making it a fun and convenient getaway for renters looking to relax by the water.

- Distance from Palm Coast: 10 miles

- Activities: Swimming, surfing, fishing, dining


3. Bulow Creek State Park

For renters who enjoy outdoor activities and historical sites, Bulow Creek State Park is an ideal destination. Home to the Fairchild Oak, one of the largest live oak trees in the South, this park offers scenic walking trails that wind through beautiful, shaded hammocks. History buffs will appreciate the remnants of the Bulow Plantation, which tell the story of early Florida settlement. The park also offers opportunities for kayaking and canoeing along Bulow Creek.

- Distance from Palm Coast: 16 miles

- Activities: Hiking, kayaking, canoeing, historic exploration


4. St. Augustine

If you're willing to venture a little further, St. Augustine, the oldest city in the United States, is a short drive from Palm Coast and offers a rich cultural experience. With its cobblestone streets, historic landmarks like the Castillo de San Marcos, and vibrant art scene, St. Augustine is a great place to spend the day. The city also features various museums, unique shops, and world-class dining options. History, culture, and entertainment come together in this charming city, making it a top destination for Palm Coast renters seeking a more urban adventure.

- Distance from Palm Coast: 30 miles

- Activities: Historical tours, shopping, dining, art galleries


5. Princess Place Preserve

For those seeking a quiet escape into nature, Princess Place Preserve is a hidden treasure located just north of Palm Coast. This historic site, once a private estate, now serves as a nature preserve with 1,500 acres of pristine wilderness. Visitors can explore miles of hiking, biking, and horseback riding trails, or enjoy kayaking in Pellicer Creek. The preserve’s historic hunting lodge, built in the late 1800s, offers a glimpse into the area’s past. The stunning scenery and peaceful atmosphere make this an ideal spot for a relaxing day in the outdoors.

- Distance from Palm Coast: 12 miles

- Activities: Hiking, kayaking, wildlife viewing, historic tours


6. Tomoka State Park

Nature enthusiasts will enjoy a visit to Tomoka State Park, a short drive from Palm Coast. Known for its diverse ecosystems, this park is home to a variety of wildlife, including manatees, dolphins, and over 160 species of birds. Renters can enjoy canoeing, kayaking, or fishing along the Tomoka River. The park also features several scenic trails and picnic areas, making it a perfect day trip for families or solo explorers.

- Distance from Palm Coast: 20 miles

- Activities: Canoeing, kayaking, fishing, birdwatching


7. Marineland Dolphin Adventure

For a family-friendly attraction that’s sure to create lasting memories, head to Marineland Dolphin Adventure, one of the world’s first oceanariums. Located along the scenic A1A coastal highway, Marineland offers visitors the chance to interact with dolphins, participate in marine life exhibits, and learn about ocean conservation. This unique experience is perfect for families or anyone interested in marine wildlife and makes for an exciting day trip from Palm Coast.

- Distance from Palm Coast: 12 miles

- Activities: Dolphin interactions, educational exhibits, ocean conservation


8. Gamble Rogers Memorial State Recreation Area

Situated along the barrier island between the Atlantic Ocean and the Intracoastal Waterway, the Gamble Rogers Memorial State Recreation Area is a fantastic spot for beach lovers and outdoor adventurers alike. The park offers a beautiful, serene beach as well as opportunities for kayaking, fishing, and birdwatching. There’s also a campground for those interested in a longer stay surrounded by nature.

- Distance from Palm Coast: 15 miles

- Activities: Beachgoing, kayaking, fishing, camping


Palm Coast is surrounded by a wide variety of attractions that cater to all interests, from natural beauty and outdoor adventures to historical exploration and family fun. Whether you're a renter looking to relax by the beach, explore Florida's rich history, or enjoy the great outdoors, Palm Coast’s prime location offers easy access to some of the best attractions in the region.


By taking advantage of these nearby destinations, renters can experience everything that makes Palm Coast a fantastic place to live and explore.
