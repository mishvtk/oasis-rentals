Waterfront Restaurants in Boca Raton

Boca Raton, located on Florida’s stunning southeastern coast, is known for its upscale lifestyle, beautiful beaches, and scenic waterfronts. For both residents and visitors, dining along the water offers an experience that blends delicious food with breathtaking views. Whether you’re looking for fresh seafood, fine dining, or a casual spot to enjoy the sunset, Boca Raton has a variety of waterfront restaurants that cater to all tastes. Below are some of the best waterfront dining spots in Boca Raton to explore.


SeaSpray Inlet Grill

Located at the Waterstone Resort & Marina, SeaSpray Inlet Grill offers a perfect blend of coastal cuisine and stunning water views. The restaurant sits right on the Intracoastal Waterway, providing a relaxing setting for brunch, lunch, or dinner.

Menu Highlights:

- Fresh seafood dishes like Coconut Shrimp and Crab Cakes.

- A selection of salads, flatbreads, and hearty mains like Grilled Mahi-Mahi.

- Craft cocktails, including tropical favorites like the Waterstone Punch.


With its laid-back yet stylish vibe, SeaSpray is a great spot to enjoy a meal by the water, whether you're visiting with friends or seeking a romantic dinner.


Boca Landing Prime Seafood & Crafted Cocktails

Boca Landing, another popular spot located within the Waterstone Resort & Marina, specializes in fresh seafood with a fine-dining twist. Its prime location on the Intracoastal makes it a top destination for waterfront dining in Boca Raton.


Menu Highlights:

- Signature seafood dishes, including Florida Stone Crab Claws and Yellowfin Tuna.

- A range of locally sourced produce and freshly caught fish.

- An extensive wine list and crafted cocktails that complement the coastal flavors.


Boca Landing is perfect for special occasions or a sophisticated night out, offering elegant dishes with spectacular water views to match.


50 Ocean

Located just steps from the beach in Delray Beach, 50 Ocean is an upscale dining spot offering panoramic ocean views and a modern coastal menu. With its floor-to-ceiling windows, the restaurant provides stunning vistas of the Atlantic while you enjoy fresh, innovative dishes.


Menu Highlights:

  • Seared Sea Scallops and Filet Mignon

  • Key West Pink Shrimp and Lobster Bisque

  • Craft cocktails like the Delray Mule and a selection of fine wines

With its unbeatable ocean views and refined atmosphere, 50 Ocean is a favorite for special occasions or a romantic night out.


Caffe Luna Rosa

For those seeking a casual yet upscale beachfront dining experience, Caffe Luna Rosa in nearby Delray Beach is just a short drive from Boca Raton. Located directly across from the beach, this popular spot combines authentic Italian cuisine with gorgeous ocean views.


Menu Highlights:

- Classic Italian dishes like Homemade Ravioli and Veal Parmigiana.

- An extensive seafood menu with options such as Grilled Swordfish and Clams Linguini.

- Breakfast favorites, including Eggs Benedict and French Toast, served with a view.


Caffe Luna Rosa offers both indoor and outdoor seating, and is ideal for those who want to enjoy a meal while watching the waves roll in.


Houston’s

A local favorite, Houston’s is situated on the Intracoastal Waterway, providing diners with a serene view of passing boats and the calm water. This American restaurant is known for its laid-back atmosphere, excellent service, and consistent menu offerings.


Menu Highlights:

- Popular dishes include the Hawaiian Ribeye, Grilled Artichokes, and the Rotisserie Chicken.

- A focus on fresh, high-quality ingredients and simple, classic flavors.

- Signature cocktails and a well-curated selection of wines and beers.


With its prime location and comfortable setting, Houston’s is a great choice for anyone looking to enjoy a casual meal with spectacular waterfront views.


Deck 84

Just a short drive from Boca Raton in Delray Beach, Deck 84 offers a relaxed, family-friendly dining experience with beautiful views of the Intracoastal. The restaurant’s spacious outdoor deck is the perfect place to enjoy a meal while watching boats pass by.


Menu Highlights:

- Seafood favorites such as Baja Fish Tacos, Mahi Sandwiches, and Shrimp Ceviche.

- Creative cocktails like the Deck Punch and frozen drinks ideal for a hot Florida day.

- A variety of shareable appetizers and daily specials.


With its laid-back vibe and waterfront location, Deck 84 is a popular spot for brunch, lunch, or dinner, offering a great balance of casual dining and scenic views.


Boca Raton’s waterfront dining scene offers a variety of options for food lovers, from upscale seafood restaurants to casual spots perfect for a sunset cocktail. Whether you’re a local or visiting for the weekend, these restaurants provide the perfect combination of delicious cuisine and stunning waterfront views. From the laid-back charm of Deck 84 to the luxury dining at Boca Landing, there’s a restaurant to suit every mood and occasion.
