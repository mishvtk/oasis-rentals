Deerfield Beach vs. Boca Raton: October 2026 Rent Comparison

Deerfield Beach ZIP 33441 was the least expensive of the four measured markets in October 2026. Its Zillow Observed Rent Index fell 2.40% from September to $2,522.51. Boca Raton ZIPs 33486 and 33432 moved higher, while ZIP 33433 slipped just 0.17% to $2,558.80, according to Zillow’s October ZIP-level data.

The difference ranged from about $36 per month compared with Boca’s 33433 to nearly $852 compared with 33432. Deerfield Beach was still 5.94% above its October 2025 level, so the monthly decline did not amount to a broad collapse in rents.

Deerfield Beach moved below every Boca ZIP in this comparison

The October result reversed the position renters saw earlier in the year, when Deerfield Beach 33441 sometimes had a higher benchmark than Boca Raton 33433. The latest ZORI series places 33441 at the bottom of this four-ZIP comparison after a sharp September-to-October correction.

What the table does not say

The figures do not mean every home in 33441 rents for $2,522.51 or every property in 33432 costs $3,374.42. A renovated waterfront condo may be priced far from an older inland apartment or a detached house with a yard, even when they share a ZIP code.

The index helps show market direction and gives renters a comparison point. Current lease terms, property condition and included expenses determine whether a particular home is fairly priced.

What Zillow’s rent index measures

ZORI is a market index, not a listing quote

Zillow describes ZORI as a smoothed measure of the typical observed market-rate rent. It is a repeat-rent index weighted to represent the broader rental housing stock instead of only the properties advertised during one month.

The dollar value comes from the middle portion of observed asking rents. Zillow’s methodology explains that the company calculates the mean between the 35th and 65th percentiles, applies market-representation weights and smooths the index with a three-month moving average.

Why that distinction matters to renters

A renter may open a search and find no available home at the exact ZORI value. The mix of available properties can lean toward larger houses one week and smaller condos the next. Association rules, furnishing and lease length can also change the advertised price.

The index works best as a way to compare ZIPs, check whether the local benchmark is rising or falling, and see whether a sudden monthly move has changed the longer trend. A particular rent offer still requires a closer look.

Deerfield Beach 33441: cheaper now, but not cheaper than last year

October erased part of a late-summer surge

Deerfield Beach 33441 climbed from $2,504.89 in July to $2,521.25 in August and $2,584.63 in September before dropping to $2,522.51 in October. Those values come from the same monthly Zillow series. They put the 2.40% October decline in context.

October did not erase the summer increase completely. The benchmark was still 5.94% above October 2025 and 7.63% above October 2023. Among the four ZIPs compared here, Deerfield Beach had the strongest three-year growth rate even after the latest monthly pullback.

A useful rule for interpreting the drop

Treat one declining month as a reason to negotiate and compare, not as proof that every landlord must cut rent.

If a 33441 home has been available for a while, ask how its asking price compares with similar nearby homes. You can also ask whether the owner will adjust the rent, deposit structure or move-in date. If a well-kept home is priced near the market benchmark and includes useful services, the headline decline alone may not justify waiting.

Boca Raton’s three ZIPs tell different stories

33433 remains the closest budget alternative

Boca Raton 33433 registered a $2,558.80 ZORI value in October, only $36.29 above Deerfield Beach 33441. Its 0.17% monthly decline was mild. Its annual gain of 2.95% and three-year gain of 4.47% were the slowest in this comparison, according to the October dataset.

For a renter who prefers Boca Raton and wants to remain close to Deerfield’s benchmark, 33433 is the first ZIP to compare. The difference is small enough that utilities, parking, association charges and commuting costs could outweigh it.

33486 has been climbing steadily

ZIP 33486 reached $2,895.19 after rising 0.52% in October. Its benchmark increased each month from June through October 2026, moving from $2,822.50 to $2,829.76, $2,851.18, $2,880.18 and then $2,895.19, as shown in Zillow’s monthly observations.

That run was steadier than Deerfield Beach’s recent pattern. The 33486 index was also 5.80% higher than in October 2025 and 5.34% above October 2023. Its October increase continued a longer advance instead of appearing as an isolated jump.

33432 carries the largest premium

Boca Raton 33432 reached $3,374.42, up 1.01% for the month, 6.06% for the year and 6.50% over three years. It had the most expensive benchmark and the strongest annual growth in the comparison, based on the October 2026 ZORI figures.

Its series also rose from $3,274.14 in June to $3,297.37 in July, $3,300.24 in August, $3,340.57 in September and $3,374.42 in October. Renters considering this ZIP should plan for a materially different budget tier rather than a small Boca premium.

What each Boca premium means for a household budget

Convert the index gap into an annual decision

The smallest difference may look trivial from month to month, although every recurring cost belongs in an annual budget. Using the October index values, the approximate premiums over Deerfield Beach 33441 are:

  • Boca Raton 33433: $36.29 per month, or $435.48 across twelve months.
  • Boca Raton 33486: $372.68 per month, or $4,472.16 across twelve months.
  • Boca Raton 33432: $851.91 per month, or $10,222.92 across twelve months.

These are arithmetic comparisons based on the published October ZORI values. They are not quotes or promises about a particular lease.

Use a total-cost decision rule

Start with the asking rent and add every mandatory monthly charge. Then run the same calculation for the competing home. A $36 rent difference should not decide the search if one property includes water or parking and the other does not.

For the larger gaps, consider what the higher-priced location gives you in return. A shorter commute, a better-fitting layout or included services can have real value, but put a number on that value. If you cannot identify the benefit, the Deerfield Beach benchmark is a good reason to broaden the search south of Boca.

Compare actual listings beyond the headline rent

Build one worksheet for every home

Before paying an application charge or holding deposit, collect the same information for each property. Keep screenshots or written responses so you do not have to rely on memory.

  • Utilities: Ask which party pays for electricity, water, sewer, trash, internet and pest control.
  • Parking: Confirm the number of assigned spaces, guest rules, decal requirements and towing procedures.
  • Association costs: Ask about application charges, screening requirements, deposits and approval timing.
  • Commute: Test the route at the hour you would normally travel, including access to I-95, Federal Highway or local streets.
  • Move-in condition: Compare appliances, windows, air-conditioning performance, storage, flooring and signs of prior water intrusion.
  • Lease details: Verify the term, renewal language, maintenance duties, pet rules and every fee that appears outside base rent.

Ask a specific flood-insurance question

Renters insurance and flood coverage are not interchangeable. Ask an insurance professional whether the policy covering your belongings excludes flood damage and whether separate contents coverage is appropriate for the address.

The floor number or distance from the beach does not settle the question. Review the property’s drainage, parking access and history of water intrusion, then make an insurance decision based on the actual address and policy language.

How to use one-month, one-year and three-year trends

Give each time frame a separate job

The monthly figure shows current momentum. The annual figure indicates whether today’s market is more or less expensive than it was during the same season a year earlier. The three-year figure filters out more short-term movement and shows the broader direction.

In October, those signals were mixed. Deerfield Beach 33441 had the largest monthly decline while still posting 5.94% annual and 7.63% three-year growth. Boca 33433 barely declined for the month and had softer annual and three-year gains. Boca 33486 and 33432 paired positive monthly momentum with strong annual increases, according to Zillow’s ZIP histories.

A practical renter decision tree

  1. If the move is immediate, compare current homes and total mandatory costs first.
  2. If two homes are close in price, use the recent monthly direction as negotiation context.
  3. If considering a longer stay, give more weight to annual and three-year patterns.
  4. If one ZIP carries a substantial premium, list the location benefits that justify it.
  5. Before applying, request the complete fee schedule and association requirements in writing.

Deerfield Beach may not remain cheaper every month. October did, however, create a measurable opening for renters who can search on both sides of the city line.

FAQ

Was Deerfield Beach cheaper than Boca Raton in October 2026?

Yes. Deerfield Beach ZIP 33441 had a $2,522.51 ZORI benchmark, below Boca Raton ZIPs 33433 at $2,558.80, 33486 at $2,895.19 and 33432 at $3,374.42.

How much did the Deerfield Beach rent index fall in October?

ZIP 33441 fell 2.40% from September to October 2026, moving from $2,584.63 to $2,522.51.

Which Boca Raton ZIP had the lowest October benchmark?

ZIP 33433 was the least expensive Boca Raton market in the comparison at $2,558.80, approximately $36.29 above Deerfield Beach 33441.

Which market had the strongest annual rent growth?

Boca Raton ZIP 33432 led the four measured ZIPs with 6.06% year-over-year growth. Deerfield Beach 33441 followed at 5.94%, and Boca 33486 recorded 5.80%.

Does ZORI show what every available home should cost?

No. ZORI is a smoothed, weighted repeat-rent index designed to track the typical market rate. A specific home’s rent can differ because of property type, condition, location, amenities, lease length and included expenses.

Should renters wait after Deerfield Beach’s monthly decline?

Not automatically. Use the decline as negotiation context, then compare current availability, total mandatory costs and lease terms. Deerfield Beach remained 5.94% above its October 2025 benchmark despite the monthly drop.
