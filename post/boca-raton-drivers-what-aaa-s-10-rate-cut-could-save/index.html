Boca Raton Drivers: What AAA’s 10% Rate Cut Could Save

AAA says rates for its Florida Select Auto and Select Package Auto products are decreasing by an overall average of 10%. For a Boca Raton driver paying $3,000 a year, a 10% reduction would equal $300 annually, or $25 a month. That is an arithmetic example, not a personalized insurance quote.

This is AAA’s third Florida auto rate decrease of 2026. According to The Auto Club Group’s announcement, it takes effect on December 1, 2026, for new policies and February 1, 2027, for renewing policies.

What AAA announced for Florida drivers

The headline reduction

AAA announced the change in November 2026 for two Florida product lines: Florida Select Auto and Select Package Auto. The company estimates that this rate action will produce more than $35 million in combined annual savings for affected Florida policyholders.

The announcement applies statewide, rather than only in Boca Raton. It still matters locally because many renters regularly drive on Interstate 95, Florida’s Turnpike, Yamato Road, Glades Road, Palmetto Park Road and Federal Highway.

When the lower rates begin

The timing depends on the transaction. AAA says the new rates take effect on December 1, 2026, for new policies and February 1, 2027, for renewal policies. Existing customers should check the effective date on the declarations page instead of expecting an immediate refund during the policy term.

AAA calls this its third Florida auto rate reduction of the year. In a separate June 2026 announcement, the company reported average decreases of 5% for monoline auto policies and 4% for package auto policies. The newly announced average 10% reduction is a separate rate action from that earlier change.

How to use the table responsibly

Find the row closest to your current annual premium and use it as a budgeting reference. If your premium is $4,000, for example, the table shows that a precise 10% decrease would save $400 over a full year. Your renewal could change by a different amount because the announced percentage is an average across a book of policies.

Compare the annual total as well as the installment amount. Monthly billing can include payment plan charges. An annual or six-month figure makes it easier to see whether the underlying insurance cost fell.

Why your policy may not fall exactly 10%

An average combines different outcomes

An overall average does not give every customer the same percentage. Some policyholders may see a decrease near 10%, while others may receive a smaller reduction. Unrelated rating changes could offset part or all of the new rate level. AAA says individual premium changes will vary with coverage and risk characteristics.

The announcement describes a change to the insurer’s overall pricing, rather than a coupon applied uniformly at checkout. A driver whose vehicle, address or coverage changed since the previous term is no longer comparing the same policy details.

Factors that can change an individual result

When reviewing a Boca Raton quote, expect the insurer to consider several policy and risk details:

  • Driver history includes accidents, violations, claims and the drivers assigned to the policy, all of which can affect the result.
  • The vehicle’s model, age, repair cost, safety equipment and use can matter.
  • Higher liability or uninsured-motorist limits generally provide more protection than lower limits, so compare the same coverage limits.
  • Raising a comprehensive or collision deductible can reduce the premium while increasing what you must pay after a covered loss.
  • A daily commute to West Palm Beach differs from limited local use around Boca Raton.
  • A move between Boca Raton ZIP codes, or from another Florida city, can change the risk calculation because the ZIP code and garaging address are factors.

Ask the agent to separate the effect of the statewide rate change from changes caused by your policy details. That is especially useful if you recently moved, replaced a vehicle, added a household driver or changed your annual mileage.

A Boca Raton renewal checklist

Start with the declarations page

Pull your current declarations page before requesting alternatives. It should list the insured vehicles, drivers, liability limits, uninsured-motorist selection, comprehensive and collision deductibles, rental reimbursement, roadside coverage and policy dates.

Use that page as the specification sheet for every quote. A lower price means little if a proposal reduces liability limits, removes rental reimbursement or uses a much higher deductible.

  1. Confirm every driver and vehicle is correctly listed.
  2. Request quotes with identical liability and uninsured-motorist limits.
  3. Match comprehensive and collision deductibles.
  4. Compare the full-term premium and payment fees.
  5. Confirm the effective date before canceling existing coverage.
  6. Ask the agent to explain every coverage difference in writing.

Check the renewal date

Existing AAA customers should check renewals beginning February 1, 2027, the effective date AAA published for renewal policies. If your term renews later, set a reminder several weeks before the expiration date. That leaves time to gather comparable quotes and correct any driver or vehicle information.

Keep the old policy active while comparing prices. A gap can leave you financially exposed, and a lender or lessor may require continuous comprehensive and collision coverage on a financed or leased vehicle.

Deductibles and uninsured-motorist protection

A deductible is money you must be ready to spend

A $1,000 deductible may produce a cheaper premium than a $500 deductible, but it places more of a covered loss on the policyholder. Before increasing it, consider whether that amount would be available in an emergency fund after paying rent, utilities and other fixed expenses.

Ask for two otherwise identical versions of the quote: one with the current deductibles and another with the proposed higher amounts. Compare the premium difference with the additional out-of-pocket exposure. If accepting $500 more risk saves only a modest amount, the lower deductible may suit your household better.

Review uninsured-motorist coverage carefully

Removing uninsured-motorist coverage simply to reach a target price deserves careful consideration. Ask a licensed agent to explain stacked and non-stacked options, the bodily-injury limits and how the selection works with the vehicles and drivers in your household.

Along with asking how to get 10% off, ask what the policy would pay if your family were injured and what you would have to pay yourselves. Those answers put the announced rate decrease in perspective.

Bundling and discounts without false savings

Compare the combined household total

AAA’s Select Package product combines eligible auto and home coverage, and the company recommends reviewing bundling opportunities. Renters should ask whether an auto policy can be paired with renters insurance. Then compare the total cost and coverage of both policies, instead of considering only the auto line.

A bundle is useful when the combined proposal remains competitive and keeps the protection you need. Weaker coverage, an unsuitable deductible or a higher price on the companion policy can erase a discount.

Ask about discounts individually

AAA’s announcement says eligible customers who purchase coverage at least seven days in advance may receive an approximate 5% advance-purchase discount for a single-car policy or 10% for a multi-car policy. It also says its AAADrive program may provide up to 10% for enrollment and up to 30% at renewal based on the participant’s driving score.

These percentages apply to separate programs with their own eligibility requirements, so do not add them mechanically to the average rate reduction. Ask which discounts appear on the written quote, what information or monitoring they require, and whether they continue automatically at the next renewal.

Put possible savings into a renter’s budget

Give the money a job

If the final renewal is lower, update the transportation line in your household budget after receiving the declarations page and payment schedule. A hypothetical $25 monthly reduction on a $3,000 annual premium can help with planning, but the money is not available to spend until the new premium is confirmed.

Drivers planning a move can review the Boca Raton renter guides and current rental information while estimating the full cost of a location. Housing and driving decisions are connected in Boca Raton. Access to I-95, the Turnpike, employment centers and daily errands can affect mileage and household transportation costs.

Requote after an address change

If you are moving, request the quote using the actual new garaging address instead of assuming the old premium will follow you. A household considering a three-bedroom layout can use an existing local profile such as the Helsinki Circle Lakeview Home to see the kind of property information worth confirming. Insurance must still be quoted for the driver’s actual address, vehicles and usage.

Keep the auto quote separate from any renters insurance requirement in the lease or association documents. Add both confirmed costs to the moving budget afterward.

A simple decision rule for renewal day

Use three columns

Make a one-page comparison with three headings: protection, out-of-pocket risk and total premium. In the protection column, record liability, uninsured-motorist and optional coverages. Record each deductible under risk. In the premium column, include the full-term price, installment charges and any discount conditions.

Choose the policy after reviewing all three columns. A quote that costs less because it removed coverage or shifted too much risk to your emergency fund does not provide the same type of savings shown in the arithmetic table.

Questions to ask the agent

  • Does this quote include the new Florida rate level?
  • Which discount is included, and when can it change?
  • Are the liability and uninsured-motorist limits identical to my current policy?
  • Did any driver, vehicle, mileage estimate or garaging information change?
  • What is the full-term cost after installment fees?
  • When does the policy begin, and when should the old policy be canceled?

FAQ

Is every AAA auto policy in Florida decreasing by exactly 10%?

No. AAA describes 10% as the overall average decrease for its Florida Select Auto and Select Package Auto products. Individual changes vary with coverage, risk characteristics and other policy factors.

When do AAA’s lower Florida auto rates take effect?

AAA says the new rates take effect December 1, 2026, for new policies and February 1, 2027, for renewal policies.

How much would a 10% reduction save on a $3,000 annual premium?

The arithmetic result is $300 per year, or $25 per month. This is an illustration, not a personalized quote or guarantee.

What should Boca Raton renters compare between auto quotes?

Compare identical liability limits, uninsured-motorist selections, comprehensive and collision deductibles, optional coverages, drivers, vehicles and mileage. Check the full-term cost, including payment fees, as well.

Can bundling auto and renters insurance produce additional savings?

It may, depending on eligibility and the insurer. Compare the combined price and coverage of both policies without reducing needed protection merely to obtain a bundle discount.

Why might moving within Boca Raton change an auto premium?

Insurers can consider the ZIP code or garaging address along with driver history, vehicle, mileage, coverage limits and deductibles. Request a new quote using the actual address instead of assuming the old premium will follow you.
