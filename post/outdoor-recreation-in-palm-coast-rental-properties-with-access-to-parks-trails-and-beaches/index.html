Outdoor Recreation in Palm Coast: Rental Properties with Access to Parks, Trails, and Beaches

​Palm Coast, Florida, is a haven for outdoor enthusiasts, offering a harmonious blend of natural beauty and recreational activities. For those seeking vacation rentals that provide seamless access to parks, trails, and beaches, Palm Coast presents an array of options to suit diverse preferences.​

Nestled between St. Augustine and Daytona Beach, Palm Coast boasts over 19 miles of cinnamon-colored sand beaches. Flagler Beach, a pristine stretch along the Atlantic Ocean, is renowned for its excellent surfing conditions. Visitors can rent surf equipment from local shops and even take lessons tailored to various skill levels. Additionally, the beach's unique coquina sand and the opportunity to witness Right Whales migrating close to shore during winter months add to its allure.


Parks and Trails ​Palm Coast, Florida, is a treasure trove for outdoor enthusiasts, boasting an extensive network of parks and trails that showcase the region's natural beauty. With over 130 miles of interconnected trails winding through diverse habitats—from coastal scrubs to ancient oak hammocks—there's an adventure awaiting every visitor.

Lehigh Greenway Rail Trail

Stretching approximately 7 miles, the Lehigh Greenway Rail Trail traces a former railroad corridor, offering a paved path ideal for walking, biking, or rollerblading. As you journey alongside the Lehigh Canal, be on the lookout for local wildlife, including waterfowl, deer, and alligators. The trail begins at the Lehigh Greenway Trailhead on Colbert Lane and extends to U.S. 1 north of Bunnell, intersecting areas like Graham Swamp Preserve and Palm Coast Town Center.

​The Lehigh Greenway Rail Trail, often referred to as the Lehigh Trail, is a notable feature in Flagler County, Florida. This trail spans approximately 8.7 miles along a repurposed railroad corridor, offering a paved pathway suitable for activities such as walking, biking, and inline skating. The trail extends from U.S. 1 north of Bunnell, traverses through Palm Coast's town center, and culminates at Colbert Lane near the former Lehigh Portland Cement Company site.

A significant portion of the trail runs adjacent to the Lehigh Canal, providing users with scenic views and opportunities to observe local wildlife, including waterfowl, deer, and alligators. The trail is enveloped by trees for most of its length, enhancing the experience with natural shade and a serene atmosphere. Notably, the trail passes through the Graham Swamp Conservation Area, a 3,000-acre zone where elevated sections offer panoramic views of the surrounding wetlands. ​

For those seeking access points, the Lehigh Trailhead on Colbert Lane serves as a primary entry, equipped with amenities such as parking, restrooms, community gardens, and separate dog parks for small and large breeds. Additional access and parking are available at Belle Terre Parkway and U.S.1

The trail intersects with several key areas, including Graham Swamp Preserve and Palm Coast Town Center, making it a vital connector within the community. Its design accommodates a range of users, from casual walkers to avid cyclists, all seeking to immerse themselves in the natural beauty of Flagler County.​

the Lehigh Greenway Rail Trail stands as a testament to the region's commitment to repurposing historical corridors into recreational spaces, offering both residents and visitors a unique way to explore Florida's diverse landscapes.

Graham Swamp Preserve Trail

For those seeking a more rugged experience, the Graham Swamp Preserve Trail offers two distinct paths:​

  • East Access: Mountain Biking Trail

Located at 5135 Colbert Lane, the East Access features a 6.8-mile single-track loop renowned for its challenging terrain. This black diamond-rated trail presents steep technical climbs, fast downhill drops, and surfaces composed of sand and coquina, making it ideal for experienced mountain bikers seeking an adrenaline-filled ride. Along the way, riders can navigate rock gardens and may encounter diverse wildlife inhabiting the preserve. ​

  • West Access: Hiking and Nature Trail

Situated at 1125 Old Kings Road N., the West Access offers a more leisurely 2.2-mile hiking experience. This unpaved trail meanders through sandhills, scrubby flatwoods, and along the edge of the Graham Swamp, providing hikers with opportunities to observe the area's diverse flora and fauna. The path includes mild elevations, boardwalks over wetland areas, and scenic views into the floodplain forest, making it suitable for nature enthusiasts and casual hikers alike.

Both trails highlight the ecological significance of Graham Swamp Preserve, which serves as the historic headwaters of Bulow Creek and encompasses over 2,500 acres of wetland hardwood swamp. The preserve plays a crucial role in retaining and filtering stormwater runoff, supporting a rich variety of wildlife, including birds, mammals, and reptiles. Visitors to the preserve can engage in activities such as hiking, biking, fishing, wildlife viewing, and nature study, all while immersing themselves in the natural beauty of northeast Florida's coastal ecosystems.

Graham Swamp Preserve offers both challenging and serene trails that cater to a range of outdoor interests, providing visitors with a comprehensive experience of Florida's diverse landscapes.

Linear Park

​Nestled between the eastbound and westbound lanes of Palm Coast Parkway, Linear Park is a 57-acre haven for passive recreation in Palm Coast, Florida. The park offers a variety of amenities, including a playground, picnic pavilions, hiking and mountain biking trails, shuffleboard, bocce ball courts, and ample opportunities for bird watching. Recognized as a Great Florida Birding Trail by the Audubon Society, Linear Park is home to over 40 bird species, making it a prime location for bird enthusiasts.

In January 2024, the City of Palm Coast, in collaboration with Flagler County, unveiled a StoryWalk® at Linear Park. This innovative initiative combines literacy with outdoor activity, featuring twenty kiosks spread over a half-mile trail that display pages of a monthly themed story. Families can enjoy a leisurely walk while reading the story, fostering both physical activity and a love for reading.

Linear Park also serves as the venue for various nature programs, including guided nature walks and early childhood education explorations. The park's amenities are designed to cater to visitors of all ages, promoting community engagement and an appreciation for the natural environment.

For those interested in hosting events or gatherings, Linear Park offers three rentable picnic pavilions and a covered patio space. These facilities provide an ideal setting for family reunions, birthday parties, and other community events.

Linear Park stands as a testament to Palm Coast's commitment to providing accessible recreational spaces that promote health, education, and community interaction. Whether you're seeking a peaceful nature walk, an educational experience for your children, or a venue for your next gathering, Linear Park offers a welcoming environment for all.

James F. Holland Memorial Park

Covering 26 acres, James F. Holland Memorial Park is a hub of activity. It features a state-of-the-art splash pad designed to represent local ecosystems, separate dog parks for small and large breeds, sports fields, and the Palm Coast Historical Society Museum. The splash pad, spanning 11,000 square feet, includes zones themed around coastal and swamp ecosystems, complete with realistic sea animal and swamp creature spray features.

​James F. Holland Memorial Park, located at 18 Florida Park Drive in Palm Coast, Florida, spans 26 acres and serves as a vibrant hub for community recreation and leisure. The park boasts a diverse array of amenities designed to cater to visitors of all ages and interests.

State-of-the-Art Splash Pad

A standout feature of the park is its expansive 11,000-square-foot splash pad, meticulously designed to represent local ecosystems. The splash pad is divided into two thematic zones:​

  • The Shore Zone: Suitable for children of all ages, this area depicts coastal ecosystems with realistic sea animal spray features, including dolphins, an octopus, a right whale, and starfish.

  • The Swamp Zone: Designed for children aged 2 to 5, this section represents local swamp ecosystems, featuring elements such as lily pads, frogs, cattails, and yellow-bellied slider turtle spray features.

The splash pad's surface is made of Life Floor, a certified aquatic play material that provides a slip-resistant surface with added fall protection compared to concrete.

Dog Parks

For canine companions, the park offers separate dog parks for small and large breeds, providing ample space for dogs to play and socialize safely. Regular maintenance ensures these areas remain clean and enjoyable for all visitors. ​

Sports and Recreational Facilities

Sports enthusiasts can take advantage of various facilities, including:​

  • Three tennis courts and one junior-sized tennis court with LED lighting.

  • Two shuffleboard courts.

  • Two basketball courts.​

  • Four striped pickleball courts.​

  • Two bocce ball courts with LED lighting.​

  • Two horseshoe pits.​

  • A sand volleyball court.​

  • Two large multi-purpose sports fields.​

Additionally, the park features ten covered pavilions available for gatherings and events. ​

Playground and Historical Museum

Children can explore a unique playground themed after the Bulow Plantation Sugar Mill, complete with coquina rock structures featuring slides, bridges, ladders, a climbing wall, a zip line, an archaeological digging area, sandpit, treehouse, metallophone, ADA-accessible rocker, sail shades, and seating areas.

The park is also home to the Palm Coast Historical Society Museum, where visitors can delve into the rich history of Palm Coast and the significance of the Bulow Plantation Sugar Mill. The museum is open on Wednesday and Sunday from 1 p.m. to 3 p.m. ​

James F. Holland Memorial Park stands as a testament to Palm Coast's commitment to providing diverse recreational opportunities, fostering community engagement, and preserving local history.​

Waterfront Park

Situated along the Intracoastal Waterway, Waterfront Park offers a serene setting for walking, running, and fishing. The shaded playground provides beautiful water views, and the park is home to the Children's Memorial Garden. The Intracoastal Waterway Trail, extending from Waterfront Park to the Hammock Dunes Bridge, spans 2 miles and is perfect for spotting dolphins and manatees. ​

​Waterfront Park, located at 150 Waterfront Park Road in Palm Coast, Florida, offers a tranquil setting along the Intracoastal Waterway for various recreational activities. Visitors can enjoy walking, running, and fishing amidst the park's natural beauty. A shaded playground provides children with play opportunities while offering scenic water views. The park is also home to the Children's Memorial Garden, a peaceful area dedicated to remembering lost loved ones. Additionally, the Intracoastal Waterway Trail extends from Waterfront Park to the Hammock Dunes Bridge, spanning approximately 2 miles and offering chances to observe local wildlife such as dolphins and manatees. ​


Community Engagement and Events

Palm Coast actively promotes community engagement through various events and initiatives. For instance, Celebrate Trails Day on April 27, 2024, highlighted the importance of trails in connecting communities and nature. Additionally, the city hosts interactive scavenger hunts, such as the turtle art installations, fostering community involvement and tourism. ​

Celebrate Trails Day

On April 27, 2024, the City of Palm Coast, in partnership with the Flagler County Tourist Development Council, hosted Celebrate Trails Day at Waterfront Park. This event encouraged residents and visitors to explore over 130 miles of scenic trails in the area, emphasizing the vital role these pathways play in connecting the community with nature. Attendees enjoyed interactive booths, games, and educational activities, including Corn Hole, Chalk Art, a Mini Ceramic Turtle Painting Station, and a Scavenger Hunt. The event also provided information about local wildlife and upcoming park improvements, fostering a deeper appreciation for the region's natural resources. ​

Turtle Trail Scavenger Hunt

Another initiative promoting community involvement is the Turtle Trail, a public art project featuring over 20 large, fiberglass loggerhead turtle sculptures, each uniquely painted by local artists. Launched in 2018 by the Flagler County Cultural Council, the trail celebrates the indigenous sea turtle species and showcases the area's vibrant arts community. Residents and tourists can participate in a scavenger hunt to locate these sculptures, using downloadable guides available through the Visit Flagler website. This interactive experience not only highlights local artistry but also raises awareness about wildlife conservation. ​


Beaches

​Palm Coast, Florida, is celebrated for its distinctive cinnamon-colored sands and serene coastal environments, offering a tranquil retreat for visitors seeking natural beauty and relaxation. This unique hue of the sand is attributed to the presence of coquina, a sedimentary rock composed of fragmented shells, which imparts a warm, reddish tint to the shoreline.​

One of the prime locations to experience these captivating sands is Varn Park. Situated at 3665 S Ocean Shore Blvd, this hidden gem is a haven for birdwatchers, fishermen, and families alike. The park features a boardwalk that traverses the dunes, leading to the expansive beach where the rich cinnamon-colored sand meets the cerulean Atlantic Ocean. Visitors can enjoy amenities such as free parking, restrooms, outdoor showers, and wheelchair access, ensuring a comfortable beach experience. While lifeguards are not present, the park's serene atmosphere and natural beauty make it a favored spot for those seeking a peaceful coastal escape.

Another notable destination is Flagler Beach, a six-mile stretch of unspoiled coastline renowned for its laid-back charm and fewer crowds. This area is popular among surfers, anglers, and beachcombers. The Flagler Beach Municipal Pier offers panoramic views of the Atlantic Ocean and serves as an ideal spot for fishing or simply enjoying the ocean breeze. Adjacent to the beach, the bustling boardwalk is lined with quaint shops and eateries, enhancing the coastal experience with local flavors and unique finds. ​

For those interested in geological wonders, Washington Oaks Gardens State Park presents a unique blend of coastal beauty and botanical splendor. Nestled between the Atlantic Ocean and the Matanzas River, the park is famed for its coquina rock formations that create picturesque tide pools along the shore. Visitors can explore formal gardens showcasing azaleas, camellias, and roses, or wander along scenic nature trails through the maritime hammock, offering a serene retreat into nature's embrace. ​

South Recreation Center ​The Southern Recreation Center, Palm Coast's latest addition to its recreational amenities, officially opened its doors to the public on February 23, 2024. Located at 1290 Belle Terre Parkway, this 11,500-square-foot facility offers a diverse range of features designed to cater to the community's athletic and social interests.​

The center boasts 12 pickleball courts, six of which are covered to facilitate year-round play, and plans are underway to add five new HydroGrid clay tennis courts, including a premium stadium court tailored for tournament events. Inside, visitors have access to locker rooms, multi-purpose event spaces, and a welcoming lobby area. Outdoor patios and balconies further enhance the facility's appeal, providing spaces for relaxation and social interaction. Beginning March 3, 2024, the on-site restaurant, Redefined Food Company, will offer concession services, emphasizing health-based, handmade menu options.

In alignment with Palm Coast's commitment to sustainability, the Southern Recreation Center was designed as a green building. It features 200 solar panels installed above the pickleball courts, offsetting approximately 75% of the building's energy consumption. Additional eco-friendly elements include low-flow water fixtures, LED lighting, heat-reflecting roof paint to enhance energy efficiency, and landscaping with native plants to reduce water usage. The facility is pursuing LEED (Leadership in Energy and Environmental Design) certification from the U.S. Green Building Council, underscoring its environmentally conscious design.

Adjacent to the recreation center, the new Lehigh Trailhead offers an ADA-compliant paved parking lot with over 70 spaces, a restroom building, community gardens featuring six ADA-accessible plots and 12 standard plots, as well as large and small dog parks. Enhanced lighting, utilities, pavilions, benches, and sidewalks further contribute to the trailhead's appeal, promoting health, wellness, and outdoor recreational opportunities for all residents. ​

The Southern Recreation Center is open Monday through Friday from 8 a.m. to 9 p.m., and on weekends from 8 a.m. to 2 p.m., with operating hours subject to change based on facility utilization. Indian Trails Sports Complex

​The Indian Trails Sports Complex in Palm Coast, Florida, stands as a premier destination for athletes and sports enthusiasts, hosting a variety of local, regional, and state tournaments. The complex offers exceptional facilities for soccer, lacrosse, flag football, baseball, and softball, making it a preferred choice for sporting events. ​

Facility Amenities

The complex features:​

  • Four baseball fields equipped with bases, pitchers' mounds, electronic scoreboards, and covered player dugouts.​

  • Four softball fields with similar amenities, including covered dugouts.​

  • Eight multi-purpose fields designed to accommodate soccer, lacrosse, and flag football, complete with bleachers and player benches.​

All 14 fields are surfaced with natural Bermuda grass, ensuring high-quality playing conditions. ​

Additional Amenities

Beyond the athletic fields, the complex offers:​

  • A playground for families and younger visitors.​

  • Three restroom buildings and water fountains for convenience.​

  • Concession stands providing refreshments during events.

  • Free Wi-Fi access throughout the facility.​

  • 213 parking spaces, with additional parking available at the adjacent middle school.​

Economic Impact and Expansion

Recognizing the complex's success in attracting tournaments and visitors, Palm Coast approved an expansion project in 2014, increasing the number of fields from eight to ten. This development aimed to capitalize on the growing interest in hosting sports events, which significantly contribute to the local economy. For instance, in fiscal year 2012, the complex hosted 10 tournaments, generating approximately $2.7 million in visitor spending. By 2014, 24 tournaments were scheduled, with spending projected to increase accordingly. ​

Location and Accessibility

Situated between St. Augustine and Daytona Beach in Northeast Florida, Palm Coast offers easy access from both Interstate 95 and U.S. 1. The complex's proximity to beaches, Marineland, and several state parks enhances its appeal. Additionally, nearby shopping centers, restaurants, and hotels provide convenient options for visitors attending events. ​

The Indian Trails Sports Complex exemplifies Palm Coast's commitment to fostering athletic excellence and community engagement. Its state-of-the-art facilities and amenities not only support local athletes but also attract visitors, contributing to the city's economic vitality.

Palm Coast Aquatics Center

The Palm Coast Aquatics Center, located at 339 Parkview Drive in Palm Coast, Florida, is a premier destination for aquatic activities and family-friendly recreation. At the heart of the center is the Frieda Zamba Swimming Pool, a heated outdoor facility that serves as a focal point for residents and visitors of all ages and abilities.

Programs and Activities

The Aquatics Center offers a diverse range of programs designed to promote water safety, fitness, and enjoyment:​

  • Learn-to-Swim Lessons: Certified instructors provide swim lessons for individuals aged six months through adulthood, emphasizing water safety and skill development. ​

  • Water Fitness Classes: Participants can engage in Aqua Zumba® and Water Aerobics, combining exercise with the therapeutic benefits of water. ​

  • Swim Development Programs: The center hosts non-competitive teams such as the Palm Coast Porpoises Youth Swim Team and the Palm Coast Piranhas Masters Swim Club, fostering skill improvement in a team environment under the guidance of certified coaches. ​

Facilities and Amenities

Beyond the swimming pool, the Aquatics Center features several amenities to enhance the visitor experience:​

  • Pavilions: Four covered pavilions equipped with picnic tables are available for seasonal reservations, providing shaded areas for gatherings and events. ​

  • Classroom Space: A small indoor classroom can be reserved for meetings, programs, or events, accommodating various community activities. ​

  • Rental Opportunities: The pool and its facilities can be rented for private events, offering a unique venue for celebrations and group activities. ​

Operating Hours and Admission

The Aquatics Center operates seasonally with varying hours. As of March 5, 2025, the facility is temporarily closed; visitors are encouraged to check the official website for the most current information.

Historical Significance

The facility honors local surfing legend Frieda Zamba, a four-time world surfing champion from Flagler Beach, whose achievements have inspired many in the community. ​

The Palm Coast Aquatics Center stands as a testament to the city's commitment to providing quality recreational opportunities, promoting health, wellness, and community engagement through its comprehensive aquatic programs and amenities.


Palm Coast Community Center

​The Palm Coast Community Center, located at 305 Palm Coast Parkway NE, stands as a vibrant hub for residents of all ages, offering a wide array of programs and amenities. Following an extensive $8 million renovation completed in March 2018, the facility expanded to approximately 21,000 square feet, more than tripling its original size. This transformation was funded primarily through local tax revenues, allowing the city to undertake the project without incurring debt.

Facility Features and Amenities

The Community Center boasts several large rooms available for rent, accommodating events such as meetings, parties, weddings, and seminars. These spaces include:​

  • Sunshine Room: The largest venue, featuring expansive windows that allow natural light to fill the space. It is ideal for weddings, banquets, and large seminars, with a seating capacity of 225 without tables and 192 with tables.​

  • Waterfront Room: Overlooking a scenic pond with a fountain, this room is suitable for birthday parties and medium-sized seminars, accommodating 175 without tables and 120 with tables.

  • Hometown Room: Designed for fun and flexibility, this space is perfect for children's parties and small weddings, with a capacity of 125 without tables and 80 with tables.​

  • Exploration Room: A classroom-style setting ideal for small seminars and meetings, seating 40 without tables and 25 with tables.​

Each room offers unique features tailored to various event needs.

In addition to indoor facilities, the center includes an open courtyard shaded by historic oak trees, a playground with dedicated toddler areas and ADA-accessible equipment, and lighted basketball courts. A trailhead for the St. Joe Walkway provides access to Linear Park, complete with a new restroom building for visitors' convenience. ​

Programs and Activities

The Community Center serves as the headquarters for Palm Coast's Parks & Recreation Department, offering a diverse range of programs:​

  • Adult & Senior Programs: Activities include art studios, woodcarving groups, horticulture workshops, book clubs, fitness classes like Zumba® and BAM! (Balance and Motion), and support classes for new mothers.​

  • Toddler, Youth, & Teen Programs: Offerings range from story time sessions like Teddy Bear Picnic to educational programs for homeschoolers and cooking classes for teens. Seasonal camps during school breaks provide additional engagement opportunities.​

These programs foster community engagement and cater to a wide array of interests and age groups.

Sustainability and Community Impact

The renovation project emphasized environmental sustainability, achieving LEED certification. The expanded parking lot features solar panels, and the preservation of historic oak trees underscores the city's commitment to maintaining its natural heritage. ​

The Palm Coast Community Center stands as a testament to the city's dedication to enhancing the quality of life for its residents, providing a versatile space for recreation, learning, and community building.


Palm Harbor Golf Club

​ Nestled in the heart of Palm Coast, Florida, Palm Harbor Golf Club offers a captivating blend of natural beauty and challenging play. Winding through corridors of towering palm trees and ancient oaks draped with Spanish moss, the course demands both accuracy and creativity from golfers of all skill levels. As a daily fee municipal facility, it provides an accessible yet memorable golfing experience amidst Florida's picturesque landscape. ​

Course Features and Amenities

Renovated and redesigned in 2009, Palm Harbor Golf Club boasts a comprehensive practice facility, including a driving range, putting green, and chipping green complete with a bunker. The pro shop offers a selection of men's and women's name-brand apparel and equipment, catering to the needs of every golfer. In April 2023, the on-site restaurant, Loopers Par & Grill, opened its doors, featuring a diverse menu suitable for both quick bites on the course and sit-down meals. With a full bar and 32 televisions, patrons can enjoy their favorite sporting events in a lively atmosphere. ​

Dress Code and Facilities

To maintain the course's standards, proper golf attire is required at all times. Collared shirts are mandatory, while jeans and t-shirts are prohibited. Additionally, Palm Harbor Golf Club is a soft spike facility, ensuring the preservation of the greens. The club also offers ample parking and restroom facilities for the convenience of its guests.

Community Engagement and Recognition

Palm Harbor Golf Club serves as a cornerstone of the local golfing community, hosting several leagues, including the Men's Golf Association League, the Women's Golf Association League, and the Palm Harbor Niners. These organizations provide structured play and foster camaraderie among members. The club's commitment to excellence has not gone unnoticed; it was voted the Best Golf Course in Flagler County by readers of the Flagler/Palm Coast News Tribune in both 2010 and 2011, reflecting its esteemed reputation among local golfers.

Operating Hours and Contact Information

Palm Harbor Golf Club operates on seasonal schedules:​

  • Winter Hours (November 1 - March 8): Monday-Sunday, 7:00 AM - 5:00 PM​

  • Summer Hours (March 9 - October 31): Monday-Sunday, 7:00 AM - 6:00 PM​

For those looking to enjoy a meal or beverage, Loopers Par & Grill is open daily from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM. The club is located at 20 Palm Harbor Drive, Palm Coast, FL 32137. To book a tee time or inquire further, contact the main line at 386-986-4653 or email
