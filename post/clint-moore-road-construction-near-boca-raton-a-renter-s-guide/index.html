Clint Moore Road Construction Near Boca Raton: A Renter’s Guide

Wall construction beside Woodfield Country Club is reportedly causing traffic backups on Clint Moore Road, especially for eastbound drivers between Florida’s Turnpike and Jog Road. Renters and home shoppers should leave extra travel time, check current conditions, and drive the route during the hours they would normally use it before signing a lease.

Jurisdiction complicates the issue. Although nearby homes may have a Boca Raton mailing address, parts of this corridor are in unincorporated Palm Beach County. The construction site and affected roadway may fall under different authorities. Published reports raised questions about approval and traffic control, but renters should confirm which agency authorized each part of the work through official records.

What is happening on Clint Moore Road

The reported construction and lane reduction

On September 7, 2026, BocaNewsNow reported that an eight-foot wall was being installed along the north side of Woodfield Country Club’s golf course, south of Clint Moore Road. According to the report, the project had reduced eastbound Clint Moore Road to one lane along much of the stretch between Florida’s Turnpike and Jog Road.

The report also said the lane closures had sometimes lasted eight to ten hours, though Palm Beach County officials reportedly had been told to expect intermittent closures. Those details came from the news organization’s reporting, rather than a project schedule published by the county or city. Conditions can change quickly as crews move, fencing is repaired, or the traffic-control plan changes.

Other work affecting the corridor

A separate September 6 traffic roundup counted 186 reported road and bridge issues across Palm Beach County for that week, including 20 state highway projects and 166 county-listed closures or work zones. The roundup also listed periodic closures for sidewalk work at Military Trail and Clint Moore Road, along with utility work on Clint Moore Road from South Military Trail to east of Park of Commerce Boulevard.

A delay near Woodfield may therefore overlap with other work farther east. If your usual trip follows Clint Moore Road toward Military Trail, I-95, an office near Congress Avenue, or errands in central Boca Raton, conditions beside the community gate will tell only part of the story.

A Boca Raton address may be outside the city

The boundary matters

“Boca Raton” may refer to a postal address, the general market area, or the incorporated city. Large parts of West Boca are in unincorporated Palm Beach County even though residents receive mail addressed to Boca Raton.

The September 7 report said an official map appeared to place the wall within the City of Boca Raton, while crews and traffic effects appeared to extend into unincorporated Palm Beach County. That was the report’s interpretation, not a final jurisdictional determination. The article also said the publication was awaiting clarification from Palm Beach County about possible traffic or code issues.

Ask the office responsible for the exact location

Before reporting a problem, note the precise side of the road, nearest intersection, direction of travel, and any visible contractor or permit information. For traffic operations on a county road or in an unincorporated area, start with Palm Beach County Engineering and Public Works. For property or permitting questions confirmed to be within city limits, use the City of Boca Raton’s official website to find the appropriate department.

A city name in a listing, HOA document, or map pin is not enough to establish jurisdiction. Ask the office to confirm responsibility for the exact parcel or road segment. This can prevent the frustrating loop of calling the city about a county-controlled location, or calling the county about work confined to a city parcel.

How the backups may affect a renter’s schedule

Commutes, schools, and ordinary errands

A lane reduction on eastbound Clint Moore Road can affect more than the morning commute. The corridor feeds Florida’s Turnpike, Jog Road, Military Trail and, farther east, routes toward I-95. A queue near one intersection can add time to school drop-off, medical appointments, grocery runs, contractor visits, or a trip connecting with an airport-bound highway.

Families should test the morning departure and afternoon return instead of relying on a routing app late at night. If two adults commute in opposite directions, each should drive their own route. A home that suits a Turnpike commuter may be less convenient for someone who must first travel east along Clint Moore before turning north or south.

Airport runs and fixed appointments

Airport trips are especially sensitive to local delays, which can use up the time set aside for highway traffic, parking, or security. When leaving during an active work period, check the local segment before choosing Clint Moore Road. Compare routes that reach Yamato Road or Glades Road, keeping in mind that diverted traffic can make those roads busier too.

Take the same approach with medical appointments, school pickup deadlines, interviews, and train departures. Add a construction buffer and record how much of it you use. After several trips, you will have your own evidence instead of a general impression that the road is "usually fine."

Touring a rental near the work zone

Visit at a useful time

A quiet weekend tour can conceal a weekday access problem. Ask for a weekday appointment and arrive early enough to drive the nearby roads before meeting the leasing agent. If possible, repeat the drive during the opposite peak period without arranging another interior tour.

A practical test-drive plan

  1. Map the exact routine. Start at the prospective home and drive to the workplace, school, caregiver, or highway entrance you expect to use.
  2. Run a weekday morning test. Travel at your normal departure time and note the queue before each major intersection.
  3. Repeat the drive in the evening. Congestion in the reverse direction can be quite different, particularly near community entrances and turn lanes.
  4. Try an alternate exit. Ask whether residents may use another gate and whether it has restricted hours, resident-only access, or separate visitor procedures.
  5. Check one route away from the construction. Test Yamato Road or another appropriate east-west road instead of relying on the estimated time shown on a map.
  6. Record the results. Save the date, departure time, weather, route, and any visible lane closure so you can distinguish a normal commute from an unusual incident.

If the tested commute works only when every light, gate, and lane operates perfectly, the location leaves too little margin for a fixed daily schedule.

Questions for the landlord or property manager

Access, guests, and deliveries

Ask questions that lead to specific answers. “Is traffic bad?” invites an opinion. “Which entrance may tenants use at 7:30 a.m., and is that entrance ever closed during construction?” will tell you more.

  • Which gates or driveways can tenants use during road work?
  • Are alternate entrances open at all hours or only during staffed periods?
  • How do rideshare drivers, food deliveries, movers, and service technicians enter?
  • Must guests approach from a particular direction to reach a staffed gate?
  • Have waste collection, package delivery, landscaping, or maintenance visits been delayed?
  • Which route should residents use if temporary fencing blocks the usual turn?

Noise and work near the home

Road backups and construction noise are separate concerns. Ask whether the unit faces Clint Moore Road, the golf course edge, a staging area, or a community entrance. During the tour, pause the conversation and listen from the bedrooms, patio, and home-office area with the air conditioner on and off.

Request any written construction notices the owner or association has received, including expected work hours and access changes. The leasing representative cannot guarantee a completion date unless it appears in a current notice from the party managing the project.

Emergency and move-in planning

Ask how emergency vehicles enter when a normal gate or lane is obstructed. Confirm the route and time window for a moving truck as well. When cones narrow the roadway, a long truck may not be able to make the same turn as a passenger car.

Put agreed move-in instructions in writing. Include the approved gate, arrival contact number, truck-size restrictions, parking location, elevator reservation if relevant, and a backup plan in case traffic control changes on moving day.

How to monitor conditions calmly

Keep a short observation log

One terrible drive confirms that a disruption occurred, but it does not show the long-term pattern. Check the corridor on several ordinary weekdays. Note whether the closure is active, how far traffic queues, and whether the alternate entrance remains usable.

Check live navigation before leaving and compare it with official or local notices. The county’s traffic resources are the starting point for county-controlled roads, while the City of Boca Raton site can direct questions about confirmed city locations. Local reporting can identify a developing problem. Official departments are better placed to confirm jurisdiction, permits, responsibility for traffic control, and complaint procedures.

Temporary inconvenience or lasting commute problem

Watch for a consistent pattern. Congestion that appears while crews occupy a lane and clears outside work hours may be a temporary construction effect. If test drives remain difficult when no lane is closed, the problem may come from normal traffic demand, the turning pattern, the school schedule, or the gate design.

Ask for the project schedule, but base the lease decision on conditions you can observe now. Construction dates change because of weather, inspections, material deliveries, or revised work plans. Treat a promised finish as planning information, not a guarantee of a particular commute after move-in.

Boca Raton rental context

Consider price and location together

Renters in Boca Raton ZIP code 33433 faced a Zillow Observed Rent Index of $2,649.46 in July 2026, up 4.34 percent from July 2025, according to Zillow’s published ZORI data. The index covers single-family homes, condominiums, and cooperatives. It is a market measure, rather than a quote for a particular property.

At that price level, access deserves as much attention as bedrooms, finishes, and amenities. A home may appear to be the better value on paper while costing a renter time every weekday if its only practical exit feeds a recurring queue.

What to do before signing

Drive Clint Moore Road at your normal departure time. From a safe location, photograph or note any active traffic-control signs, and ask the property manager to confirm alternate gate and move-in procedures in writing. If a question about jurisdiction or approval could affect your decision, contact the city or county with the parcel and road location instead of relying on the postal address.

FAQ

What construction is reportedly causing traffic on Clint Moore Road?

BocaNewsNow reported on September 7, 2026, that construction of an eight-foot wall along Woodfield Country Club had narrowed eastbound Clint Moore Road to one lane along much of the segment between Florida’s Turnpike and Jog Road.

Is the Woodfield construction inside the City of Boca Raton?

The location crosses a city-and-county boundary. A published report interpreted an official map as placing the wall in the city, while crews and traffic effects appeared to extend into unincorporated Palm Beach County. Renters should ask the appropriate government office to confirm jurisdiction for the exact parcel or road segment.

When should I test-drive a commute from a rental near Clint Moore Road?

Drive the route on an ordinary weekday at your usual morning departure time, then repeat it during your evening return. Test an alternate gate or route as well so your decision does not depend on a single entrance.

What should I ask a landlord about the construction?

Ask about alternate entrances, gate hours, guest and delivery access, construction notices, noise near the unit, moving-truck routes, and the emergency-access plan. Request the operating details in writing before signing.

How can I tell if the delay is temporary or part of the normal commute?

Keep a short log over several weekdays. Note whether a lane closure is active, how long the queue lasts, and what happens outside work hours. Congestion that persists when crews are absent may point to an underlying traffic or access issue rather than a temporary construction delay.
