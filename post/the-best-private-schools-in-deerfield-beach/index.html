The Best Private Schools in Deerfield Beach

For families considering a move to Deerfield Beach, Florida, finding the right school is often a top priority. Fortunately, Deerfield Beach is home to several top-tier private schools that provide an excellent education for children of all ages. Whether you’re seeking a school with a strong academic reputation, a focus on arts and athletics, or an emphasis on values-based education, there are plenty of great options.


In this guide, we’ll highlight some of the best private schools in Deerfield Beach to help you make an informed decision for your child’s education.


Saint Ambrose Catholic School

Location: 363 SE 12th Ave, Deerfield Beach, FL 33441

Grade Levels: Pre-K through 8th Grade


Saint Ambrose Catholic School in Deerfield Beach offers a faith-based education with a strong focus on both academic achievement and spiritual growth. The school emphasizes a nurturing environment where students are encouraged to develop their talents and character. With small class sizes, students benefit from personalized attention, and the curriculum includes religious studies, core academics, arts, and physical education.


Why It Stands Out:

Saint Ambrose is known for its welcoming community, family involvement, and comprehensive approach to education. Their strong academic program is complemented by extracurricular activities such as sports, music, and community service initiatives.


Highlands Christian Academy

Location: 501 NE 48th St, Pompano Beach, FL 33064

Grade Levels: Pre-K through 12th Grade


Although technically located in neighboring Pompano Beach, Highlands Christian Academy is a popular choice for families in Deerfield Beach. This Christian school offers a rigorous academic curriculum integrated with biblical principles, preparing students for both college and life beyond. The school offers a wide range of programs, including honors and Advanced Placement (AP) courses, as well as a variety of extracurricular activities such as athletics, performing arts, and community service opportunities.


Why It Stands Out:

Highlands Christian Academy is well-regarded for its commitment to academic excellence and character development. The school’s athletic program is also strong, with teams competing at a high level in various sports, including basketball, soccer, and volleyball.


Pine Crest School

Location: 2700 St Andrews Blvd, Boca Raton, FL 33434

Grade Levels: Pre-K through 12th Grade


Another non-religious private school located near Deerfield Beach is Pine Crest School in Boca Raton. Pine Crest is widely recognized for its exceptional academic programs, including innovative STEM courses, a competitive arts program, and global studies. Students are encouraged to pursue excellence in academics, arts, and athletics, with a focus on developing leadership skills, creativity, and critical thinking.


Why It Stands Out:

Pine Crest consistently ranks among the top private schools in Florida and nationwide. Their commitment to fostering innovation and preparing students for the future through advanced technology programs and collaborative learning environments makes it a top choice for families looking for academic excellence.


American Heritage School

Location: 6200 Linton Blvd, Delray Beach, FL 33484

Grade Levels: Pre-K through 12th Grade


Located in nearby Delray Beach, American Heritage School is a prestigious non-religious private school known for its academic rigor and emphasis on college preparation. The school offers a challenging curriculum, including Advanced Placement (AP) courses, honors programs, and a specialized Pre-Med track for students interested in healthcare professions. American Heritage is also renowned for its state-of-the-art facilities and strong programs in the arts, athletics, and science.


Why It Stands Out:

With a high college acceptance rate and a wide variety of extracurricular activities, American Heritage prepares students for success in both academics and personal development. The school’s robust STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering, and Math) programs and fine arts offerings make it an excellent option for families seeking a balanced education.


North Broward Preparatory School

Location: 7600 Lyons Rd, Coconut Creek, FL 33073

Grade Levels: Pre-K through 12th Grade


North Broward Preparatory School is one of the top private schools in South Florida, known for its prestigious International Baccalaureate (IB) program and exceptional academic track record. While it is located in nearby Coconut Creek, many Deerfield Beach families choose North Broward because of its world-class faculty, state-of-the-art facilities, and diverse extracurricular programs. The school offers a global education that prepares students for college and life in a competitive, interconnected world.


Why It Stands Out:

North Broward Preparatory’s IB program and commitment to individualized learning make it a standout for families seeking a challenging and enriching educational environment. The school also offers excellent arts, athletics, and technology programs, providing students with a wide range of opportunities to explore their interests and talents.


Deerfield Beach and the surrounding areas offer several best private schools for families looking for a high-quality, personalized education for their children. Whether you prioritize academic rigor, faith-based instruction, or extracurricular activities, there’s a school in the area that fits your needs. As you explore these schools, be sure to schedule visits and meet with administrators to find The Best Private Schools in Deerfield Beach for your kids.
