2025 Boca Raton Events Calendar: What to Expect Near Your Rental Home

As Boca Raton approaches its centennial in 2025, the city is gearing up for a year-long celebration filled with events that reflect its rich history and vibrant community spirit. Residents and visitors alike can look forward to a diverse lineup of festivities throughout the year. Here's a curated list of some standout events to mark on your calendar:

1. Boca Street Fest

On Saturday, January 25, 2025, from 12:00 PM to 4:00 PM, Boca Raton will launch its centennial celebrations with the inaugural Boca Street Fest at Mizner Park Amphitheater, located at 590 Plaza Real, Boca Raton, FL 33432.

Event Highlights:

  • Main Stage Performances:

    • Spred the Dub: South Florida's renowned "feel good reggae" band.

    • Wolfhawk: Known for their high-energy and melodic rock tunes.

    • Leave It To Us: Delivering energizing and upbeat pop-punk sounds.

  • Community Stage Acts:

    • Fusho Daiko: Japanese Taiko drumming ensemble.

    • Mariena en El Mundo: Peruvian dance troupe.

    • Aloha Islanders: Polynesian dance performers.

    • Gratitude Steel Band: Island steel drum musicians.

    • Rhythms School: Indian classical dance group.

    • Miami Klezmer Band: Yiddish and Jewish musical ensemble.

  • Beer Garden: Featuring local brews, it's an ideal spot to relax and immerse oneself in the festival's ambiance.

  • Marketplace: Mizner Park's interior will transform into a pedestrian promenade, hosting a variety of local vendors, artisans, food stalls, and the park's resident businesses.

Additional Information:

  • Admission: Free for all attendees.

  • Parking: Complimentary parking is available at City Hall (201 W. Palmetto Park Rd), Downtown Library (400 NW 2nd Ave), and Building Administration (200 NW 2nd Ave).

  • Contact: For more details, email CommunityEvents@myboca.us.

Boca Street Fest is more than just an event; it's a celebration of Boca Raton's rich cultural tapestry, offering residents and visitors an opportunity to come together and commemorate the city's 100-year journey.

  1. Festival of the Arts BOCA: Centennial Celebration

On Sunday, March 9, 2025, the Festival of the Arts BOCA will culminate its 19th annual celebration with a special Centennial Celebration at the Mizner Park Amphitheater, located at 590 Plaza Real, Boca Raton, FL 33432.

Event Highlights:

  • Festival Boca Jazz Orchestra Performance: The evening will feature the Festival Boca Jazz Orchestra, conducted by Kyle Prescott, performing music that spans nearly every decade of Boca Raton's rich 100-year history.

  • World Premiere Composition: Attendees will be treated to the world premiere of a specially commissioned piece by Florida Atlantic University composer Kevin Wilt, created exclusively for this centennial occasion.

Additional Information:

  • Tickets: Prices range from $15 to $120 and are available for purchase at

  • Contact: For more details, call (561) 757-4762 between 9 a.m. and 9 p.m.

This centennial celebration not only honors Boca Raton's past but also highlights its cultural evolution, making it a must-attend event for residents and visitors alike.

  1. Centennial Celebration Weekend Concert

On Saturday, May 24, 2025, Boca Raton will host a Centennial Celebration Weekend Concert at the Mizner Park Amphitheater, located at 590 Plaza Real, Boca Raton, FL 33432. This event is a highlight of the city's year-long centennial festivities, bringing together musical talents that reflect Boca Raton's diverse cultural heritage. Specific performers and ticket information will be announced closer to the date.

The Centennial Celebration Weekend Concert is part of a series of events commemorating Boca Raton's 100-year journey, honoring the visionaries who have shaped the city and celebrating the diverse and thriving community it has become.

Residents and visitors are encouraged to mark their calendars for this memorable evening of music and community spirit. As more details become available, updates will be provided on the official Boca Raton Centennial website.

Stay tuned for further announcements and prepare to join in the festivities as Boca Raton commemorates its centennial milestone.

4. Centennial Celebration Weekend Drone Show

On Sunday, May 25, 2025, Boca Raton will host a Centennial Celebration Weekend Drone Show at the Boca Raton Innovation Campus (BRiC), located at 5000 T-Rex Avenue, Boca Raton, FL 33431.

Event Highlights:

  • Spectacular Drone Display: Attendees will witness a mesmerizing drone show that will illuminate the night sky, showcasing synchronized drones crafting intricate patterns and narratives that honor Boca Raton's 100-year history.

  • Historic Venue: Hosting the event at BRiC is particularly significant, as it stands as the birthplace of the IBM personal computer, symbolizing Boca Raton's pioneering role in technology and innovation.

Additional Information:

  • Admission: The event is free and open to the public, offering a unique opportunity for residents and visitors to come together in celebration.

  • Family-Friendly Atmosphere: Designed to captivate audiences of all ages, the drone show promises to be a visual spectacle that reflects the city's innovative spirit and forward-thinking vision.

As Boca Raton commemorates its centennial, this drone show serves as a testament to the city's rich history and its continual evolution as a hub of invention and progress. Attendees are encouraged to arrive early to secure optimal viewing spots and immerse themselves in this awe-inspiring tribute to Boca Raton's past, present, and future.

For more details and updates on the Centennial Celebration Weekend Drone Show, visit the official Boca Raton Centennial website.


5. Holiday Street Parade

On Thursday, December 4, 2025, from 7:30 PM to 9:00 PM, Boca Raton will host its 53rd Annual Holiday Street Parade, concluding the city's centennial celebrations with a vibrant display of community spirit.

Event Highlights:

  • Festive Floats and Performances: Over 60 decorated floats, community groups, and bands will parade along Federal Highway, starting at SE 5th Street and proceeding north to the Mizner Park Amphitheater. This year's theme, "Boca Raton's Centennial Celebration," promises a spectacular show celebrating the city's 100-year history.

Additional Information:

  • Admission: Free and open to the public.

  • Parking: Complimentary parking is available at several locations, including City Hall (201 W. Palmetto Park Rd), Building Administration (200 NW 2nd Ave), City Parking Lot (300 NW 2nd Ave), and Downtown Boca Library (400 NW 2nd Ave).

  • Road Closures: Federal Highway will be closed from Camino Real to Glades Road from approximately 5:00 PM to 11:00 PM. Traffic will be directed to Dixie Highway or surrounding streets.

Residents and visitors are encouraged to arrive early, utilize alternative transportation methods such as walking, biking, carpooling, or ride-sharing services, and secure their viewing spots along the parade route.

For more information and updates on the Holiday Street Parade, visit the official City of Boca Raton Centennial website.

Join the community in celebrating Boca Raton's rich history and the festive season at this cherished annual tradition.

Beyond these signature events, Boca Raton will host various community happenings, art exhibitions, and cultural festivals throughout 2025. Whether you're a long-time resident or a visitor, the centennial year offers a unique opportunity to engage with the community, celebrate the city's history, and look forward to its promising future.

For a comprehensive and up-to-date list of events, visit the official Boca Raton Centennial
