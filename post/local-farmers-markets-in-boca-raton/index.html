Local Farmers’ Markets in Boca Raton

Boca Raton, Florida, is known for its upscale lifestyle, beautiful beaches, and vibrant community. However, beyond the luxury and the coastline, Boca Raton is also home to a variety of farmers’ markets that bring fresh, locally grown produce, artisanal products, and handmade goods to residents and visitors alike. Whether you’re looking for organic fruits and vegetables, delicious baked goods, or unique crafts, the local farmers’ markets in Boca Raton have something for everyone. Here’s a guide to the best farmers’ markets in Boca Raton.


1. Farmer's Boca Raton Green Market

The Boca Raton Green Market is one of the most popular and longest-running farmers’ markets in the area. Held every Saturday during the fall and winter months, this market offers a wide variety of local produce, fresh flowers, artisan cheeses, homemade baked goods, and much more. Shoppers can also find handmade crafts, organic skincare products, and freshly prepared foods from local vendors. Located in downtown Boca Raton, the market is a community gathering spot and a great way to spend a Saturday morning.

  • Location: Royal Palm Place, 100 N.E. Mizner Blvd, Boca Raton

  • Open: Saturdays, 8 AM to 1 PM (October through May)

  • Highlights: Fresh produce, artisan foods, handmade crafts, live music


2. Boca Raton Green Market at Mizner Park

An extension of the original Boca Raton Green Market, the Green Market at Mizner Park is another excellent option for fresh produce and local goods. The market features an array of fresh fruits, vegetables, flowers, and homemade items such as honey, jams, and sauces. Held in the heart of Mizner Park, Boca Raton’s premier shopping and dining destination, this farmers’ market combines the best of local farming with the sophisticated atmosphere of the city’s most popular gathering spot. It’s perfect for those who want to shop for groceries and enjoy a day of boutique shopping and dining.

  • Location: Mizner Park, 327 Plaza Real, Boca Raton

  • Open: Select Sundays, 10 AM to 4 PM (seasonal)

  • Highlights: Fresh, local produce, artisan honey and sauces, prime shopping location


3. Boca Center Farmers Market

For those looking for a more intimate market experience, the Boca Center Farmers Market offers a carefully curated selection of local vendors selling fresh produce, baked goods, and artisanal items. Located at Boca Center, a popular shopping and dining destination, this market is ideal for picking up a few fresh ingredients for dinner or discovering unique, handcrafted goods. The smaller size of this market makes it a relaxed and easy shopping experience, perfect for a quick visit.

  • Location: Boca Center, 5150 Town Center Circle, Boca Raton

  • Open: Sundays, 10 AM to 2 PM (seasonal)

  • Highlights: Fresh fruits and vegetables, artisanal breads, local handmade goods


4. Parkland Farmers’ Market

Just a short drive from Boca Raton, the Parkland Farmers’ Market is a great option for those looking for a larger market with a wide selection of local products. Located in the nearby city of Parkland, this market offers everything from organic produce and freshly baked goods to gourmet coffee and handmade crafts. With food trucks, live music, and kid-friendly activities, it’s more than just a shopping experience—it’s a fun weekend outing for the whole family.

  • Location: 8350 Ranch Rd, Parkland

  • Open: Sundays, 9 AM to 1 PM (twice a month, October through April)

  • Highlights: Organic produce, food trucks, live entertainment, family-friendly atmosphere


5. Delray Beach GreenMarket

Another short drive from Boca Raton, the Delray Beach GreenMarket is one of the top markets in South Florida. Held in downtown Delray Beach, this market boasts an impressive selection of vendors offering fresh produce, baked goods, prepared foods, and artisanal products. The market is known for its friendly vibe and community feel, making it a favorite for both locals and tourists. You can grab a coffee and pastry while browsing the market, or pick up fresh ingredients for a homemade meal. This is a must-visit for anyone looking to explore the area’s best farmers’ markets.

  • Location: Old School Square, 51 N Swinton Ave, Delray Beach

  • Open: Saturdays, 9 AM to 2 PM (October through May)

  • Highlights: Fresh produce, baked goods, locally crafted items, vibrant community atmosphere


Shopping at farmers’ markets in Boca Raton offers numerous benefits for both shoppers and the community. Here’s why these markets are a valuable resource:

  • Fresh, Organic Produce: Farmers’ markets often feature locally grown, organic fruits and vegetables that are picked at peak freshness. This ensures better flavor and higher nutritional value compared to produce shipped from long distances.

  • Support Local Farmers and Artisans: Buying directly from local farmers and artisans helps support small businesses and the local economy. It also promotes sustainable farming practices and reduces the environmental impact of long-distance transportation.

  • Healthier Choices: Many vendors at farmers’ markets offer organic and non-GMO produce, as well as homemade products made with natural ingredients. Shopping at these markets makes it easier to incorporate healthier, fresh foods into your diet.

  • Unique Finds: From handcrafted goods to locally made jams and sauces, farmers’ markets are a great place to discover unique products that you won’t find in traditional grocery stores.

  • Community Engagement: Farmers’ markets are more than just shopping destinations—they’re community events that bring people together. Many markets feature live music, local performers, and family-friendly activities, creating a fun and vibrant atmosphere for all ages.


Boca Raton’s farmers’ markets offer a wonderful opportunity to enjoy fresh, local produce and support the community. From the bustling Boca Raton Green Market to smaller, intimate markets like Boca Center, there’s something for everyone. Whether you’re looking to shop for healthy ingredients, explore handmade crafts, or simply spend a relaxing morning enjoying local flavors, Boca Raton’s farmers’ markets provide the perfect setting.
