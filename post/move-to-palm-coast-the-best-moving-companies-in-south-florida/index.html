Move to Palm Coast: The Best Moving Companies in South Florida

Palm Coast, with its beautiful coastlines, lush parks, and vibrant communities, is an increasingly popular place to call home. If you're considering a move to this scenic Florida town, finding a reliable and efficient moving company can make all the difference. The right moving company ensures a smooth transition, from packing up your current residence to settling into your new Palm Coast property. Here’s a look at some of the best moving companies in South Florida, known for excellent customer service, professional handling, and competitive pricing.


All My Sons Moving & Storage

All My Sons Moving & Storage is a family-owned business with over 20 years of experience, known for its customer-centric approach. They offer comprehensive moving services, including local and long-distance moves, packing and unpacking, and storage solutions. All My Sons also provides specialized care for high-value items like pianos, antiques, and fine art, making them a top choice for those who need extra protection for their valuables.

They’re highly rated for their professionalism and efficiency, with many customers highlighting their clear communication and timely service.



Good Greek Moving & Storage

Good Greek Moving & Storage has built a reputation as one of the most reliable moving companies in South Florida. They offer a full suite of services, including packing, moving, storage, and specialty item handling. Good Greek Moving also provides customizable moving plans to cater to both residential and commercial clients. Their “Best Move Ever” guarantee is a commitment to providing high-quality service, promising safe handling and timely delivery of belongings.

Good Greek’s transparent pricing and emphasis on customer satisfaction have made them a popular choice for those moving to Palm Coast.



Two Men and a Truck

Two Men and a Truck is a well-known name in the moving industry, with numerous franchises across South Florida. They are recognized for their efficient, friendly service and flexible scheduling options. Services include packing and unpacking, furniture rearrangement, and labor-only options for customers who prefer to handle the packing themselves.

Two Men and a Truck also offers specialized packing materials to protect fragile items during transport. Their commitment to customer satisfaction and positive reviews makes them a reliable choice for those moving to Palm Coast.



Joe Bonnie & Son Moving & Storage

With over 70 years of experience, Joe Bonnie & Son Moving & Storage is a family-owned and operated company with a strong commitment to quality service. Based in Delray Beach, they specialize in both local and long-distance moves, including relocation services to and from Palm Coast. The company also offers packing, custom crating, and climate-controlled storage options. Joe Bonnie & Son is noted for its professional staff and careful handling of items.

They are ideal for individuals who want a smaller, family-owned company with deep local roots and a long-standing reputation for reliability.



FlatRate Moving

FlatRate Moving brings an innovative pricing model to the South Florida moving industry, offering fixed-price moving solutions based on item inventory rather than hourly rates. This company is ideal for budget-conscious movers who want a clear and predictable price upfront. They offer a range of services, including long-distance and international moving, packing and unpacking, and storage solutions. FlatRate Moving has a strong reputation for punctuality and professionalism.

Many customers appreciate the transparent pricing and detailed inventory management, which reduces surprises during the moving process.



Miami Movers for Less

For those seeking a budget-friendly moving service, Miami Movers for Less offers competitive rates without sacrificing quality. They provide local and long-distance moving services, as well as storage and packing services. This company has a reputation for reliability, and they’re well-reviewed by customers who emphasize their affordability and efficiency.

Miami Movers for Less is a good option for individuals and families moving on a budget, with services tailored to meet essential moving needs.



Father & Son Moving & Storage

Father & Son Moving & Storage has been serving South Florida for over 40 years, specializing in both residential and commercial moves. Known for their detailed approach, Father & Son offers packing, storage, and special item transportation, including pianos and artwork. They are highly regarded for their careful handling and prompt service, with a focus on customer satisfaction.

Their seasoned team and variety of service options make them a trusted choice for those looking to relocate to Palm Coast.



Tips for Choosing the Right Moving Company

When moving to a new area, finding the best moving company for your specific needs is essential. Here are some tips to help guide your selection:

  • Compare Quotes: Obtain quotes from at least three moving companies to compare rates, services, and potential fees.

  • Check for Licensing and Insurance: Confirm that the company is licensed and insured, which is especially important for long-distance moves.

  • Read Reviews: Check customer reviews on reputable sites like Yelp, Google, or the Better Business Bureau to understand past client experiences.

  • Ask About Specialty Services: If you have items that require special handling, ensure the company has experience with items like pianos, antiques, or fine art.

  • Inquire About Storage Options: Many moving companies offer temporary or long-term storage solutions, which can be helpful if there’s a gap between move-out and move-in dates.
