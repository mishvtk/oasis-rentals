Palm Coast's Most Instagrammable Spots

Palm Coast, Florida, is known for its natural beauty, serene beaches, and stunning parks, making it a perfect destination for capturing memorable moments on camera. Whether you’re a visitor or a resident looking to refresh your Instagram feed, Palm Coast offers plenty of scenic spots to explore. From sunset views to lush green trails, here are some of Palm Coast’s most photogenic locations—and a comfortable home base to return to, with Oasis Rentals.


Washington Oaks Gardens State Park Palm Coast's Instagrammable Spots

With its lush gardens, sprawling oak trees, and unique coquina rock formations along the shoreline, Washington Oaks Gardens State Park is a treasure trove for photography lovers. The park offers rose gardens, koi ponds, and shaded pathways, each with a unique setting for the perfect shot. The best time to capture photos here is in the early morning when the light filters softly through the trees.



Princess Place Preserve

Located along the Matanzas River, Princess Place Preserve is a stunning mix of historical architecture and natural beauty. The rustic lodge, scenic water views, and sprawling oak-lined trails create a serene environment perfect for capturing rustic outdoor shots. It’s especially popular for golden hour photography, where the light casts a warm glow across the landscape.


The Hammock Beach Resort

For a luxurious beachside aesthetic, Hammock Beach Resort offers Instagrammable views of the ocean, pools, and coastal landscape. Even if you’re not staying at the resort, you can enjoy nearby beaches and walkways that showcase the Atlantic Ocean’s beauty. The surrounding beach areas are ideal for seaside sunset photos, with pink and golden hues reflecting off the water.



Bing’s Landing

This waterfront park along the Intracoastal Waterway is a popular spot for outdoor enthusiasts and photographers alike. Bing’s Landing offers boat ramps, a fishing pier, and scenic picnic areas, all surrounded by coastal flora. The park also houses the Mala Compra Plantation Archaeological Site, adding a touch of history to your shots. It’s a fantastic place for nature photography, with plenty of opportunities to capture Florida’s wildlife.



European Village

For a unique and colorful setting, the European Village in Palm Coast is a popular spot for both locals and tourists. The European-inspired architecture, with its charming facades, cobblestone walkways, and cozy courtyards, makes it an ideal location for lifestyle and food photography. Snap a picture in front of one of the many restaurants or boutique storefronts, especially during evening hours when lights add a warm ambiance.



Waterfront Park

Palm Coast’s Waterfront Park offers stunning views of the Intracoastal Waterway and is an ideal spot for sunrise photography. The park has paved walking paths, benches, and beautiful landscaping, making it perfect for both nature shots and portraits. With the golden morning light, this park provides a serene, peaceful setting for your feed.



Linear Park

Linear Park is a hidden gem that’s perfect for nature photography. The 57-acre park features a series of trails lined with lush vegetation, ponds, and a butterfly garden. A boardwalk runs through part of the park, offering a peaceful path through scenic greenery. This spot is ideal for quiet, nature-inspired photos that capture Palm Coast’s natural beauty.
