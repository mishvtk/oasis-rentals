The Best Art Galleries and Museums in Palm Coast

Palm Coast, Florida, may be best known for its scenic beaches and outdoor activities, but it also has a thriving arts and culture scene. Whether you're a local resident or a visitor looking to explore the artistic side of the area, Palm Coast and its surrounding cities offer a variety of art galleries and museums that showcase local talent, historical artifacts, and world-class exhibitions. Here’s a guide to the best art galleries and museums in and around Palm Coast.


1. Flagler County Art League in Palm Coast

Located in the heart of Palm Coast, the Flagler County Art League (FCAL) is a hub for local artists and art enthusiasts. This gallery regularly hosts rotating exhibitions featuring a diverse range of artwork, from paintings and photography to sculptures and mixed media. FCAL also offers workshops and classes for those looking to develop their artistic skills. It’s a great place to see the work of local artists and to participate in the vibrant arts community of Palm Coast.

  • Location: City Marketplace, Palm Coast

  • Highlights: Local art exhibits, art classes, workshops

  • Website: flaglercountyartleague.org


2. Florida Agricultural Museum

Just a short drive from Palm Coast, the Florida Agricultural Museum in nearby Palm Coast is not your typical art museum, but it offers a fascinating look into Florida’s agricultural history through historical exhibits, architecture, and hands-on experiences. The museum is set on over 400 acres of farmland, where visitors can explore restored historic buildings, old-fashioned tools, and artifacts that highlight Florida’s rural past. The museum also hosts art-related events and festivals throughout the year.

  • Location: 7900 Old Kings Rd N, Palm Coast

  • Highlights: Historical exhibits, agricultural artifacts, family-friendly events

  • Website: floridaagmuseum.org


3. Gallery of Local Art (GOLA) in Palm Coast

Located in nearby Flagler Beach, just a short drive from Palm Coast, the Gallery of Local Art (GOLA) is a charming space that showcases work by local artists in a variety of mediums. GOLA is well-known for its community-oriented approach, supporting both established and emerging artists in the area. Visitors can browse a collection of paintings, photography, jewelry, and crafts. The gallery also frequently hosts art classes, giving art lovers the chance to create their own masterpieces while being inspired by the local talent.

  • Location: 208 S Central Ave, Flagler Beach

  • Highlights: Local art for sale, art classes, coastal-inspired works

  • Website: galleryoflocalart.com


4. Ormond Memorial Art Museum & Gardens

While not located directly in Palm Coast, the Ormond Memorial Art Museum & Gardens is a short drive south to Ormond Beach and is well worth the trip. The museum features rotating exhibitions of contemporary art, focusing on a variety of mediums including painting, sculpture, and photography. In addition to the art exhibits, the museum is home to beautiful gardens, which provide a peaceful outdoor space for visitors to explore. The museum also honors veterans with its memorial and is known for its commitment to art education, offering workshops and events throughout the year.

  • Location: 78 E Granada Blvd, Ormond Beach

  • Highlights: Contemporary art exhibits, memorial gardens, art workshops

  • Website: ormondartmuseum.org


5. Cultural Arts Center at the Palm Coast Community Center

Palm Coast’s Cultural Arts Center, located within the Palm Coast Community Center, regularly hosts art exhibitions and cultural events. While not a traditional museum, the center features works from local artists and art students, giving residents a chance to engage with the creative community. The space is also used for workshops and cultural programming, making it a versatile venue for promoting arts and culture in the city.

  • Location: 305 Palm Coast Parkway NE, Palm Coast

  • Highlights: Local art exhibits, cultural events, community workshops

  • Website: palmcoastgov.com/community-center


6. Southeast Museum of Photography

For a more in-depth dive into photography, a visit to the Southeast Museum of Photography in nearby Daytona Beach is a must. It’s one of the largest photography museums in the southeastern United States and offers a wide range of exhibitions, showcasing everything from historical photography to cutting-edge contemporary work. The museum is part of Daytona State College and offers free admission, making it an accessible and enriching destination for art lovers.

  • Location: 1200 W International Speedway Blvd, Daytona Beach

  • Highlights: Historical and contemporary photography exhibits, lectures, and educational programs

  • Website: smponline.org


7. St. Augustine Art Association

Located about 30 miles from Palm Coast, the St. Augustine Art Association is one of the oldest and most respected art organizations in the region. This gallery hosts a wide range of art exhibitions throughout the year, featuring work by local, regional, and national artists. From traditional paintings and sculptures to experimental art, the gallery offers something for every art enthusiast. The association also plays a vital role in promoting arts education, offering workshops, youth programs, and lectures.

  • Location: 22 Marine St, St. Augustine

  • Highlights: Rotating art exhibits, historic art association, art workshops

  • Website: staaa.org


Palm Coast and its surrounding areas offer a diverse selection of art galleries and museums that cater to all tastes, from contemporary art to historical exhibits. Whether you're looking to explore local talent or take a cultural day trip to nearby cities, there’s no shortage of artistic and cultural experiences for art lovers to enjoy.

By visiting these galleries and museums, Palm Coast residents and visitors alike can support local artists, learn more about Florida’s rich history, and take part in a vibrant and growing arts community.
