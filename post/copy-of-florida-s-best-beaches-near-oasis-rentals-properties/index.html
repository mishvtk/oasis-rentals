Florida’s Best Beaches Near Oasis Rentals Properties

Florida’s coastline offers some of the most beautiful beaches in the United States, and if you’re living in an Oasis Rentals property, you’re never far from a stunning spot to relax by the water. Whether you’re in Palm Coast, Deerfield Beach, or Boca Raton, you’ll find a beach that suits your vibe—whether it’s for family fun, peaceful solitude, or exciting activities like snorkeling and paddleboarding. Here’s a guide to the Florida's best beaches near Oasis Rentals properties and what makes each unique.


1. Palm Coast: Flagler Beach

Why Visit?

Located just minutes from Palm Coast, Flagler Beach is a charming seaside escape with a laid-back vibe. It’s known for its reddish-golden sands, friendly atmosphere, and lack of high-rise buildings, preserving its Old Florida charm.

Highlights:

  • Outdoor Activities: Surfing, fishing, and kayaking are popular here, with plenty of open space to enjoy the water.

  • Dining: Check out beachfront eateries like The Golden Lion Cafe for fresh seafood and tropical drinks.

  • Pet-Friendly: Flagler Beach welcomes dogs, making it perfect for pet owners.

Proximity to Oasis Rentals:

Approximately a 15-minute drive from most Palm Coast rental properties.



2. Deerfield Beach: Deerfield Beach International Fishing Pier

Why Visit?

Deerfield Beach, awarded the “Blue Wave” certification for its clean and pristine environment, is a favorite for locals and visitors alike. Its crystal-clear waters and well-maintained facilities make it a standout destination.

Highlights:

  • Fishing Pier: The iconic Deerfield Beach Pier is perfect for anglers or anyone who loves ocean views.

  • Water Sports: Rent paddleboards or jet skis right on the beach for some aquatic adventure.

  • Quiet Water Park: Nearby, this park offers a mix of freshwater fun and outdoor activities.

Proximity to Oasis Rentals:

Located within a short drive or even walking distance for many Deerfield Beach properties.



3. Boca Raton: South Beach Park

Why Visit?

South Beach Park in Boca Raton is a peaceful, family-friendly beach that strikes the perfect balance between beauty and accessibility. Its lush dune-lined paths and clear waters make it a favorite for locals.

Highlights:

  • Scenic Paths: Walk along the boardwalk through natural Florida vegetation.

  • Calm Waters: Ideal for swimming or paddleboarding, with lifeguards on duty.

  • Picnic Areas: Enjoy a family meal with tables shaded by palm trees.

Proximity to Oasis Rentals:

A quick 5-10 minute drive from Boca Raton properties managed by Oasis Rentals.


4. Palm Coast: Washington Oaks Gardens State Park

Why Visit?

While primarily known for its gardens, this park boasts a unique shoreline with coquina rock formations that create dramatic and photogenic scenery. It’s a quieter alternative to busier beaches.

Highlights:

  • Natural Beauty: Explore the beach’s rocky tide pools or stroll through the formal gardens.

  • Wildlife Watching: Keep an eye out for dolphins and coastal birds.

  • Perfect for Photography: The combination of rocks, ocean, and lush greenery offers stunning photo opportunities.

Proximity to Oasis Rentals:

Approximately a 20-minute drive from Palm Coast properties.



5. Boca Raton: Red Reef Park

Why Visit?

Red Reef Park is a haven for snorkeling enthusiasts, with an underwater reef just offshore. It’s a great spot to explore marine life without needing a boat or scuba gear.

Highlights:

  • Snorkeling: See tropical fish, sea turtles, and other marine life close to shore.

  • Nature Trails: The Gumbo Limbo Nature Center is nearby and offers educational programs and a butterfly garden.

  • Family-Friendly: With picnic facilities and restrooms, it’s convenient for a day trip.

Proximity to Oasis Rentals:

Less than 10 minutes from most Boca Raton properties.


6. Deerfield Beach: South Inlet Park

Why Visit?

This smaller beach is perfect for a more tranquil experience, with fewer crowds and a scenic inlet setting. It’s ideal for picnics, quiet reflection, or just watching boats drift by.

Highlights:

  • Secluded Feel: Enjoy a quieter beach away from the hustle of main tourist spots.

  • Fishing: Great for fishing enthusiasts, with spots along the inlet.

  • Playground: A fun spot for families with young children.

Proximity to Oasis Rentals:

Only a short drive from Deerfield Beach properties.



Tips for Visiting Florida’s Best Beaches

  • Pack Smart: Don’t forget sunscreen, water, and reusable bags to help protect the environment.

  • Check Parking: Some beaches have limited parking, so arrive early or use rideshare services.

  • Be Mindful of Wildlife: Respect sea turtle nesting areas and follow local guidelines to protect the environment.
