Best Public Library Activities for Kids in Boca Raton

Boca Raton isn’t just known for its beaches and vibrant community; it’s also home to several public libraries that offer fantastic programs for children of all ages. Whether you’re a local resident or planning a long-term stay, these libraries provide a great way to keep your kids engaged, learning, and entertained throughout the year. From storytimes to STEM workshops, here’s a roundup of some of the best public library activities for kids in Boca Raton.


1. Story Time Adventures

One of the most beloved programs for younger children is storytime, where librarians bring books to life through animated readings, songs, and interactive activities. The Downtown Library offers regular storytimes for toddlers and preschoolers, helping little ones develop a love for reading early on. These sessions typically include fun themes like animals, space, or favorite characters, making each visit exciting and different.

Storytime isn’t just about listening to a book—it often includes songs, fingerplays, and crafts that go along with the theme of the day. Kids get to be creative, socialize with peers, and explore their imaginations, while parents enjoy watching their children engage with literature in an interactive way.


2. STEM for Kids

Boca Raton’s public libraries also provide a wide variety of STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering, and Math) programs designed to engage kids in hands-on learning. The Spanish River Library offers STEM workshops that cover topics like robotics, coding, and basic engineering. These activities are often geared toward elementary and middle school students, making them perfect for children who are curious about how things work and love to tinker.

Programs like LEGO Robotics allow kids to build and program their own robots, while STEAM (Science, Technology, Engineering, Art, Math) Storytime merges storytelling with educational crafts and experiments. By integrating creative and logical thinking, these sessions ensure kids have fun while learning valuable skills for the future.


3. Arts and Crafts Workshops

If your child is more artistically inclined, Boca Raton’s libraries offer plenty of opportunities to get crafty. Kids can participate in DIY craft workshops, where they’ll make everything from holiday-themed decorations to unique art projects. The Summer Craft Series is especially popular, featuring themed crafts that allow kids to express their creativity while learning about new techniques like painting, drawing, or even sculpting.

The Downtown Library frequently hosts seasonal craft events, such as creating Halloween masks, holiday ornaments, and spring flower collages. These activities not only spark creativity but also improve fine motor skills and concentration.


4. Reading Challenges and Book Clubs

For kids who love reading, the libraries offer Summer Reading Programs and year-round Reading Challenges that encourage children to read more books and explore different genres. Children earn prizes and certificates for completing reading goals, motivating them to spend more time with books and less time on screens.

Additionally, the Junior Book Club at the Spanish River Library is a great way for kids ages 8-12 to dive deeper into their favorite stories and discuss them with peers. These book clubs are designed to inspire critical thinking, improve comprehension skills, and foster a lifelong love of reading.


5. Technology and Digital Literacy Classes

In today’s digital world, it’s important for children to develop tech skills early. Boca Raton’s libraries offer classes in digital literacy, where kids can learn basic computer skills, internet safety, and how to use creative software programs. These sessions are designed to help children become comfortable navigating technology, providing them with the tools they’ll need both in school and in everyday life.

Programs such as “Coding for Kids” introduce young learners to coding languages in a fun, game-like environment. They learn how to build simple websites, create animations, and even design basic video games. These tech-driven activities not only sharpen problem-solving skills but also foster innovation and creativity.


6. Music and Movement Classes

For younger children who love to move and groove, Boca Raton’s libraries offer music and movement classes that combine dancing, singing, and instrument play. Programs like “Music Together” are perfect for toddlers and preschoolers, as they encourage physical activity while exposing children to the basics of rhythm, melody, and musical expression.

These classes help children develop their motor skills, coordination, and a sense of rhythm. Plus, they’re a great way to burn off energy in a structured, educational environment!


7. Special Events and Performances

Throughout the year, Boca Raton’s libraries host special events for children, including puppet shows, live performances, and themed parties. These events are often tied to holidays or special occasions, providing unique experiences like Halloween costume parties, holiday sing-alongs, and interactive storytelling sessions with professional performers.

Parents should keep an eye on the libraries’ event calendars, as these special events can fill up quickly and are always a hit with kids and families alike.


8. Family-Friendly Movie Nights

Another great way to enjoy time together as a family is through the libraries’ Family Movie Nights. Held at both the Downtown and Spanish River locations, these screenings feature kid-friendly films in a relaxed, community-centered environment. Children are encouraged to wear pajamas, bring pillows or blankets, and enjoy popcorn with other families.

Movie selections often include animated classics, family-friendly comedies, and seasonal favorites, creating a perfect evening out that’s fun for everyone.
