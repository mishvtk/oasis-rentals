Palm Coast's Best Fitness Centers and Health-Focused Amenities for Renters

​Palm Coast, Florida, offers a variety of fitness centers and health-focused amenities that cater to renters seeking an active and wellness-oriented lifestyle. Here are some top options to consider:​

Anytime Fitness: 24/7 Access to Health and Wellness

For those seeking a convenient and well-equipped fitness center in Palm Coast, Anytime Fitness stands out as a top choice. Located at 260 Cypress Edge Dr. Ste. 106, this gym offers 24/7 access to a comprehensive range of fitness equipment and amenities tailored to support an active lifestyle.

State-of-the-Art Equipment and Facilities

Anytime Fitness is designed to accommodate all fitness levels, whether you're a beginner or a seasoned athlete. The gym features:

  • Cardio Equipment: Treadmills, elliptical cross-trainers, rowing machines, and stationary bikes.

  • Strength Training: Free weights, squat racks, cable machines, and resistance training stations.

  • Functional Training Area: Kettlebells, battle ropes, medicine balls, and more for dynamic workouts.

Personalized Fitness Plans

One of the key advantages of Anytime Fitness is its commitment to helping members achieve their goals. Members can access:

  • Personal Training Services – Certified trainers provide customized workout plans and coaching.

  • Virtual Training Programs – Digital resources and guided workouts for those who prefer independent fitness routines.

  • Wellness Support – Nutritional advice and progress tracking tools to ensure long-term success.

Additional Amenities for a Complete Fitness Experience

Beyond equipment and training, Anytime Fitness offers various perks to enhance the workout experience:

  • Private Restrooms and Showers – Ensuring a clean and comfortable environment.

  • Tanning Facilities – Available for members looking to maintain a sun-kissed glow year-round.

  • Massage Chair/Bed – Helping members recover and relax post-workout.

Global Access for a Flexible Lifestyle

A standout feature of Anytime Fitness is its global gym access. Members can work out at any of the 5,000+ locations worldwide, making it ideal for frequent travelers or those who relocate.

Why Choose Anytime Fitness?

  • Open 24/7, allowing members to work out at their convenience.

  • A welcoming and non-intimidating environment suitable for all fitness levels.

  • Personalized training options to help individuals achieve their health and wellness goals.

  • Access to a wide range of equipment and additional amenities that enhance the gym experience.

For those looking for a flexible, high-quality, and accessible fitness center, Anytime Fitness in Palm Coast is a fantastic option. Whether you're focused on strength training, weight loss, or overall health improvement, this gym provides the tools and support needed for success.

Peak Fitness & Rehab: Elevating Health and Performance in Flagler County

For those in Flagler County seeking a fitness center that prioritizes both performance and rehabilitation, Peak Fitness & Rehab is a premier destination. Known for its personalized approach and expert-designed programs, this facility offers a comprehensive blend of strength training, rehabilitation, and wellness services to help individuals reach their peak health.

Comprehensive Training and Rehabilitation Programs

At Peak Fitness & Rehab, members have access to a wide range of services designed by physical therapists and strength and conditioning specialists, ensuring a well-rounded approach to health and fitness. Their programs include:

  • Personal Training – Customized workout plans based on individual fitness levels and goals.

  • Pilates – A focus on core strength, flexibility, and improved posture through guided exercises.

  • Golf Fitness – Specialized training to enhance strength, flexibility, and coordination for golfers.

  • Concierge Physical Therapy – One-on-one therapy sessions tailored for recovery and injury prevention.

A Supportive and Family-Oriented Approach

What sets Peak Fitness & Rehab apart is its community-driven philosophy. Clients are not just members—they are treated like family. The staff prioritizes accountability, motivation, and personalized attention, ensuring each person receives the guidance they need to succeed.

Why Choose Peak Fitness & Rehab?

  • Expert-designed programs backed by physical therapy and strength training professionals.

  • A holistic approach that focuses on both performance and rehabilitation.

  • Specialized services, including golf fitness and concierge physical therapy, catering to unique needs.

  • A welcoming environment that fosters accountability, motivation, and long-term wellness.

For those looking to enhance their fitness, recover from injuries, or build a stronger body, Peak Fitness & Rehab provides the tools and support needed to achieve lasting results.

Pointe Grand Palm Coast Apartments: Luxury Living with Health-Focused Amenities

For renters seeking a blend of luxury, convenience, and wellness, Pointe Grand Palm Coast Apartments stands out as a premier choice. This brand-new apartment community offers modern two-bedroom units designed with upscale interiors, complemented by a variety of health-focused amenities that promote an active lifestyle.

A Focus on Health and Fitness

Pointe Grand Palm Coast provides residents with access to top-tier amenities designed to support their physical and mental well-being, including:

  • 24-Hour Fitness Center – Fully equipped with strength and cardio machines, allowing residents to maintain their workout routines anytime.

  • Resort-Style Pool – A relaxing and refreshing space featuring private cabanas for lounging.

  • Starbucks Coffee/Tea Bar – The perfect spot to grab a morning boost or unwind with a warm drink.

Pet-Friendly Features

Pet owners will love the on-site amenities that make caring for furry companions easier, such as:

  • Bark Park with Agility Equipment – A dedicated space for dogs to exercise and socialize.

  • Pet Spa – Offering grooming stations for convenience and care.

Why Choose Pointe Grand Palm Coast?

  • Modern two-bedroom apartments with luxurious interiors.

  • Health and wellness amenities that promote an active lifestyle.

  • A pet-friendly community with spaces designed for furry friends.

  • Convenient access to shopping, dining, and outdoor recreation in Palm Coast, FL.

Pointe Grand Palm Coast is an ideal choice for renters who value a healthy, vibrant, and amenity-rich lifestyle.

Pine Lake Apartments: A Wellness-Focused Community for Active Living

For renters seeking a vibrant, health-focused lifestyle, Pine Lake Apartments in Palm Coast offers an ideal living experience. This community is designed to promote fitness, recreation, and relaxation, making it a top choice for those who value wellness and convenience.

Fitness and Recreation at Your Fingertips

Pine Lake Apartments provides an impressive array of on-site health and wellness amenities, allowing residents to stay active without leaving the community. Key features include:

  • 24-Hour Fitness Center – Equipped with modern cardio machines, free weights, and strength-training equipment to support any workout routine.

  • Resort-Style Pool with Free Wi-Fi – Perfect for swimming, lounging, or working remotely in a relaxing environment.

  • Basketball and Pickleball Courts – Encouraging friendly competition and social engagement.

  • Game Room – A fun space for residents to unwind and connect with neighbors.

Outdoor Adventure and Equipment Rentals

To further promote an active lifestyle, Pine Lake Apartments offers complimentary outdoor equipment rentals, including:

  • Bicycles – Encouraging exploration of nearby trails and scenic areas.

  • Pickleball Equipment – For residents who want to stay active and improve their game.

  • Other Outdoor Gear – Making it easy to enjoy Palm Coast’s beautiful weather and green spaces.

Why Choose Pine Lake Apartments?

  • Health-focused amenities for a balanced and active lifestyle.

  • A pet-friendly community with outdoor spaces for furry companions.

  • Social and recreational facilities that enhance the resident experience.

  • Convenient location near Palm Coast’s best shopping, dining, and outdoor activities.

With its commitment to fitness, recreation, and modern living, Pine Lake Apartments is the perfect place for renters who want to combine comfort with an active lifestyle.


Hammock Beach Golf Resort & Spa: Luxury Fitness and Wellness in Palm Coast

For those seeking a blend of luxury, relaxation, and fitness, Hammock Beach Golf Resort & Spa in Palm Coast offers an exceptional health and wellness experience. This premier resort not only provides world-class accommodations but also ensures guests and members have access to top-tier fitness and wellness amenities.

State-of-the-Art Fitness Center

The Hammock Beach Golf Resort & Spa fitness center is designed for both casual exercisers and fitness enthusiasts. It includes:

  • Peloton Bikes – Offering high-energy virtual cycling classes.

  • Elliptical Machines with Built-in TV Screens – Keeping workouts engaging and interactive.

  • Weight Training Equipment – Featuring free weights, resistance machines, and functional training areas.

Group Fitness Classes & Personalized Training

For those who enjoy a structured workout or prefer expert guidance, Hammock Beach Resort offers a variety of fitness programs, including:

  • Spinning and Aerobics Classes – High-energy sessions for cardiovascular endurance.

  • Yoga and Pilates – Perfect for flexibility, mindfulness, and core strength.

  • Professional Wellness Counselors – Offering customized guidance for a well-rounded fitness journey.

  • Personal Training – One-on-one coaching for those who want an individualized approach to fitness.

A Destination for Health and Relaxation

Beyond the fitness center, Hammock Beach Golf Resort & Spa provides additional wellness-focused amenities, including:

  • A full-service spa for relaxation and recovery.

  • Golf and tennis facilities to promote an active outdoor lifestyle.

  • Resort pools and beach access for aquatic fitness and leisure.

Why Choose Hammock Beach Golf Resort & Spa?

  • State-of-the-art fitness center with top-tier equipment.

  • A variety of group fitness classes led by professionals.

  • Personal training and wellness counseling for customized fitness support.

  • Luxury spa, golf, and beachside amenities for a complete health and relaxation experience.

Whether you're a guest, a member, or a local looking for a premium fitness and wellness destination, Hammock Beach Golf Resort & Spa provides everything needed to maintain an active and balanced lifestyle.

In addition to these facilities, the City of Palm Coast maintains several recreational centers, including the Southern Recreation Center, which offers tennis and pickleball courts, and the Palm Coast Aquatics Center, home to the Frieda Zamba swimming pool. These amenities provide renters with diverse options to engage in physical activities and maintain a healthy lifestyle. ​

Whether you're looking for a fully-equipped gym, fitness classes, or residential communities with health-focused amenities, Palm Coast offers a variety of options to support your wellness journey.
