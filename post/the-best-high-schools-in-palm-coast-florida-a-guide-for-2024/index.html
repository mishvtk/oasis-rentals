The Best High Schools in Palm Coast, Florida: A Guide for 2024

When it comes to high school education in Palm Coast, Florida, families have access to excellent public schools that offer strong academic programs and extracurricular activities. The top best schools in Palm Coast include Matanzas High School and Flagler-Palm Coast High School, each providing unique strengths and opportunities for students.


Matanzas High School


Matanzas High School is renowned for its solid academic performance and supportive learning environment. Serving grades 9-12, the school has a student population of approximately 1,716 students. With a graduation rate of 94%, it exceeds the state average, reflecting the dedication of its faculty and staff to student success. The school offers a variety of clubs and extracurricular activities, creating a vibrant community for students to explore their interests and talents. Academically, Matanzas High School has a reading proficiency rate of 52% and a math proficiency rate of 43%, both of which are respectable figures for the region​ (Public School Review)​​ (Niche)​.


Furthermore, Matanzas High School is undergoing a significant $18 million expansion to accommodate an additional 366 students by the 2024-25 school year. The project, part of a larger $165 million capital plan by the Flagler County school district, includes a new 20,000-square-foot building that will house a modern, collaborative media center and an art lab. The design aims to create a more open and airy feel, moving away from traditional institutional architecture. Other district projects include a new middle school, updates to Belle Terre Elementary, and various infrastructure improvements. The timeline and scope of these projects depend on future student enrollment figures​ (Home - Matanzas High School)​​ (Observer Local News)​​ (Observer Local News)​.



Flagler-Palm Coast High School


Flagler-Palm Coast High School is another leading institution in the area, catering to grades 9-12 with a larger student body of around 2,662 students. Known for its diverse academic programs, the school emphasizes both STEM and humanities, providing a well-rounded education. The graduation rate at Flagler-Palm Coast High School stands at 88%, and the school offers various Advanced Placement (AP) courses, making it a great choice for students aiming for college readiness. The school has a reading proficiency rate of 49% and a math proficiency rate of 37%, showcasing a commitment to academic excellence​ (Public School Review)​​ (Public School Review)​.


Pedro Menendez High School


Just outside of Palm Coast, in St. Johns County, Pedro Menendez High School is a notable option. The school serves approximately 1,482 students and is praised for its inclusive environment and strong extracurricular programs. Students benefit from a variety of sports and clubs, fostering a well-rounded education. Pedro Menendez High has a graduation rate of around 89% and offers a supportive community for students of all backgrounds​ (Niche)​​ (Niche)​.


Conclusion


These high schools in and near Palm Coast, Florida, offer strong academic programs, supportive environments, and a variety of extracurricular opportunities. Whether you're looking for advanced academic courses, athletic programs, or a community-oriented school experience, these schools provide excellent options for students and families in the region, and offer opportunities for a competitive edge in students' college admissions.
