Winter Break Concerts and Shows in Boca Raton: A 2024 Guide

Boca Raton is a fantastic destination during the winter break, offering a mix of warm weather and lively entertainment. Whether you're a music lover, a theater enthusiast, or someone looking to get into the holiday spirit, there are plenty of concerts and shows to enjoy. From performances at outdoor venues to holiday-themed productions, Boca Raton’s cultural scene has something for everyone this winter.

Here’s your guide to the top concerts and shows happening during winter break in 2024 in Boca Raton.


Holiday Concerts at Mizner Park Amphitheater

  • Dates: Throughout December 2024

  • Location: Mizner Park Amphitheater, 590 Plaza Real, Boca Raton, FL

The Mizner Park Amphitheater is one of Boca Raton’s premier venues, and during the winter break, it becomes a hub for holiday festivities. Concerts featuring live orchestras, local musicians, and festive performances take center stage in this open-air setting. Expect classic holiday songs, jazz concerts, and special family-friendly performances, perfect for enjoying a warm winter evening under the stars.


Concerts and Shows in Boca Raton:

  • Holiday Brass Concert: Enjoy a night of holiday classics performed by a brass ensemble, adding a fun twist to your favorite Christmas tunes.

  • Family Holiday Sing-Along: A fun and interactive event for families, where the audience gets to sing along with local choirs and performers.


Boca Ballet Theatre’s “The Nutcracker”

  • Dates: December 20–23, 2024

  • Location: Olympic Heights Performing Arts Theater, 20101 Lyons Rd, Boca Raton, FL

One of the most beloved holiday traditions in Boca Raton is the Boca Ballet Theatre’s production of The Nutcracker. This classic ballet, featuring beautiful choreography, stunning costumes, and the enchanting music of Tchaikovsky, is a must-see for families and dance enthusiasts. The Boca Ballet Theatre brings together talented local dancers, along with guest performers, to create a magical holiday experience.

Tip: Tickets sell out quickly, so it’s best to book in advance!


Festival of the Arts Boca: Winter Edition

  • Dates: December 26–29, 2024

  • Location: Mizner Park Cultural Arts Center, 201 Plaza Real, Boca Raton, FL

The Festival of the Arts Boca is known for its spring festival, but the winter edition brings a special lineup of concerts, including classical music, jazz, and contemporary performances. Set in the intimate Mizner Park Cultural Arts Center, this festival provides an excellent opportunity to enjoy high-caliber performances in a cozy setting.

Event Highlights:

  • Classical Christmas Concert: Featuring holiday favorites by composers like Handel, Tchaikovsky, and Vivaldi.

  • Jazz Under the Stars: A night of smooth jazz featuring local and national artists, perfect for a relaxing evening.


Symphonia Boca Raton: Holiday Pops Concert

  • Date: December 22, 2024

  • Location: Roberts Theater at Saint Andrew’s School, 3900 Jog Rd, Boca Raton, FL

The Symphonia Boca Raton, one of South Florida’s leading chamber orchestras, presents a special Holiday Pops Concert during the winter break. This festive program features a mix of holiday favorites, classical pieces, and popular tunes. Conducted by guest maestros, the Symphonia’s Holiday Pops concert is an elegant and joyful way to celebrate the season with family and friends.

Tip: This concert is perfect for classical music lovers and those looking to add a touch of sophistication to their holiday plans.


Boca Raton Community Church’s Christmas Eve Concert

  • Date: December 24, 2024

  • Location: Boca Raton Community Church, 470 NW 4th Ave, Boca Raton, FL

For a deeply moving and spiritual experience, the Boca Raton Community Church’s Christmas Eve Concert is a beautiful celebration of the season. Featuring a live orchestra, choir, and soloists, this concert offers a peaceful and uplifting way to celebrate Christmas Eve. The program includes a variety of traditional carols, hymns, and contemporary Christian music, perfect for those seeking a meaningful holiday celebration.


The Wick Theatre’s Winter Musical Concert in Boca Raton

  • Dates: December 6, 2024 – January 7, 2025

  • Location: The Wick Theatre & Costume Museum, 7901 N Federal Hwy, Boca Raton, FL

The Wick Theatre is known for its high-quality productions, and during the winter break, it typically showcases a holiday or family-themed musical. In 2024, the Wick Theatre is presenting an exciting winter production (TBA), guaranteed to be full of festive spirit, exceptional performances, and impressive stage design. The Wick Theatre is also home to a renowned costume museum, where visitors can explore a collection of iconic stage costumes.

Tip: Combine your visit to the show with a tour of the Costume Museum for a full afternoon or evening of entertainment.


Carols by Candlelight at Sanborn Square

  • Date: December 18, 2024

  • Location: Sanborn Square, 72 N Federal Hwy, Boca Raton, FL

One of the most enchanting holiday traditions in Boca Raton, Carols by Candlelight, takes place in the beautiful outdoor setting of Sanborn Square. This free community event invites residents and visitors to gather for a night of carol singing, live performances by local choirs, and festive music. The event is illuminated by hundreds of candles, creating a warm and festive atmosphere as everyone sings along to holiday classics.

Tip: Bring a blanket or chair to sit on, and arrive early to secure a good spot near the stage.


FAU Presents: Winter Jazz Concert

  • Date: December 27, 2024

  • Location: Florida Atlantic University, University Theater, 777 Glades Rd, Boca Raton, FL

Florida Atlantic University’s jazz ensembles showcase some of the best talent in the area with their annual show Winter Jazz Concert. This performance highlights a range of jazz styles, from traditional big band sounds to modern improvisational performances. It’s a great opportunity to enjoy live jazz music in an intimate setting, while also supporting local and emerging musicians.


Boca Raton offers a diverse range of concerts and shows during the 2024 winter break, catering to all tastes and ages. From classical music and ballet to jazz and family-friendly performances, there’s no shortage of entertainment to make your holiday season memorable. Whether you’re looking to embrace the holiday spirit with carols and Christmas music or enjoy a night out at the theater, Boca Raton has plenty to offer.


Be sure to plan ahead and book your tickets early, as many of these events are popular and tend to sell out quickly during the holiday season. Enjoy your winter break with some of the best concerts and shows in Boca Raton has to offer!
