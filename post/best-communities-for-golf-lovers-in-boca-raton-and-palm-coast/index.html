Best Communities for Golf Lovers in Boca Raton and Palm Coast

For golf enthusiasts seeking premier communities in Florida, both Boca Raton and Palm Coast offer exceptional options. These areas are home to luxurious residential enclaves centered around world-class golf courses and a wealth of upscale amenities.

Boca Raton Golf Communities

Boca Raton, located in South Florida, is renowned for its upscale lifestyle and boasts several distinguished golf communities:


Boca Woods Country Club: A Premier Golf Community in Boca Raton

Nestled in the heart of Boca Raton, Florida, Boca Woods Country Club is an exclusive, gated community that offers an unparalleled lifestyle for golf enthusiasts and luxury seekers alike. Spanning 600 acres of beautifully manicured landscapes, Boca Woods is a haven for those who appreciate world-class golf, top-tier amenities, and a vibrant social atmosphere.

World-Class Golfing Experience

Boca Woods Country Club is home to two championship golf courses, meticulously designed to challenge players of all skill levels. These courses boast stunning fairways, strategically placed bunkers, and picturesque water features that enhance both the beauty and complexity of the game. Golfers can enjoy state-of-the-art practice facilities, including a driving range, putting greens, and expert-led golf clinics.

Resort-Style Amenities

Beyond its exceptional golf offerings, Boca Woods provides residents with an array of resort-style amenities designed to promote wellness, relaxation, and recreation. The community features:

  • Eight Har-Tru Tennis Courts: Perfect for both casual and competitive players, the well-maintained courts provide an excellent setting for tennis enthusiasts.

  • Pickleball Facilities: With the growing popularity of pickleball, Boca Woods ensures residents have access to top-notch courts for this engaging sport.

  • Modern Fitness Center: A state-of-the-art gym equipped with cutting-edge exercise machines, personal training services, and group fitness classes.

  • Sophisticated Clubhouse: The heart of the community, the clubhouse offers exquisite dining options, a lively bar, and spaces for social gatherings and events.

  • Spa and Wellness Services: Residents can indulge in relaxation with spa treatments, massage therapy, and wellness programs designed for a balanced lifestyle.

Luxurious Residential Living

Boca Woods Country Club comprises 645 elegant homes, ranging from spacious single-family residences to custom-built estates. Many of these homes offer breathtaking views of the lush fairways, pristine lakes, and natural landscapes. Residents enjoy a serene and private atmosphere while benefiting from the convenience of living in a highly sought-after Boca Raton location.

A Thriving Social Community

Boca Woods is more than just a golf community; it is a lively and welcoming neighborhood that fosters strong social connections. The club hosts numerous events throughout the year, including themed parties, holiday celebrations, wine tastings, and live entertainment. Additionally, members can participate in various interest groups, from book clubs to bridge tournaments, ensuring that there is always something exciting happening within the community.

A Prime Location

Conveniently situated in Boca Raton, Boca Woods offers easy access to pristine beaches, high-end shopping, fine dining, and cultural attractions. The community is also close to top-rated schools, medical facilities, and major highways, making it an ideal choice for families, retirees, and professionals alike.

Experience Boca Woods Country Club

Boca Woods Country Club is a premier destination for those seeking an active, luxurious lifestyle in one of South Florida’s most desirable locations. Whether you're an avid golfer, a tennis enthusiast, or simply looking for a welcoming and dynamic community, Boca Woods offers an exceptional living experience.

For more information about Boca Woods Country Club, visit the official website or schedule a private tour to explore everything this remarkable community has to offer.

Broken Sound Country Club: A Luxurious Golf Community in Boca Raton

Situated in the heart of Boca Raton, Florida, Broken Sound Country Club is an exclusive and vibrant residential community offering world-class golf, luxury amenities, and an active social scene. Spanning 1,000 acres, this prestigious community encompasses 27 distinct villages with over 1,600 homes, each offering unique architectural styles and lush landscapes.

Exceptional Golfing Experience

Broken Sound is home to two championship golf courses that cater to players of all skill levels. These meticulously designed courses feature breathtaking fairways, strategically placed hazards, and serene water features. The community also boasts state-of-the-art practice facilities, including a driving range, putting greens, and professional coaching programs to help players refine their skills.

Premier Amenities for an Active Lifestyle

Beyond its unparalleled golfing experience, Broken Sound offers an array of resort-style amenities that enhance the luxurious lifestyle of its residents:

  • 23 Tennis Courts: Whether you're a beginner or a seasoned player, the well-maintained courts provide the perfect setting for casual matches and competitive play.

  • Modern Fitness Center: A cutting-edge fitness facility featuring advanced workout equipment, personal training services, and group exercise classes.

  • Pickleball and Bocce Courts: Catering to the growing popularity of these sports, Broken Sound provides top-tier courts for friendly and competitive play.

  • Full-Service Spa and Wellness Center: Residents can indulge in massages, facials, and various wellness treatments to promote relaxation and self-care.

  • Resort-Style Pools: Multiple pools, including a lap pool and a family-friendly resort pool, offer the perfect retreat for relaxation and fitness.

Diverse Residential Options

The community consists of 27 unique villages, each offering a selection of homes that range from elegant single-family residences to luxurious estate properties. Many homes feature spectacular golf course views, lush landscaping, and modern interiors designed for comfort and style.

A Thriving Social Scene

Broken Sound is not just a golf and tennis community; it is a hub for social engagement. Residents can partake in numerous events and activities, including themed parties, wine tastings, art exhibits, and live entertainment. The community fosters a welcoming atmosphere where neighbors can connect and form lifelong friendships.

Convenient Location

Ideally located in Boca Raton, Broken Sound offers residents easy access to the city's best shopping, dining, and entertainment options. The community is also near top-rated schools, medical facilities, and major highways, making it an attractive choice for families, retirees, and professionals alike.

Experience the Luxury of Broken Sound Country Club

Broken Sound Country Club offers an unparalleled living experience, combining championship golf, first-class amenities, and an engaging social environment. Whether you're an avid golfer, a fitness enthusiast, or someone who enjoys an active and luxurious lifestyle, Broken Sound is the perfect place to call home.

For more information about Broken Sound Country Club, visit the official website or schedule a private tour to explore everything this prestigious community has to offer.

Woodfield Country Club

This luxurious gated community offers an 18-hole championship golf course, 20 tennis courts, and a 97,000-square-foot clubhouse. Housing options range from single-family homes to townhouses and villas, many with scenic lake and golf course views. The community is known for its family-friendly environment and diverse social activities.

The Polo Club of Boca Raton

A private, gated community spanning 1,100 acres, The Polo Club features two championship golf courses, 25 tennis courts, and a 35,000-square-foot spa and fitness center. The community comprises 24 distinct villages with a variety of housing options, from single-family homes to custom-built estates. Residents enjoy a robust social scene with numerous events and activities.


Palm Coast Golf Communities

Located along Florida's northeast coast, Palm Coast offers serene coastal living complemented by top-tier golf communities:

  • Hammock Dunes: This vibrant oceanfront private golf community features two award-winning championship courses—the Creek Course by Tom Fazio and the Links Course by Rees Jones. Residents have access to a Mediterranean-inspired clubhouse, tennis facilities, and seaside croquet. Housing options include custom estate homes, condos, patio homes, and villas, many with oceanfront, lake, or golf course views.

  • Hammock Beach Golf Resort & Spa: Situated along the Atlantic Coast, this resort-style community offers two golf courses, eight lighted Hydro-Grid clay tennis courts, multiple dining venues, a 91,000-square-foot pool complex, marina, spa, and fitness center. Accommodations range from homes and condos to villas with ocean, golf, and lake views.

  • Grand Haven: Nestled around the scenic Intracoastal Waterway and a 4,000-acre nature preserve, Grand Haven boasts a Jack Nicklaus-designed golf course recognized among the "Top 100 Courses in North America." The community offers luxury estates, maintenance-free condos, and patio homes, complemented by amenities such as a clubhouse, fitness center, and various recreational facilities.


These communities exemplify the finest in golf-centric living, combining meticulously designed courses with luxurious residences and a plethora of amenities to enhance the lifestyle of their residents.
