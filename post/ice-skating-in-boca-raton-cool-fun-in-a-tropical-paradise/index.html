Ice Skating in Boca Raton: Cool Fun in a Tropical Paradise

Though Boca Raton is known for its sunny beaches and warm weather, you can still enjoy wintertime fun in the form of ice skating. For families and individuals looking to experience a slice of winter magic, Boca Raton offers some great options for ice skating all year round. Whether you’re a seasoned skater or a beginner, there are places in and around Boca where you can glide across the ice and cool off from the Florida heat.


Boca Ice & Fine Arts Center

A newer addition to Boca Raton’s ice skating scene is the Boca Ice & Fine Arts Center, a state-of-the-art facility offering a variety of ice sports and activities. This multi-purpose ice rink features public skating sessions, figure skating programs, and hockey leagues for all ages and skill levels. Boca Ice also stands out with its focus on fine arts, providing a unique blend of ice sports and creative arts in one location.


Boca Ice offers Learn to Skate programs for beginners and competitive training for advanced skaters. It's a great place for both recreational skating and those looking to hone their skills in figure skating or hockey. In addition to its athletic programs, the center regularly hosts birthday parties, special events, and themed skating nights.


Address: 900 Peninsula Corporate Cir, Boca Raton, FL 33487

Phone: (561) 275-4463


Palm Beach Skate Zone

Located just a short drive from Boca Raton in nearby Lake Worth, the Palm Beach Skate Zone is a popular destination for ice skating enthusiasts. This facility features three rinks: two for ice skating and one for roller skating, making it a versatile option for anyone looking to lace up their skates. The Skate Zone offers public skating sessions daily, so whether you’re an experienced skater or trying it for the first time, you’ll find a welcoming environment to hit the ice. They also offer skating lessons for kids and adults, as well as hockey leagues and figure skating programs for those looking to take their skills to the next level.


Address: 8125 Lake Worth Rd, Lake Worth, FL 33467

Phone: (561) 963-5900


Florida Panthers IceDen

Located in nearby Coral Springs, the Florida Panthers IceDen is the official practice facility for the NHL’s Florida Panthers and one of the top ice skating rinks in South Florida. The IceDen offers public skating sessions, hockey leagues, and figure skating programs, as well as Learn to Skate classes for beginners of all ages. Whether you want to practice your skating skills, take up ice hockey, or try out figure skating, this rink has everything you need to enjoy some cool fun on the ice.


The IceDen also features special events such as holiday-themed skating sessions, making it a fun destination no matter the season. It’s a short trip from Boca Raton and is worth the drive for anyone looking for a quality ice skating experience.


Address: 3299 Sportsplex Dr, Coral Springs, FL 33065

Phone: (954) 341-9956


Public Skating Sessions

If you’re simply looking to enjoy a leisurely skate without any lessons or leagues, Boca Ice, Palm Beach Skate Zone, and the Florida Panthers IceDen offer regular public skating sessions. These sessions typically last a few hours, giving you plenty of time to enjoy the ice. Skate rentals are available at all locations, making it easy for those who don’t have their own equipment to join in the fun. Be sure to check each venue’s schedule in advance, as public skating hours can vary based on other activities and events.


Holiday Ice Skating Rinks

During the winter holiday season, Boca Raton and its surrounding areas sometimes host special temporary ice rinks as part of festive celebrations. These pop-up ice skating rinks are often outdoors and provide a magical holiday experience. While they may only be available for a short time, these rinks are a great way to enjoy ice skating without leaving the local area. Keep an eye out for announcements from the City of Boca Raton during the holiday season for these seasonal ice skating opportunities.


Skating for Fitness and Fun

Ice skating isn’t just a fun activity—it’s also a great way to stay active. Skating offers an excellent cardiovascular workout while improving balance and coordination. Whether you’re taking lessons, playing hockey, or just skating for fun, you’ll be working muscles you don’t normally use, making it a full-body workout. Plus, it's a refreshing way to exercise in Boca’s warm climate!
