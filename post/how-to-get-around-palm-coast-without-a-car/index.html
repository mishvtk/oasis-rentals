How to Get Around Palm Coast Without a Car

Palm Coast, known for its beautiful beaches, lush parks, and laid-back lifestyle, is a charming place to live and visit. While the city is designed with drivers in mind, you don’t need a car to explore its scenic beauty or access key services. Here’s how to navigate Palm Coast without a vehicle, whether you're a local resident or just visiting.


Palm Coast Public Transportation

Palm Coast offers a convenient public transportation system run by Flagler County Public Transit. This service provides door-to-door transportation across the county, making it ideal for residents without a car. While it primarily serves seniors and disabled individuals, all residents can book rides for various needs, such as doctor’s appointments or shopping trips.

For those who prefer more freedom, the Palm Coast Connector operates buses that cover major routes, including shopping centers, local attractions, and government offices. Keep an eye on the schedules and plan ahead to use this cost-effective and eco-friendly option.


Ride-Sharing Services

If public transit doesn’t meet your needs, ride-sharing apps like Uber and Lyft are widely available throughout Palm Coast. These services offer a flexible, convenient way to travel without owning a car. You can easily hail a ride from your phone and reach your destination in minutes.

For frequent riders, both Uber and Lyft offer subscription services with discounts on rides, which can be useful if you're regularly commuting around Palm Coast.


Biking Around the City

Palm Coast is a bike-friendly community with over 125 miles of scenic trails. You can explore the city’s parks, waterways, and neighborhoods on a bicycle, avoiding the need for a car. Popular biking routes include the Lehigh Trail, which winds through natural landscapes, and the Graham Swamp Trail, known for its mix of terrain and wildlife views.

You can either bring your own bike or rent one from local businesses, such as PC Bike, which offers rentals for various timeframes. Additionally, many residential communities and parks have safe, well-maintained biking paths.


Walking: Explore at a Slower Pace

Palm Coast’s temperate climate and abundance of outdoor spaces make walking a pleasant alternative to driving. Many neighborhoods are designed with sidewalks and walking paths, so it’s easy to get around without a car.

For a more relaxed experience, explore Central Park at Town Center or take a stroll along the Intracoastal Waterway at Waterfront Park. These areas are pedestrian-friendly and provide scenic views for walkers.


Car Rentals and Zipcar

For those rare occasions when you do need a vehicle, you can rent a car from services like Enterprise or use Zipcar, which allows short-term rentals at affordable rates. Renting a car is ideal for day trips, moving heavy items, or accessing places that may not be reachable by public transit.


Golf Carts: A Local Favorite

In some communities, golf carts are a popular mode of transportation. If you live in or are visiting one of the local resorts or gated communities, you may be able to use a golf cart to get around. Some areas of Palm Coast even have roads specifically designed to accommodate these smaller vehicles.


Taxis and Shuttle Services

While not as common as ride-sharing apps, traditional taxis are still available in Palm Coast. Local taxi companies offer reliable transportation, especially if you prefer scheduling in advance. For airport transfers or special events, shuttle services are also available to provide comfortable, pre-arranged rides.


Getting around Palm Coast without a car is easier than you might think. With options like public transit, ride-sharing, biking, and walking, you can explore this beautiful city without worrying about driving or parking. Whether you’re a resident or a visitor, Palm Coast’s transportation alternatives ensure you can get everything the city has to offer while staying car-free.
