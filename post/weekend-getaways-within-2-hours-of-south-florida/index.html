Weekend Getaways Within 2 Hours of South Florida


🏝️ Key Largo: A Perfect Escape Just 1–1.5 Hours from Miami and Fort Lauderdale

Why Go Key Largo is the first and northernmost of the Florida Keys, making it an easy, breezy getaway less than two hours from the Miami and Fort Lauderdale metro areas. Known as the “Diving Capital of the World,” Key Largo offers a fantastic mix of outdoor adventure and laid-back relaxation. Whether you’re a seasoned diver, a nature lover, or simply looking to unwind by turquoise water, this slice of the Keys has something for everyone.

What Makes It Special

Snorkeling & Diving at John Pennekamp Coral Reef State Park: Discover America’s First Undersea Wonderland

Nestled off the coast of Key Largo, John Pennekamp Coral Reef State Park holds the distinction of being the first undersea park in the United States. Established in 1963, this protected marine sanctuary spans roughly 70 nautical square miles and offers a window into one of the most vibrant coral reef systems in the Western Hemisphere.

For snorkelers and divers alike, Pennekamp is a bucket-list destination brimming with living coral gardens, schools of tropical fish, and fascinating underwater landmarks.

Why It’s Special John Pennekamp Coral Reef State Park is famous for:

  • Rich Marine Biodiversity: Expect to encounter colorful parrotfish, angelfish, sergeant majors, sea turtles, nurse sharks, and occasional rays gliding past.

  • Stunning Coral Formations: The park protects part of the Florida Reef, the only living coral barrier reef in the continental U.S.

  • Unique Dive Sites: Among the most iconic attractions is the Christ of the Abyss, an 8.5-foot bronze statue submerged in 25 feet of water.

Snorkeling Adventures Beginners and families will find the shallow reefs ideal for snorkeling. Popular excursions depart daily by boat to spots like:

  • Molasses Reef: Known for exceptional visibility and abundant marine life.

  • Grecian Rocks: A shallow reef perfect for first-timers.

  • The Christ of the Abyss site: A memorable blend of art and nature.

For those who prefer to stay closer to shore, there is a designated snorkeling lagoon just steps from the visitor center.

Scuba Diving Experiences Certified divers can explore:

  • Shipwrecks like the Benwood: A World War II-era freighter now teeming with coral and fish.

  • Deeper reef walls and coral canyons: Home to barracuda, moray eels, and lobsters.Dive shops in the park offer guided trips, rental equipment, and refresher courses.

Visitor Tips✅ Reserve tours and rental gear in advance, especially on weekends and holidays.✅ The park has a visitor center with aquariums, an educational theater, and exhibits about coral conservation.✅ Arrive early to secure parking and enjoy the day without crowds.✅ Bring reef-safe sunscreen to help protect the fragile marine environment.

If you’re looking for an unforgettable underwater adventure in Florida, John Pennekamp Coral Reef State Park is a must-see destination where you can immerse yourself in the wonders of America’s only coral barrier reef.

Kayaking & Wildlife in Florida Bay: Drift Through Mangrove Tunnels and Encounter Nature Up Close

For visitors to Key Largo and the Upper Florida Keys, few experiences are as peaceful—or as memorable—as kayaking through the serene mangrove tunnels of Florida Bay. This shallow estuary, part of Everglades National Park and the surrounding marine preserves, is a haven for wildlife and a paddler’s paradise.

With calm, clear waters and intricate passageways lined by tangled mangrove roots, Florida Bay invites you to slow down and discover an extraordinary ecosystem at your own pace.

Why It’s Special Paddling here is not only an outdoor adventure but also an intimate wildlife encounter. As you glide quietly along the winding channels, you’re likely to see:

  • Herons and Egrets: Wading among the roots, searching for minnows.

  • Manatees: Curious “sea cows” often surface near kayaks to breathe.

  • Mangrove Snapper: Flashing beneath your boat in the clear shallows.

  • Ospreys and Pelicans: Soaring overhead or diving for fish.

These sheltered waterways are protected from large waves, making them accessible to beginners and families, yet still rewarding for experienced paddlers.

Top Kayaking Experiences✅ Mangrove Tunnel Tours: Several outfitters in Key Largo and Islamorada offer guided eco-tours through narrow mangrove creeks. Guides share insights into the delicate balance of this unique coastal habitat.

✅ Self-Guided Rentals: Confident paddlers can rent single or tandem kayaks and explore on their own. Popular launch spots include the Florida Bay side of John Pennekamp Coral Reef State Park and the Florida Keys Wild Bird Center.

✅ Sunset Paddles: Evening tours provide spectacular views of the sun melting into Florida Bay—and a chance to see nocturnal creatures stirring.

Visitor Tips✅ Bring polarized sunglasses to better spot fish and marine life below the surface.✅ Apply reef-safe sunscreen and insect repellent.✅ Keep a respectful distance from manatees and other wildlife.✅ Bring a waterproof bag for your phone and essentials.✅ Check tides and wind forecasts before setting out, as conditions can change.

Why You’ll Love It Whether you’re an avid nature lover or simply looking for a quiet escape, kayaking Florida Bay offers a rare chance to disconnect and immerse yourself in the rhythms of coastal life. It’s one of the most rewarding ways to experience the natural beauty of the Keys.

Marine Encounters in Key Largo: Dolphins, Glass-Bottom Boats, and Unforgettable Ocean Adventures

Key Largo isn’t just about beaches and cocktails—it’s also a place where you can experience the wonders of the ocean up close. From meeting playful dolphins to gliding over coral reefs aboard a glass-bottom boat, marine encounters here create memories that last a lifetime.

Whether you’re traveling as a couple, with friends, or as a family, these activities bring you face to face with the vibrant marine life that makes the Florida Keys famous.

Why It’s Special Key Largo’s unique location at the edge of the Florida Reef, the only living coral barrier reef in the continental United States, makes it an exceptional place to explore underwater ecosystems without needing scuba gear.

Here are just a few reasons marine encounters here are so popular:

  • Dolphin Encounters: Opportunities to swim with, feed, or learn about dolphins in ethical and educational settings.

  • Glass-Bottom Boat Tours: An effortless way to see coral reefs and tropical fish without getting wet—perfect for all ages and abilities.

  • Family-Friendly Fun: Accessible activities that combine adventure, learning, and appreciation for marine conservation.

Top Experiences✅ Dolphin Encounters at Dolphins Plus and Dolphins Cove

These marine facilities offer structured programs where you can swim alongside Atlantic bottlenose dolphins. Interactions range from shallow-water experiences to deep-water swims where dolphins pull you gently across the lagoon. All programs emphasize education and conservation.

✅ Glass-Bottom Boat Tours at John Pennekamp Coral Reef State Park Step aboard a spacious catamaran with large viewing panels that reveal coral gardens teeming with parrotfish, rays, sea turtles, and colorful sponges. Tours typically last 2–2.5 hours and include narration by naturalists.

✅ Wildlife Observation Cruises Eco-tours combine reef viewing and wildlife spotting along the mangroves and open water. You may see manatees, sea birds, and even the occasional sea turtle surfacing for air.

Visitor Tips✅ Book dolphin experiences and boat tours ahead of time, especially on weekends and holidays.✅ Bring polarized sunglasses for clearer reef views through the boat’s glass panels.✅ Use reef-safe sunscreen to help protect the delicate marine ecosystems.✅ If you’re prone to seasickness, take precautions before boarding.

Why You’ll Love It Whether you’re marveling at coral gardens through the glass or sharing a moment with a dolphin, these experiences connect you to the ocean in a way that’s inspiring and unforgettable. For anyone visiting Key Largo, a marine encounter is a must.

🌿 Everglades National Park: A Wild Escape Just 90 Minutes from Miami

Why Go Sprawling across 1.5 million acres of wetlands, Everglades National Park is one of the most extraordinary natural areas in the United States. Recognized as both a UNESCO World Heritage Site and a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve, this subtropical wilderness is the largest of its kind in the country—and an unforgettable destination for wildlife enthusiasts, photographers, and anyone who loves the outdoors.

Here, you’ll discover a mesmerizing mosaic of sawgrass prairies, mangrove forests, freshwater sloughs, and cypress domes, all teeming with life. From American alligators sunning along the banks to roseate spoonbills wading in the shallows, the Everglades is a rare place where wildlife thrives in plain view.

✅ Take an Iconic Airboat Tour or a Canoe Paddle in Everglades National Park

Few experiences capture the spirit of Everglades National Park quite like skimming over the “River of Grass” on an airboat or drifting silently through hidden waterways in a canoe. These two styles of exploring the park couldn’t be more different—but both deliver unforgettable encounters with this unique ecosystem and its abundant wildlife.

Airboat Tours: The Classic Everglades Adventure

Why Go Airboat rides are the quintessential Everglades experience. With their roaring engines and shallow draft, airboats can travel over wide expanses of sawgrass marsh that would be impossible to navigate any other way.

What to Expect

  • Exhilarating Speeds: Airboats skim across the water, creating a thrilling ride that feels part safari, part amusement park.

  • Guided Wildlife Viewing: Knowledgeable captains slow down in key areas to point out alligators, turtles, and dozens of wading birds like herons, egrets, and ibises.

  • Accessibility: Airboat tours are a great choice for first-time visitors and families since they cover large distances quickly and require no paddling experience.

Tips:✅ Wear ear protection (most operators provide it).✅ Bring sunglasses—airboats kick up a refreshing spray.

Canoeing and Kayaking: A Quiet Journey Into the Wild

Why Go If you prefer a slower, more immersive way to explore, paddling a canoe or kayak offers the chance to connect with the Everglades’ tranquil beauty.

What to Expect

  • Mangrove Tunnels and Freshwater Sloughs: Unlike airboats, canoes and kayaks can slip into narrow channels and shallow pools where motorized boats can’t go.

  • Peace and Solitude: With no engine noise, you’ll hear the natural soundtrack of the Everglades—birdsong, rustling reeds, and the splash of fish.

  • Closer Wildlife Encounters: Watch for manatees, roseate spoonbills, otters, and alligators just a paddle’s length away.

Tips:✅ Start early in the morning when wildlife is most active and temperatures are cooler.✅ Bring plenty of water, sun protection, and insect repellent.✅ Check conditions—wind and tides can make paddling more challenging.

Why You’ll Love It Whether you crave the excitement of an airboat ride or the peaceful rhythm of paddling, these experiences showcase the Everglades in all its wild beauty. You’ll come away with a deeper appreciation for one of the most remarkable ecosystems in North America.


✅ Visit Shark Valley: The Heart of the Everglades

If you want a front-row seat to the Everglades’ sweeping landscapes and remarkable wildlife, Shark Valley is the place to start. Located off U.S. Highway 41, about 30 miles west of Miami, this iconic section of Everglades National Park offers some of the best opportunities for spotting alligators, wading birds, and panoramic vistas of the “River of Grass.”

Shark Valley gets its name not from actual sharks but from its location within the Shark River Slough, the broad, slow-moving freshwater corridor that defines this part of the Everglades.

What to Do

✅ Bike the 15-Mile Loop Road

  • Experience: Rent a bike at the visitor center or bring your own to pedal the paved trail that winds through open sawgrass prairie.

  • Why You’ll Love It: You can stop whenever you like to watch wildlife. Alligators often bask right next to the trail—making for thrilling (and safe) photo ops.

  • Tip: Plan for 2–3 hours at a relaxed pace, especially if you enjoy frequent stops for photos and birdwatching.

✅ Ride the Shark Valley Tram Tour

  • Experience: A narrated, two-hour open-air tram ride led by naturalists who explain the park’s ecology and point out wildlife along the way.

  • Why You’ll Love It: Perfect if you prefer not to bike or are traveling with kids or grandparents. The guides are knowledgeable and the pace is leisurely.

  • Tip: Tours fill quickly, especially in winter—reserve ahead online or arrive early.

✅ Climb the Observation Tower

  • Experience: About halfway around the loop, you’ll reach a 65-foot tower overlooking the endless wetlands.

  • Why You’ll Love It: From the top, you get a 360-degree view of the Everglades stretching to the horizon—a truly unforgettable sight.

  • Tip: Bring binoculars for spotting distant flocks of birds or gators swimming through the slough.

Why It’s Special Shark Valley is especially famous for its abundance of alligators, which congregate here during the dry season (December–April) as water levels recede. You’ll also see turtles, anhingas, herons, ibises, and sometimes even the rare Everglades snail kite.

Visitor Tips✅ Timing: Mornings are cooler and better for wildlife viewing.✅ Bring: Sun protection, water, snacks, and insect repellent.✅ Safety: Stay on the paved trail and maintain a safe distance from wildlife—especially alligators.✅ Parking: The lot often fills by mid-morning in peak season; arrive early to avoid a wait.

Why You’ll Love It Whether you bike or ride the tram, Shark Valley lets you experience the Everglades up close in a way that’s both accessible and awe-inspiring. From alligator encounters to panoramic vistas, it’s a must-see highlight of any visit to South Florida’s wild heart.

🌆 Fort Lauderdale: A Coastal Escape Just 30–45 Minutes from Miami

Why Go Nicknamed the “Venice of America” thanks to its winding canals and intracoastal waterways, Fort Lauderdale is a dynamic mix of beach-town charm, family-friendly attractions, and upscale leisure. Located just a short drive from Miami, it’s a perfect weekend or day-trip destination that blends culture, coastal relaxation, and adventure.

Whether you’re hopping on a water taxi, exploring museums, or enjoying fresh seafood along the beach, Fort Lauderdale offers something for everyone.


Top Experiences in Fort Lauderdale

✅ Explore the Riverwalk and Museum of Discovery & Science in Fort Lauderdale

Whether you're a first-time visitor or a local looking for a fresh day out, downtown Fort Lauderdale offers two of the city’s most engaging and family-friendly attractions: the Riverwalk and the Museum of Discovery & Science. Situated along the picturesque New River, this area blends scenic beauty with cultural discovery, making it a must-visit destination for couples, families, and solo travelers alike.

Riverwalk Fort Lauderdale: The City’s Waterfront Promenade

What It Is Known as the “crown jewel” of downtown, the Riverwalk is a beautifully landscaped pedestrian path that winds along the New River, connecting a variety of urban parks, public art spaces, museums, cafés, and marinas.

Why Go

  • Stunning Views: Watch yachts glide past or enjoy sunset views over the river.

  • Relaxed Atmosphere: Perfect for walking, biking, or sipping coffee at a waterfront café.

  • Local Culture: You’ll find public art, street performances, and seasonal events along the way.

  • Access to Attractions: The Riverwalk links to other downtown landmarks, including Esplanade Park, the Broward Center for the Performing Arts, and historic Stranahan House.

Museum of Discovery & Science: Fun Meets Learning

What It Offers Located just steps from the Riverwalk, the Museum of Discovery & Science (MODS) is one of the largest science museums in Florida, drawing families and curious visitors year-round.

Top Features:

  • Interactive Exhibits: Explore physics, space travel, weather patterns, and ecology through hands-on displays.

  • Flight Simulator & Aviation Station: Try your hand at piloting or explore real aircraft.

  • Florida Ecoscapes: Encounter live otters, alligators, turtles, and a coral reef ecosystem.

  • IMAX Theater: The museum’s five-story AutoNation IMAX Theater offers nature documentaries and Hollywood blockbusters in jaw-dropping clarity.

Why Families Love It: With multiple floors of activities, MODS is fun for toddlers, teens, and adults alike. It’s an ideal indoor escape on hot or rainy days and a great educational addition to your beach vacation.

Tips for Your Visit✅ Park nearby in one of the city garages and explore on foot.✅ Visit early to avoid crowds at MODS, especially on weekends.✅ Check the calendar for special exhibitions, Riverwalk events, and science workshops.✅ Consider a combo trip—visit the museum in the morning and enjoy lunch or dinner along the Riverwalk.

Why You’ll Love It Whether you're strolling through scenic riverfront parks or discovering the wonders of science in a hands-on museum, this corner of Fort Lauderdale offers a perfect blend of leisure and learning. It’s one of the best ways to experience the city beyond the beach.

✅ Enjoy Beach Town Charm in Lauderdale-By-The-Sea

Overview Just a few miles north of Fort Lauderdale’s bustling main strip, Lauderdale-By-The-Sea offers a laid-back, small-town vibe that feels like a step back to classic Old Florida. With its colorful low-rise buildings, walkable downtown, and uncrowded beaches, this hidden gem is beloved by locals and visitors looking for a more relaxed coastal experience.

Why Go Lauderdale-By-The-Sea is all about slowing down and soaking up simple pleasures: morning coffee with an ocean breeze, casual seafood lunches, and evenings strolling along the pier. Unlike some of Florida’s bigger resort towns, this community has held on to a retro charm that makes you feel instantly at home.

Top Experiences in Lauderdale-By-The-Sea

✅ Stroll the Downtown District

  • What to Expect: Downtown is compact and walkable, centered around Commercial Boulevard, where you’ll find beachside cafés, ice cream shops, family-owned restaurants, and colorful murals that celebrate the town’s maritime heritage.

  • Why You’ll Love It: You can park your car and spend the day exploring on foot, ducking into boutiques or relaxing under the shade of a palm tree.

✅ Visit the Historic Anglin’s Fishing Pier

  • What to Expect: Extending 900 feet over the Atlantic, this classic wooden pier is a perfect spot to fish, people-watch, or catch a sunrise.

  • Why You’ll Love It: It’s open 24 hours a day, and no fishing license is required if you fish from the pier itself.

✅ Snorkel Straight from Shore

  • What to Expect: Lauderdale-By-The-Sea is one of the few places in Florida where you can swim right off the beach to coral reefs teeming with marine life.

  • Where to Go: The reefs lie about 100 yards offshore—just a short swim or snorkel from the sand.

  • Why You’ll Love It: Expect to see colorful parrotfish, angelfish, sea fans, and maybe even a sea turtle or two.

Tips for Your Visit✅ Bring your own snorkel gear or rent from local dive shops on Commercial Boulevard.✅ Check tide and surf conditions before heading out to the reefs.✅ Arrive early for parking, especially on weekends.✅ Visit in the late afternoon to grab dinner and watch the sunset from the pier.

Why You’ll Love It If you’re looking for a slice of Florida that feels refreshingly unhurried, Lauderdale-By-The-Sea is the perfect escape. From easy-access snorkeling to charming streets lined with mom-and-pop shops, this little town invites you to unwind and enjoy the simple pleasures of the beach.

✅ Visit Butterfly World: A Magical Escape into Nature

Tucked away in Coconut Creek, just a short drive north of Fort Lauderdale, Butterfly World is a place where nature feels almost enchanted. As the largest butterfly park in the world, it’s home to thousands of brilliantly colored butterflies, free-flying hummingbirds, and lush tropical gardens.

Since opening in 1988, Butterfly World has welcomed generations of families, photographers, and garden lovers eager to experience this vibrant oasis. Whether you’re traveling with children or simply curious about the natural world, it’s a destination that sparks wonder at any age.

What You’ll See

✅ Thousands of Butterflies

  • Experience: Walk through vast, climate-controlled aviaries where butterflies flutter freely around you. The park houses more than 20,000 live butterflies representing over 50 species from across the globe.

  • Why You’ll Love It: Some butterflies may even land on your shoulder or hand if you stay still long enough.

✅ Hummingbird Aviary

  • Experience: Step into a peaceful sanctuary filled with tiny, jewel-toned hummingbirds darting among flowering plants.

  • Why You’ll Love It: It’s one of the rare places where you can see so many hummingbirds up close in a naturalistic setting.

✅ Tropical Gardens and Insectarium

  • Experience: Stroll through lush gardens brimming with orchids, passionflowers, and cascading waterfalls. The Insectarium showcases exotic insects like giant walking sticks, beetles, and tarantulas behind glass.

  • Why You’ll Love It: The gardens are carefully designed to support butterfly and hummingbird life cycles, blending education and beauty.

Tips for Your Visit✅ Bring a Camera: You’ll want photos of the butterflies perched on flowers—or on you!✅ Wear Bright Colors: Butterflies are attracted to vivid hues.✅ Allow 2–3 Hours: You’ll want time to explore the aviaries, gardens, and educational exhibits.✅ Check the Weather: Most areas are under cover, but some gardens are outdoors.✅ Visit Early: Morning visits tend to be less crowded and butterflies are most active when it’s warm and sunny.

Why You’ll Love It From the moment you step inside, Butterfly World feels like a secret garden brought to life. Whether you’re watching clouds of butterflies dance around blooming flowers or learning about their fascinating life cycles, you’ll leave inspired and uplifted.

🎨 Boca Raton & Delray Beach: Arts, Culture, and Coastal Charm Just 1–1.5 Hours from Miami

Why Go If you’re craving a coastal escape that pairs sophisticated culture with laid-back beach vibes, Boca Raton and Delray Beach deliver the perfect blend. Located just over an hour north of Miami, these two Palm Beach County gems each have their own unique personality—Boca with its upscale events and refined feel, Delray with its vibrant arts scene and energetic downtown.

Whether you’re strolling galleries, relaxing on golden beaches, or sampling fresh seafood along Atlantic Avenue, you’ll find no shortage of reasons to linger.

Boca Raton: Elegant Events and Relaxed Sophistication

✅ Why You’ll Love It Boca Raton is known for its manicured landscapes, Mediterranean Revival architecture, and a year-round lineup of community events that bring locals and visitors together.

✅ Top Experiences:

  • Attend the Greek Festival: A beloved annual event featuring traditional food, live music, and cultural performances.

  • Catch the Boca Bowl: If you’re in town during college football season, the Boca Raton Bowl is a fun way to soak up the local spirit.

  • Relax on Boca’s Beaches: South Beach Park and Red Reef Park offer pristine sand, calm water, and excellent snorkeling.

  • Explore the Cultural Scene: Visit the Boca Raton Museum of Art or take in a show at the Mizner Park Amphitheater.

Delray Beach: Lively Arts and Laid-Back Fun

✅ Why You’ll Love It Delray Beach has earned a reputation as one of Florida’s most vibrant small cities, thanks to its colorful downtown and creative energy.

✅ Top Experiences:

  • Stroll Atlantic Avenue: The heart of Delray, this lively strip is lined with cafés, art galleries, live music venues, and boutiques.

  • Relax on Delray Municipal Beach: Consistently ranked among Florida’s best beaches for its clean sand and crystal-clear water.

  • Visit the Silverball Museum: Play dozens of vintage pinball and arcade machines while learning about their history.

  • Explore the Pineapple Grove Arts District: Home to murals, sculptures, and the Arts Garage performance venue.

Tips for Your Visit✅ Parking: Both towns are walkable, but parking fills up fast on weekends. Plan to arrive early.✅ Festivals: Check local calendars—events like Delray Affair and Boca’s art shows happen throughout the year.✅ Dining: Don’t miss the seafood and farm-to-table restaurants on Atlantic Avenue in Delray or Mizner Park in Boca.

Why You’ll Love It From upscale cultural experiences to beachside relaxation and creative downtowns, Boca Raton and Delray Beach are perfect destinations for a weekend of discovery. Just an hour north of Miami, they feel like a true getaway.

🌻 West Palm Beach & Lake Worth Beach: Art, Wildlife, and Coastal Fun Just 1.5–2 Hours from Miami

Why Go If you’re looking for a getaway that blends culture, shopping, wildlife, and Florida sunshine, West Palm Beach and Lake Worth Beach are the perfect pair. Located about two hours north of Miami, these vibrant communities offer everything from upscale urban experiences to quirky festivals and laid-back coastal charm.

West Palm Beach: Shopping, Manatees, and Family Attractions

✅ Why You’ll Love It West Palm Beach has evolved into one of South Florida’s most dynamic cities, combining trendy new developments with classic Florida attractions.

✅ Top Experiences:

  • Bike or Stroll Along Rosemary Square Formerly CityPlace, Rosemary Square is a lively district filled with boutiques, restaurants, public art, and live music. It’s the perfect place to shop, grab a coffee, and people-watch.

  • Visit Manatee Lagoon Especially popular in winter, this free attraction offers a front-row seat to see Florida manatees gathering in warm waters near the FPL power plant. Educational exhibits and observation decks make it a hit for all ages.

  • Explore the Palm Beach Zoo & Conservation Society Home to more than 550 animals, including jaguars, koalas, and giant tortoises, the zoo is beautifully landscaped and offers plenty of shade and splash areas to keep kids cool.

Lake Worth Beach: Artsy Vibe and Historic Charm

✅ Why You’ll Love It Just south of West Palm, Lake Worth Beach feels like a colorful, creative small town—equal parts retro and bohemian.

✅ Top Experiences:

  • Discover the Artsy Downtown Stroll along Lake and Lucerne Avenues, where you’ll find galleries, vintage shops, sidewalk cafés, and murals splashed across nearly every block.

  • Catch a Show at the Lake Worth Playhouse This historic theater, built in 1924, is the oldest operating art deco movie house in Florida and hosts films, musicals, and live performances year-round.

  • Don’t Miss Annual Art Events Lake Worth’s Street Painting Festival—one of the largest in the U.S.—turns downtown into an open-air museum every February as artists create massive chalk masterpieces on the pavement.

Tips for Your Visit✅ Best Time: Winter and early spring are ideal for manatee viewing and outdoor festivals.✅ Parking: Both cities have public garages and lots, but they fill quickly during events—arrive early.✅ Dining: Rosemary Square has upscale options; Lake Worth Beach is known for funky bistros and locally owned spots.

Why You’ll Love It From manatee encounters and modern shopping to vintage theaters and giant street murals, West Palm Beach and Lake Worth Beach are full of surprises. It’s an easy, rewarding escape that showcases the cultural side of South Florida.
