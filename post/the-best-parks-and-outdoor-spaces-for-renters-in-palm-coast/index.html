James F. Holland Memorial Park: A Premier Outdoor Destination in Palm Coast

This expansive park features an 11,000-square-foot splash pad designed to represent local ecosystems, a dog park with separate areas for small and large dogs, and various sports facilities. It's an ideal spot for families and pet owners.

Located in the heart of Palm Coast, James F. Holland Memorial Park stands out as one of the city’s most beloved outdoor spaces. Spanning 26 acres, this park is designed to accommodate a wide range of recreational activities, making it a top choice for families, pet owners, and outdoor enthusiasts alike.

A Splash of Fun for Kids

One of the park’s main attractions is its 11,000-square-foot splash pad, which is among the largest in the area. This interactive water feature is designed to represent local ecosystems, offering a fun and educational experience for children. It provides a safe and engaging way for kids to cool off, especially during Florida’s hot summer months.

A Haven for Dog Lovers

For pet owners, the Holland Park Dog Park is a standout feature. This designated area includes separate sections for small and large dogs, allowing pets to play safely with others their size. With shaded seating, water stations, and agility equipment, it’s the perfect spot for dogs to socialize and burn off energy while their owners relax.

Sports and Recreation for All

In addition to its family-friendly amenities, James F. Holland Memorial Park caters to sports enthusiasts with a variety of athletic facilities, including:

  • Basketball courts

  • Tennis and pickleball courts

  • Multi-purpose fields for soccer and other sports

  • A well-equipped skate park

These facilities provide a space for both casual play and organized sports, making the park a hub for community recreation.

Scenic Trails and Picnic Areas

For those looking for a more relaxing outdoor experience, the park features walking and biking trails that wind through scenic green spaces. Several pavilions and picnic areas are available for gatherings, birthday parties, and community events.

A Community-Focused Destination

James F. Holland Memorial Park isn’t just a place for leisure—it’s a community gathering spot. Throughout the year, the park hosts various events, including festivals, markets, and outdoor movie nights, fostering a sense of connection among Palm Coast residents.

Whether you’re looking to entertain the kids, exercise with your pet, play sports, or simply unwind in nature, James F. Holland Memorial Park offers something for everyone. With its well-maintained facilities and welcoming atmosphere, it remains a must-visit destination for locals and visitors alike.

Address: 18 Florida Park Drive N, Palm Coast, FL 32137

Hours: Open daily from 7:00 AM to 11:00 PM

More Information: Palm Coast Parks & Recreation


Bird of Paradise Nature Reserve

Nestled along the shoreline of Bird of Paradise Lake in Palm Coast, Florida, the Bird of Paradise Nature Reserve offers a tranquil retreat for nature enthusiasts and those seeking a peaceful outdoor experience. This passive park provides visitors with opportunities for fishing, wildlife observation, and non-motorized boating, making it a cherished spot for locals and visitors alike.

Fishing Opportunities

Anglers will find the reserve an inviting destination, with a healthy population of largemouth bass inhabiting the lake. A bait station and fishing line disposal container are available on-site, ensuring a convenient and environmentally friendly fishing experience. Visitors have noted the serene atmosphere, making it an ideal spot to cast a line and unwind.

Wildlife Viewing

The park's natural setting provides ample opportunities for wildlife observation. While some visitors have reported limited bird sightings, the area is home to various species, offering moments of quiet reflection and connection with nature. The open grassy areas and shoreline create habitats for different forms of wildlife, enhancing the park's appeal to nature lovers.

Boating and Water Activities

For those interested in exploring the lake, the reserve features a boat launch suitable for non-motorized watercraft such as canoes, kayaks, and paddleboards. Paddlers can enjoy the calm waters and scenic views, making it a favored spot for leisurely water activities. The lake's connection to the Intracoastal Waterway allows for extended exploration for the more adventurous.

Amenities and Accessibility

The Bird of Paradise Nature Reserve is designed as a passive park, emphasizing a natural and undisturbed environment. Amenities include a picnic table for outdoor meals or quiet contemplation and a wide stretch of grass suitable for picnics, yoga practices, or casual games. Visitors should note that there are no restroom facilities on-site, so planning accordingly is advisable.

Visitor Information

  • Address: 179 Bird of Paradise Drive, Palm Coast, FL 32137

  • Hours: Open daily from 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM

  • Contact: For more information, call (386) 986-2323 or email customer-service@palmcoastgov.com

Whether you're an angler seeking a quiet fishing spot, a paddler eager to explore calm waters, or someone looking to immerse yourself in nature, the Bird of Paradise Nature Reserve offers a serene and welcoming environment. Its blend of natural beauty and recreational opportunities makes it a must-visit destination in Palm Coast.

Belle Terre Park

A recreational complex equipped with multi-purpose athletic fields, lighted tennis and pickleball courts, handball/racquetball courts, and playgrounds. The park also offers pavilions and a gazebo available for rent, making it suitable for gatherings and sports activities. Belle Terre Park, located at 339 Parkview Drive in Palm Coast, Florida, is a versatile recreational facility catering to a wide range of athletic and social activities. Spanning 10 acres behind Wadsworth Elementary School and adjacent to the Frieda Zamba Swimming Pool, the park offers numerous amenities designed to engage visitors of all ages.

Athletic Facilities

Sports enthusiasts can take advantage of the park's well-maintained athletic facilities, which include:

  • Tennis Courts: Three lighted courts are available for both casual play and competitive matches.

  • Handball/Racquetball Courts: Two lighted three-wall courts cater to handball and racquetball players.

  • Soccer/Football Fields: Two unlighted fields accommodate soccer and football games, suitable for both practice sessions and organized events.

  • Pickleball Courts: The park features five outdoor pickleball courts with permanent lines. Players are encouraged to bring their own nets.

Recreational Amenities

Beyond sports, Belle Terre Park provides several facilities for relaxation and community gatherings:

  • Gazebo: A picturesque gazebo serves as a charming backdrop for small events and photo opportunities.

  • Pavilions: Shaded areas equipped with seating are perfect for picnics and group activities.

  • Playground: A safe and engaging play area is available for children, making the park family-friendly.

Facility Rentals

The park's pavilions and gazebo are available for rent, providing ideal venues for gatherings such as birthday parties, family reunions, and community meetings. Reservations can be made through the City of Palm Coast's Parks & Recreation Department.

Additional Features

Visitors to Belle Terre Park can also enjoy the nearby Frieda Zamba Swimming Pool, which includes a meeting room for events and activities.

Whether you're looking to engage in sports, host a social event, or simply enjoy the outdoors, Belle Terre Park offers a diverse array of facilities to meet your recreational needs.

Waterfront Park

Situated along the Intracoastal Waterway, Waterfront Park provides picturesque walking and biking trails, fishing piers, and picnic areas. It's an excellent location for outdoor enthusiasts and those looking to enjoy scenic water views. Nestled along the scenic Intracoastal Waterway in Palm Coast, Florida, Waterfront Park offers visitors a serene retreat to engage with nature and enjoy a variety of outdoor activities. Spanning 20 acres, this community park is a haven for those seeking picturesque views and recreational opportunities. Walking and Biking Trails

The park features paved trails that wind along the waterway, providing an ideal setting for walking, jogging, or cycling. These paths connect to Palm Coast's extensive 130-mile trail system, allowing for extended exploration of the area's natural beauty. Along the trails, visitors can often spot local wildlife, including manatees and dolphins, making each visit a unique experience.

Fishing Pier

Anglers will appreciate the park's fishing pier, which extends into the Intracoastal Waterway. This spot offers a tranquil environment to cast a line and enjoy the peaceful surroundings. Whether you're a seasoned fisherman or a novice, the pier provides easy access to the water and a chance to catch a variety of fish species.

Picnic Areas and Pavilions

For those looking to relax and dine outdoors, Waterfront Park offers several picnic pavilions equipped with tables and grills. These shaded areas are perfect for family gatherings, casual outings, or simply enjoying a meal amidst nature. The park's layout ensures that picnic spots are conveniently located near other amenities, enhancing the overall visitor experience.

Playground and Children's Memorial Garden

Families with children will find a well-equipped playground featuring swings, slides, and climbing structures, ensuring young visitors have a fun and safe environment to play. Additionally, the park is home to the Children's Memorial Garden, a peaceful area dedicated to reflection and remembrance, located just a short walk north along the Intracoastal Waterway Trail.

Water Access and Rentals

Recent enhancements to Waterfront Park include the addition of a floating dock, providing access for non-motorized watercraft such as kayaks and paddleboards. Visitors can rent equipment on-site through a convenient kiosk operated in partnership with PADL, making it easy to explore the waterway from a new perspective.

Recognition and Community Engagement

In 2024, Waterfront Park was honored as a finalist in the 11th Annual Great Places in Florida People's Choice Award contest, highlighting its significance and appeal within the community. The park continues to serve as a central hub for events and gatherings, fostering a sense of community and connection among residents and visitors alike.

Whether you're seeking an active day of biking and kayaking or a peaceful afternoon picnic by the water, Waterfront Park in Palm Coast offers a diverse array of amenities to suit all interests.


Central Park at Town Center

Located in the heart of Palm Coast, Central Park hosts various community events and features walking paths, a lake, and open green spaces. It's a hub for social gatherings and recreational activities.

Nestled in the heart of Palm Coast, Florida, Central Park at Town Center stands as a vibrant community hub, offering residents and visitors a picturesque setting for recreation, events, and relaxation. Spanning several acres, this open space is encircled by a half-mile brick walking path that meanders around a serene lake adorned with a decorative fountain. The park's well-maintained green lawns and shaded areas provide an inviting atmosphere for picnics, leisurely strolls, and outdoor activities.

Central Park is renowned for hosting a variety of community events throughout the year, making it a focal point for social gatherings in Palm Coast. Signature events such as Food Truck Tuesdays, Arbor Day celebrations, and the Egg'Stravaganza draw crowds eager to partake in the festivities. During the holiday season, the park transforms into a winter wonderland with the annual Tree Lighting Ceremony and the Starlight Holiday Parade, featuring marching bands and festive floats that bring the community together in celebration.

The park is equipped with modern amenities to enhance visitor experience. A state-of-the-art sound system permeates the area, ensuring high-quality audio for events and performances. The concrete amphitheater serves as a stage for concerts, theater productions, and ceremonies, providing a platform for local artists and performers to showcase their talents. For those looking to stay active, the lighted walking trail offers a safe environment for evening walks or jogs. Additionally, ample parking and clean restroom facilities contribute to the park's convenience and accessibility.

Central Park's commitment to fostering a sense of community is evident in its diverse range of free events and activities. From live music performances spanning genres like jazz, rock, and classical, to outdoor movie nights under the stars, there's always something happening to engage and entertain visitors of all ages. These events not only provide entertainment but also serve as opportunities for residents to connect and build lasting relationships.

Whether you're a local resident or a visitor exploring Palm Coast, Central Park at Town Center offers a welcoming environment to unwind, participate in community events, and enjoy the natural beauty of the area. Its blend of recreational facilities, cultural events, and scenic charm makes it a cherished landmark in the heart of the city.

These parks and outdoor spaces enhance the quality of life for Palm Coast residents, offering diverse recreational opportunities and natural beauty. Whether you're a renter or a long-term resident, exploring these areas can provide enjoyable experiences and a deeper connection to the community.
