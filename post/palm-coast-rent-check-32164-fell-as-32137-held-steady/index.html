Palm Coast Rent Check: 32164 Fell as 32137 Held Steady

Palm Coast’s typical rent in October 2026 was $1,978.16 in 32137 and $1,835.86 in 32164, according to Zillow’s ZIP-level Observed Rent Index. The difference was $142.30 per month, or $1,707.60 over twelve months. These ZIP-wide figures are market benchmarks, not quotes for individual homes.

The ZIPs also moved differently in October: 32164 fell 0.45% from September, while 32137 edged down only 0.02%. Renters comparing northern and southern Palm Coast should consider what the lower ZIP-wide benchmark buys once the commute, utilities, lawn care and the home itself enter the calculation.

The gap has widened since last October

In October 2025, Zillow’s series placed 32137 at $1,939.37 and 32164 at $1,825.58, a difference of $113.79 per month. By October 2026, the difference had expanded to $142.30.

This does not put every northern home above every southern home. The index shows that the typical market levels moved farther apart as 32137 recorded stronger annual growth.

What the $142.30 monthly difference can change

Give the gap its own budget line

A difference of $142.30 each month and $1,707.60 across a year can affect a household decision. It could cover part of an electric bill, renter’s insurance, lawn service or the fuel needed for a longer commute. Paying more may still make sense if the home cuts out a daily drive or includes services left to the tenant under the cheaper lease.

Compare the two candidates using an "all-in housing" line that includes more than rent:

  • Advertised monthly rent and any recurring resident fees.
  • Electricity, water, sewer and trash responsibility.
  • Lawn, pest and irrigation maintenance.
  • Renter’s insurance and any pet-related charge.
  • Expected fuel, toll and vehicle costs for regular trips.
  • The value of included appliances, storage, fencing or a garage.

Use a break-even test

If a 32137 home costs about the ZIP-level gap more than a 32164 alternative, list what the extra payment replaces. A shorter trip to work, school, groceries or recurring activities has a household value. Owner-paid lawn service and a newer, more efficient air-conditioning system may have value as well.

Keep the less expensive home in contention if you cannot identify enough value to justify the difference. When the cheaper lease shifts several bills and chores back to the tenant, however, its advertised advantage can shrink quickly.

October momentum favored renters in 32164

The southern ZIP gave back some rent

From September to October 2026, Zillow’s typical rent for 32164 moved from $1,844.22 to $1,835.86. That was a 0.45% monthly decline, modest but more noticeable than the movement in 32137.

Renters searching around Belle Terre Parkway, State Road 100, Town Center, Lehigh Woods or Seminole Woods can use that softness as a reason to compare several available homes. It may also support a polite request for details about renewal terms, included maintenance or move-in timing. The change does not prove that an individual landlord will discount a particular property.

32137 was nearly flat for the month

In 32137, the index changed from $1,978.58 in September to $1,978.16 in October 2026. Zillow reports that as a 0.02% decline, which is better understood as "nearly unchanged" than as a meaningful price drop.

That steadiness applies to the broader market around Palm Harbor, Indian Trails and the Matanzas Woods side of the city. One soft month in southern Palm Coast does not automatically change asking rents in northern neighborhoods.

The longer view is more balanced

Both ZIPs gained about the same amount over three years

The recent paths differ, but the three-year results are almost identical. From October 2023 to October 2026, the Zillow index increased 2.85% in 32137 and 2.84% in 32164.

The ZIPs already had different starting values. Zillow recorded $1,923.30 for 32137 and $1,785.08 for 32164 in October 2023. Similar percentage growth preserved the north-south gap instead of eliminating it.

The latest year created the sharper split

Over the latest twelve-month comparison, 32137 rose 2.00%, while 32164 rose 0.56%. This difference explains more of the current gap than the nearly identical three-year percentages.

Renters should look at more than one time window. The monthly result describes immediate direction. The annual result shows recent market strength, while the three-year result keeps a single season from dominating the comparison.

What north and south mean in daily Palm Coast life

32137 can reduce certain north-side trips

ZIP 32137 covers much of northern Palm Coast, including areas associated with Indian Trails, Matanzas Woods and Palm Harbor. For households with frequent north-side commitments, the location may be worth more than the raw rent difference.

For example, the city describes Indian Trails Sports Complex as a site for soccer, lacrosse, flag football, baseball and softball. A family making repeated trips for practices or tournaments should map both candidate homes at the actual departure time instead of relying on the ZIP label.

32164 can suit Town Center and southern routines

ZIP 32164 reaches across much of southern and central Palm Coast, including Lehigh Woods, Seminole Woods, Quail Hollow and the Town Center side of the city. It may suit households whose routines point toward State Road 100, U.S. 1 or the southern sections.

The city says Lehigh Trail runs east to west for 6.7 miles, passing through Town Center and Graham Swamp Preserve before ending at the trailhead on Colbert Lane. Access to a regular walking, cycling or dog-walking route can matter, though it should be checked from the exact address.

Renters exploring the southern market can review more Palm Coast guides, browse current rental availability, and use a local example such as Palm Coast Paradise Unit A to make a feature-by-feature comparison. Compare the actual leases and homes rather than using one listing as a substitute for the ZIP-wide index.

Compare the lease, home and commute before choosing

Start with the home type

Zillow’s file combines single-family homes and condos in one smoothed series. A detached house with a fenced yard, a duplex unit and a condo with shared amenities are not interchangeable, even when all three are in the same ZIP.

Before comparing prices, match the bedroom count, bathroom count, usable living area, parking arrangement, outdoor space, age and condition. Then note whether the home is detached, attached or part of an association. A lower rent can be reasonable when the property has less space or fewer features.

Ask who handles Florida-specific upkeep

For a Palm Coast house, clarify responsibility for lawn mowing, edging, irrigation, pest control, air-filter replacement and storm preparation before signing. Put each answer beside the monthly rent. Do not count a vague verbal promise as an included service.

Give utilities the same attention. The Palm Coast Utility Department handles drinking water, wastewater and reclaimed water services and directs customers to rates, billing and service information. Ask whose name appears on each account and whether the owner or manager bills any utility directly.

Test the route instead of guessing

Map each home to work, school, medical care, groceries and the activity you visit most often. Check the route during the hours you would actually drive. Belle Terre Parkway, Palm Coast Parkway, State Road 100, Old Kings Road, U.S. 1 and Interstate 95 serve different routines. Two homes with similar rents can therefore produce very different weeks.

How to use Zillow’s index correctly

ZORI measures a market, not a particular lease

Zillow explains that ZORI measures changes in asking rents over time while controlling for changes in the quality of the available rental stock. It uses repeated observations of the same units and weights the sample to better represent the broader rental market.

The index can help answer questions such as "Is typical rent rising?" and "How far apart are these ZIP-level markets?" It is not an appraisal, a guarantee of availability or proof that a particular home is overpriced.

Build a small comparable set

Use the ZIP figure as a starting point, then compare the home with genuinely similar alternatives. Keep the search narrow enough for the differences to be meaningful:

  1. Match the home type and general size.
  2. Keep the condition and renovation level comparable.
  3. Separate furnished, seasonal and annual leases.
  4. Record deposits, recurring fees and included services.
  5. Confirm availability directly before relying on an advertised price.
  6. Compare total monthly cost and commute, not rent alone.

A home may reasonably sit above the index because of its condition, space, location or included services. Another may sit below it because it lacks a garage, has older finishes, transfers lawn duties to the tenant or is priced to lease quickly.

A practical Palm Coast decision rule

Shortlist the best home in each ZIP, then add the rent, recurring fees, estimated utilities, required upkeep and commute costs. If 32137 still costs more, write down the specific benefits you receive for that premium. If those benefits do not matter to your household, 32164’s lower October benchmark deserves closer attention.

FAQ

What was the typical rent in Palm Coast ZIP 32137 in October 2026?

Zillow’s ZIP-level Observed Rent Index placed typical rent in 32137 at $1,978.16 for October 2026. It is a market index, not a quote for every available home.

What was the typical rent in Palm Coast ZIP 32164 in October 2026?

Zillow’s index placed typical rent in 32164 at $1,835.86 for October 2026, down 0.45% from September.

How much cheaper was 32164 than 32137?

The October 2026 difference was $142.30 per month. Multiplying that benchmark gap by twelve gives an annual difference of $1,707.60.

Are Zillow ZIP-level rent figures the expected rent for a specific house?

No. ZORI measures typical asking-rent movement across a market while controlling for changes in the rental stock. A particular home can differ because of its type, size, condition, location, lease term, fees and included services.

What should renters compare besides rent?

Compare recurring fees, electricity, water, lawn and pest responsibilities, parking, renter’s insurance, home condition, commute costs and lease inclusions. Use the all-in monthly cost to compare two homes fairly.
