Boca Raton 33433: A renter's guide to Boca Del Mar

Boca Del Mar and the surrounding 33433 communities may suit renters looking for a central Boca Raton location, nearby shopping, and more housing for the money than central or east Boca. The area works best for households comfortable driving most places. Renters who care more about beach access, downtown nightlife, or a genuinely walkable routine may prefer a more expensive ZIP farther east.

In September 2026, Zillow's typical rent for 33433 was $2,586.42, up 3.93% from September 2025. That was about $420 below 33486 and $700 below downtown and east-Boca ZIP 33432. Those numbers put 33433 in the middle of the local market, rather than at the bargain-basement end.

What the September 2026 rent numbers show

Zillow's Observed Rent Index, or ZORI, estimates the typical market rent across apartments, condominiums and single-family homes. It is a ZIP-level benchmark, so a particular two-bedroom condo or three-bedroom townhouse may rent for a different amount.

Use the index for comparison

A listing above $2,586.42 is not necessarily overpriced. A renovated townhouse with a garage, private outdoor space and community amenities differs from an older condominium with shared parking. A home below the ZIP benchmark may have association fees, dated systems or restrictions that account for the lower rent.

Ask for a complete list of recurring costs before applying. Add up the base rent, association charges, required internet or cable, parking, pet costs and renter's insurance. The total gives you a fairer comparison than advertised rent alone.

Where Boca Del Mar fits within Boca Raton

A suburban setting in central-west Boca

Boca Del Mar generally refers to the residential area west of Military Trail and east of Florida's Turnpike, around Palmetto Park Road, Camino Real and Powerline Road. Much of it lies outside the City of Boca Raton's incorporated boundary, even though properties have a Boca Raton mailing address. Renters should check whether city programs, rates or resident benefits apply to a particular address.

The street pattern is suburban. Major roads run between clusters of condominiums, townhomes, golf-oriented communities, shopping centers and detached homes. Streets inside individual developments are often quieter and more shaded, though a grocery trip may still involve crossing a wide arterial road.

Housing varies from one community to another

The rental pool is a mix of low-rise condominium buildings, attached villas, townhouses and single-family homes. Many are in associations with separate rules for landscaping, parking and recreation.

Two homes with the same bedroom count can feel quite different in daily use. One may have a garage and private patio but no community pool. Another may include a pool, tennis courts and maintained grounds, along with vehicle, guest and move-in restrictions. To see the range before touring, review the broader Boca Raton renter guides, then compare current rental availability with an archived Boca example such as the three-bedroom Helsinki Circle home. Availability and pricing can change, so property pages are better used as layout references than as evidence of neighborhood-wide rents.

Commuting from 33433 is mainly a driving decision

Routes to central Boca, downtown and FAU

Palmetto Park Road is the main east-west route toward central Boca, downtown and Federal Highway. Glades Road, accessible from the northern side of the area, runs directly toward Florida Atlantic University's main campus at 777 Glades Road. Military Trail and Powerline Road carry many north-south trips.

Drivers heading to I-95 usually continue east on Palmetto Park Road or Glades Road. Florida's Turnpike is more convenient for many homes on the western side of 33433, though the best interchange depends on the exact address and direction of travel.

A single map estimate taken at lunchtime will not tell you enough. Run the route from the rental's driveway to your workplace during your usual departure hour, then check the return trip. School traffic, turn lanes and the distance from a community entrance to the main road can matter as much as straight-line mileage.

Transit is available, but check the first and last mile

Palm Tran's official route list shows Route 92 along Palmetto Park Road, connecting West Boca with SE Mizner Boulevard. Route 91 uses Glades Road and serves Florida Atlantic University, while Route 3 follows Military Trail.

Those routes work for some commutes, but a bus line on a nearby arterial does not make every gated development easy to navigate without a car. Walk from the exact unit to the stop, check the sidewalks and lighting, and review the schedule in both directions. A car-free plan depends on the first mile and the return trip as well as the route itself.

Everyday errands and recreation

Plan errands along the main corridors

Palmetto Park Road, Powerline Road and Military Trail are the main corridors for groceries, pharmacies, restaurants, fitness studios and routine services. Renters can usually put together an efficient errand loop without driving downtown or east to Federal Highway.

Here, "nearby" often means a short drive instead of a pleasant walk. During a tour, locate the grocery store, pharmacy, fuel stop and weekday takeout option you would probably use. Check the turns needed to reach them, particularly if the community allows only right turns at a busy exit.

Sugar Sand Park is a helpful nearby reference point

On the eastern side of the broader central-Boca area, Sugar Sand Park has a community center, Children's Science Explorium, Willow Theatre, field house, carousel, walking trail and athletic facilities. It gives 33433 renters a nearby recreation option, although residency may affect eligibility and fees for some reservations or programs.

Check the development's own amenities as well. If a listing advertises a pool or racquet facility, confirm that tenants receive access, ask which passes are required, and find out whether renovations or seasonal closures are planned. See the amenity in person instead of relying on a photo from an old listing.

Beach trips still take planning

The beach can remain part of a Boca Del Mar weekend, but reaching the coast requires a planned drive rather than a walk outside. If regular ocean access matters to you, test the full trip to your preferred beach, including parking, before choosing the lower inland rent.

Many renters face a straightforward choice: pay less each month and drive east for recreation, or pay the 33432 premium to live closer to downtown and the coast. The better choice depends on the household.

School zones require address-level verification

A Boca Raton mailing address is not proof of assignment

ZIP codes, neighborhood names and municipal boundaries do not determine a child's assigned public school on their own. Attendance boundaries can divide nearby developments, and assignments may change between academic years.

The School District of Palm Beach County provides a Find My School tool and official attendance-boundary maps. Enter the complete street address, including the unit when applicable, and select the map for the school year in which the student will enroll.

A careful verification sequence

  1. Obtain the exact rental address from the owner, manager or licensed agent.
  2. Enter that address in the district's Find My School tool.
  3. Save the result and note the academic year displayed.
  4. Contact the district or assigned school if the result is unclear.
  5. Confirm required enrollment and residency documents before paying nonrefundable association or application charges.

A landlord, listing agent or current resident may know which school has historically served a community, but district verification should settle the question. Check the assignment as part of your due diligence rather than relying on marketing copy.

Tradeoffs renters should examine closely

Walkability and traffic

Some developments have pleasant internal sidewalks, lakeside paths or short connections to neighborhood shops. The wider area may still be difficult to navigate on foot. Wide roads, long signal cycles, heat, rain and community walls can make a nearby destination awkward to reach.

Visit during the weekday commute and again after dark. Listen for road noise from Palmetto Park Road, Powerline Road, Military Trail or the Turnpike, and time how long it takes to leave the development. A unit deep inside a community may be much quieter than one beside the perimeter wall.

Association approval and rules

Condominium and homeowners associations may screen tenants separately from the landlord. Before paying, ask for the current tenant application, rules, fee schedule and expected review process in writing. Check vehicle limits, pickup-truck or commercial-vehicle restrictions, guest parking, pet rules, moving hours and minimum lease terms.

Ask what happens if the association denies the application. The lease or application documents should state which payments are refundable. Approval from the owner does not automatically guarantee association approval.

Amenities and maintenance responsibility

Community amenities differ, even in neighboring developments. Verify pool access, fitness-room hours, court reservations, gate credentials and any separate tenant fee. If golf views affect the asking rent, keep in mind that the view may not include club membership or course access.

For the home itself, find out who handles air-conditioning filters, pest service, landscaping, appliance repairs and water leaks. In a condominium, the tenant, owner and association may divide these responsibilities. Save the reporting instructions in your phone before move-in so you know whom to contact when a maintenance problem occurs.

A practical Boca Del Mar tour checklist

Before the appointment

  • Confirm the full monthly cost, deposit structure and association charges.
  • Request the association's tenant application and current rules.
  • Check the address in the school district's official tool if schools matter.
  • Map work, FAU, I-95 and Turnpike routes at your usual travel times.
  • Ask which utilities and amenity passes are included.

At the property

  • Test mobile reception inside the unit and garage.
  • Look for water staining around windows, ceilings and air-conditioning equipment.
  • Open closets and check usable storage as well as advertised square footage.
  • Stand outside with the air conditioner off and listen for arterial-road noise.
  • Inspect the assigned parking space and the route from guest parking.
  • Visit the pool, gate, mail area and waste enclosure in person.

After the tour

Score each candidate on total monthly cost, commute, layout, parking, rules and condition. Weight the categories according to your household's needs so a renovated kitchen or attractive pool does not decide the whole lease.

My decision rule: If the lower 33433 rent covers your added driving costs and still improves your monthly budget, Boca Del Mar deserves consideration. If driving for most errands and recreation will bother you, the savings may not make up for the location.

FAQ

Is Boca Del Mar a good place to rent without a car?

It can work for a limited number of address-specific commutes, particularly near Palm Tran routes on Palmetto Park Road, Glades Road or Military Trail. Most renters should expect a car-oriented routine and walk the exact route from the unit to the nearest stop before signing.

What was the typical rent in Boca Raton ZIP 33433 in September 2026?

Zillow's ZIP-level ZORI was $2,586.42 in September 2026, up 3.93% year over year. It is a market benchmark across rental types, not the advertised price of every available home.

How much cheaper was 33433 than 33486 and 33432?

In September 2026, Zillow's typical rent for 33433 was $420.25 below 33486 and $700.34 below 33432.

Does a 33433 address guarantee a particular public school?

No. Renters should enter the complete address in the School District of Palm Beach County's Find My School tool and verify the applicable academic year before signing.

What association rules should Boca Del Mar renters check?

Request the current tenant application, fees and rules. Check approval timing, pets, vehicle restrictions, guest parking, moving hours, amenity access and minimum lease terms.

What should I test during a Boca Del Mar rental tour?

Test the actual commute, development exit, mobile reception, parking and road noise. Inspect the air-conditioning area and signs of water intrusion, then visit the advertised pool, gate and other amenities in person.
