How Far Is Your Rental from Major Airports in Boca, Deerfield, and Palm Coast?

When planning a stay in Boca Raton, Deerfield Beach, or Palm Coast, Florida, it's essential to know the proximity of major airports to ensure convenient travel arrangements. Here's a breakdown of the nearest major airports to these locations


Boca Raton

Palm Beach International Airport (PBI), located approximately 25 miles north of Boca Raton, serves as a primary aviation hub for the region, offering both domestic and international flights. Situated in West Palm Beach, PBI is conveniently accessible to travelers from Boca Raton and surrounding areas.

Facilities and Services

The airport spans 2,120 acres and features three asphalt runways, with the longest measuring 10,001 feet. The terminal comprises 32 gates across three concourses:

  • Concourse A: Houses four ground-level gates primarily serving regional aircraft.

  • Concourse B: Contains 13 gates serving airlines such as Allegiant Air, American Airlines, Avelo Airlines, Southwest Airlines, Sun Country Airlines, and United Airlines.

  • Concourse C: Includes 15 gates accommodating Air Canada, Breeze Airways, Delta Air Lines, Frontier Airlines, JetBlue, Porter Airlines, and Spirit Airlines.

The terminal offers a variety of amenities, including dining options, retail shops, and services designed to enhance passenger comfort and convenience.


Accessibility

PBI's strategic location near major highways like I-95 and Southern Boulevard ensures easy access for travelers from Boca Raton and other parts of Palm Beach County. The airport is approximately 2.5 miles west of downtown West Palm Beach and 3.5 miles west of Palm Beach, making it a convenient choice for both business and leisure travelers.


Parking and Ground Transportation

The airport provides ample parking facilities, including short-term, long-term, and park-and-ride options, totaling over 9,600 spaces. Ground transportation services such as taxis, ride-sharing, shuttle buses, and rental car agencies are readily available to accommodate diverse traveler needs.

Passenger Traffic

On average, more than six million passengers pass through PBI annually, with commercial airlines, including commuter aircraft, operating approximately 70,000 flights each year. The airport also handles nearly 113,500 general aviation, freight, and other flights annually, with approximately 19,000 tons of cargo passing through PBI each year.

Historical Context

Established in 1936 as Morrison Field, the airport played a significant role during World War II before transitioning to commercial operations. Over the decades, PBI has undergone numerous expansions and renovations to accommodate growing passenger numbers and technological advancements.

For travelers in Boca Raton and the surrounding areas, Palm Beach International Airport offers a convenient and efficient gateway to various domestic and international destinations, supported by comprehensive facilities and services.



Fort Lauderdale–Hollywood International Airport (FLL): A Comprehensive Overview

Fort Lauderdale–Hollywood International Airport (FLL) is a major public airport located in Broward County, Florida, approximately 21 miles south of Boca Raton. It serves as a vital gateway for both domestic and international travelers, offering extensive connectivity across the globe.

Historical Background

Established in 1929 as Merle Fogg Field, the airport underwent significant transformations during World War II, serving as Naval Air Station Fort Lauderdale. Post-war, it transitioned to commercial operations and was renamed Broward County International Airport in 1946. The current name, Fort Lauderdale–Hollywood International Airport, was adopted in 1959, coinciding with the opening of its first permanent terminal building.

Facilities and Infrastructure

FLL spans approximately 1,380 acres and features two primary runways:

  • Runway 10L/28R: Measuring 9,000 feet in length, surfaced with asphalt.

  • Runway 10R/28L: Extending 8,000 feet, surfaced with concrete; this runway was extended in 2014 to accommodate larger aircraft.

The airport comprises four terminals, each equipped with multiple concourses to handle a diverse range of airlines and flights. In December 2022, FLL hosted 100 based aircraft, including single-engine planes, multi-engine planes, jets, and helicopters.

Airlines and Destinations

FLL serves as a hub for several major carriers, offering both domestic and international flights. Notably, Spirit Airlines, which has a significant presence at FLL, filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection three months ago but continues to operate flights as it restructures.

Ground Transportation

The airport is accessible via major roads, including U.S. Route 1, Florida State Road 818, Interstate 95, and Interstate 595. Public transportation options include Tri-Rail commuter trains and Brightline services, both providing shuttle bus connections to the airport terminals. Additionally, ride-sharing services, taxis, and rental car facilities are readily available to accommodate travelers' needs.

Recent Developments

Recent data indicates that FLL offers some of the most affordable airfares in the United States, with an average fare of $252, attributed to the presence of budget airlines and competitive pricing.

However, the airport has also experienced operational challenges, with a reported delay rate of 31.9%, highlighting the importance for travelers to plan accordingly.

Future Plans

FLL has several projects underway as part of its master plan, including constructing airside terminal connections between Terminals 1, 2, and 3; developing an intermodal center; building Terminal 5; and implementing an automated people mover system. These initiatives aim to enhance operational efficiency and passenger experience, with anticipated completion by the end of the decade.

For travelers from Boca Raton and the surrounding regions, Fort Lauderdale–Hollywood International Airport remains a pivotal hub, offering extensive domestic and international connections alongside a range of amenities and services.

Miami International Airport (MIA):

Miami International Airport (MIA) is a major public airport located in Miami-Dade County, Florida, approximately 50 miles south of Boca Raton.

Renowned as one of the busiest international airports in the United States, MIA offers extensive flights worldwide, serving as a vital gateway between the U.S. and international destinations.

Historical Background

Established in 1928 as Pan American Field, MIA has evolved into a significant aviation hub over the decades. Its strategic location has facilitated Miami's development as a central point for international travel and commerce.

Facilities and Infrastructure

MIA boasts a comprehensive array of facilities to accommodate the needs of modern air travel:

  • Runways: The airport features multiple runways designed to handle a wide variety of aircraft, ensuring efficient operations for both domestic and international flights.

  • Terminals: MIA's terminals are equipped with state-of-the-art amenities, including numerous dining and shopping options, lounges, and business services, enhancing the passenger experience.

  • MIA e Train: To facilitate seamless movement within the airport, the MIA e Train connects the satellite building of Concourse E (gates E20-E33) with the main terminal area (gates E2-E11). This cable-driven people mover system, operational since 2016, was developed by Leitner-Poma at a cost of $87 million.

Accessibility from Boca Raton

Travelers from Boca Raton can reach MIA by car via the I-95 S route, covering a driving distance of approximately 50 miles, which typically takes around 58 minutes under normal traffic conditions.

Recent Performance Metrics

While MIA continues to be a pivotal international hub, it has faced challenges concerning operational efficiency. A recent survey by Upgraded Points identified MIA as the U.S. airport where travelers experience the most time wastage, averaging 47.1 minutes lost. Factors contributing to this include departure delays and extended taxi-out times.

Miami International Airport remains a cornerstone of international travel in the United States, offering a wide array of flights and services to destinations around the globe. Its proximity to Boca Raton makes it a convenient option for residents and visitors seeking extensive domestic and international connections.


Deerfield Beach

Deerfield Beach, Florida, is strategically located near several major airports, offering residents and visitors a variety of options for domestic and international travel. Below is an overview of the primary airports accessible from Deerfield Beach:

Fort Lauderdale–Hollywood International Airport (FLL)

Situated approximately 17 miles south of Deerfield Beach, Fort Lauderdale–Hollywood International Airport is a major public airport in Broward County. It serves as a hub for both domestic and international flights, with over 700 daily flights to 135 destinations worldwide. The airport is conveniently accessible via Interstate 95, making it a practical choice for travelers from Deerfield Beach.


Palm Beach International Airport (PBI)

Located roughly 25 miles north of Deerfield Beach, Palm Beach International Airport offers a variety of flight options for travelers. Serving both domestic and limited international destinations, PBI provides a more relaxed travel experience compared to busier airports. The airport's proximity to Deerfield Beach, combined with its manageable size, makes it an attractive alternative for those seeking convenience and shorter wait times.

Miami International Airport (MIA)

Approximately 38 miles south of Deerfield Beach, Miami International Airport is one of the busiest international airports in the United States. Serving as a major hub for international travel, MIA offers flights to numerous destinations worldwide, including extensive connections to Latin America and Europe. While it requires a longer commute from Deerfield Beach, MIA's extensive flight network provides travelers with a wide array of options.

Deerfield Beach's location offers convenient access to multiple major airports, each providing unique advantages. Whether prioritizing proximity, flight availability, or international connections, travelers can select the airport that best suits their needs.

Daytona Beach International Airport (DAB): A Convenient Gateway for Palm Coast Residents

Located approximately 35 miles south of Palm Coast, Florida, Daytona Beach International Airport (DAB) offers residents and visitors a convenient option for regional flights with connections to major hubs across the United States.


Airlines and Destinations

DAB is served by several major airlines, providing multiple daily flights to key destinations:

  • American Airlines: Offers non-stop flights to Charlotte Douglas International Airport (CLT), facilitating connections to numerous domestic and international destinations.

  • Delta Air Lines: Provides daily non-stop flights to Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport (ATL), one of the busiest airports globally, offering extensive onward connections.

  • Avelo Airlines: Since June 2023, Avelo has been operating twice-weekly flights to New Haven, Connecticut (HVN), and has expanded services to include Hartford, Connecticut (KHFD), Wilmington, Delaware (ILG), and starting April 3, 2025, Concord, North Carolina (USA).

  • Breeze Airways: Initiated services in November 2024 with twice-weekly flights to Westchester County, New York (HPN). In February 2025, Breeze added flights to Providence, Rhode Island (PVD); Hartford, Connecticut (BDL); and Raleigh-Durham, North Carolina (RDU).

These airlines collectively ensure that travelers from Palm Coast have access to a wide range of destinations with convenient connections.

Airport Facilities and Services

DAB is designed to provide a seamless and efficient travel experience:

  • Modern Amenities: The airport features comfortable seating areas, easy access to rental cars, and short security lines, with average wait times of less than five minutes.

  • Customer Service Initiatives: Programs like complimentary postcards, a TSA mail-back service for prohibited items, and a partnership with the Volusia County Library for free digital downloads enhance the passenger experience.

  • Accessibility: As a participant in the Hidden Disabilities Sunflower program, DAB offers additional assistance to passengers with non-visible disabilities, ensuring a supportive travel environment for all.

These features contribute to DAB's reputation for exceptional customer service and passenger satisfaction.

Economic Impact and Community Engagement

DAB plays a significant role in the local economy and community:

  • Economic Contribution: According to a 2022 study by the Florida Department of Transportation, the airport provides an annual economic impact of $3.2 billion to the region.

  • Educational Partnerships: The airport maintains a strong relationship with Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University (ERAU), supporting pilot training programs that are crucial for the aviation industry's future.

  • Event Support: DAB collaborates with Daytona International Speedway to manage increased air traffic during major events, accommodating both commercial and private aircraft.

These initiatives underscore DAB's commitment to fostering economic growth and community development.

Awards and Recognition

DAB's dedication to excellence has been acknowledged through various accolades:

  • Commercial Service Airport of the Year: The Florida Department of Transportation named DAB the Commercial Service Airport of the Year in both 2021 and 2024, highlighting the airport's exceptional aviation safety, aesthetic improvements, and innovative management solutions.

These awards reflect the airport's ongoing efforts to provide top-tier services to its passengers and partners.

For residents of Palm Coast, Daytona Beach International Airport offers a convenient and efficient travel option, with a variety of airlines providing connections to major hubs and destinations. The airport's commitment to customer service, modern facilities, and community involvement make it a valuable asset to the region.
