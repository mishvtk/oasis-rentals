Waterfront Restaurants You Can’t Miss in Deerfield Beach

Deerfield Beach is a charming coastal city with pristine shores, breathtaking ocean views, and a laid-back atmosphere that beckons food lovers. Whether you're staying in one of our Oasis Rentals properties or just visiting the area, there are plenty of incredible waterfront dining options that combine delicious meals with unforgettable views. Here are some must-visit waterfront restaurants in Deerfield Beach that you won't want to miss.


1. The Whale’s Rib

For a true local favorite, The Whale’s Rib is a Deerfield Beach staple. This iconic seafood spot is just a stone’s throw from the beach and has been serving up fresh seafood in a casual setting for decades. Known for its famous Whale Fries and lobster rolls, it’s the perfect place to grab a bite after a day at the beach. Their seafood platters and oysters are also highly recommended if you're in the mood for fresh, flavorful coastal cuisine.

Address: 2031 NE 2nd St, Deerfield Beach, FL 33441


2. JB’s on the Beach

With its prime location right on the sand, JB’s on the Beach is perfect for dining with your toes in the sand. Known for its relaxed yet upscale atmosphere, this beachfront restaurant offers stunning ocean views and live music on most nights. Their menu features an array of mouthwatering dishes, from seared ahi tuna to the famous seafood tower. Whether you're here for a weekend brunch or a romantic sunset dinner, JB’s on the Beach is an absolute must for those who want to enjoy the best of coastal dining.

Address: 300 NE 21st Ave, Deerfield Beach, FL 33441


3. Ocean’s 234

For an upscale dining experience right on the water, look no further than Ocean’s 234. With its sleek décor and stunning oceanfront terrace, it’s the perfect place to enjoy a luxurious meal by the beach. Their menu is focused on fresh, locally sourced ingredients, with standout dishes such as pan-seared scallops, lobster mac and cheese, and yellowtail snapper. Whether you're celebrating a special occasion or just want to enjoy a top-notch meal with incredible views, Ocean’s 234 is a Deerfield Beach favorite for fine dining enthusiasts.

Address: 234 N Ocean Dr, Deerfield Beach, FL 33441


4. Two Georges at The Cove

Two Georges at The Cove offers a laid-back dining experience along the Intracoastal Waterway, making it a go-to spot for those looking to relax and enjoy the views. The outdoor seating area is perfect for watching boats sail by while enjoying tropical drinks and fresh seafood. The menu is packed with favorites like blackened mahi sandwiches, crab cakes, and shrimp tacos. Two Georges also offers a popular happy hour, which pairs perfectly with the vibrant waterfront atmosphere.

Address: 1754 SE 3rd Ct, Deerfield Beach, FL 33441


5. The Cove Waterfront Restaurant & Tiki Bar

If you’re looking for a true tropical experience, The Cove Waterfront Restaurant & Tiki Bar is the place to be. Situated on the Intracoastal Waterway, this restaurant offers a lively atmosphere and is a local favorite for drinks and casual dining. With its tiki bar setup and open-air seating, it’s the perfect spot for a sunny lunch or a sunset dinner. The menu features a range of seafood options, along with burgers and refreshing tropical cocktails like their signature rum punch.

Address: 1755 SE 3rd Ct, Deerfield Beach, FL 33441
