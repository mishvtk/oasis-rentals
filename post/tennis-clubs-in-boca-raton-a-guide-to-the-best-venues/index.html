Tennis Clubs in Boca Raton: A Guide to the Best Venues

Boca Raton, Florida, is renowned for its vibrant tennis community, offering a variety of clubs and facilities that cater to players of all skill levels. Whether you're a seasoned athlete or a beginner, the city's tennis venues provide exceptional opportunities for play, instruction, and social engagement. Here's a curated list of some of the premier tennis clubs in Boca Raton:


1. The Boca Raton

Formerly known as the Boca Raton Resort & Club, The Boca Raton boasts a full-service Racquet Club featuring 12 Hydro-Grid tennis courts and four pickleball courts. The facility offers a comprehensive tennis concierge service, expert instruction, and a variety of programs for both adults and juniors. A significant transformation is underway to enhance the Racquet Club, including the addition of more courts and a courtside bar.



2. Broken Sound Club

Recognized as a leading tennis community in South Florida and honored as a USTA Facility of the Year in 2014, Broken Sound Club provides 23 Har-Tru tennis courts. The club offers private and group instruction, a renowned Junior Tennis Academy, and active league play across various categories. Members can also enjoy annual Pro-Am tournaments and exhibitions featuring world-renowned tennis champions.


3. The Swim & Racquet Center

Managed by the City of Boca Raton, The Swim & Racquet Center features 26 Har-Tru courts and hosts an average of 300 patrons daily. The center offers a range of programs, including adult clinics for beginners and junior clinics categorized by age and skill level. Professional instructors are available for personalized coaching, and various membership options cater to residents and non-residents alike.


4. Patch Reef Tennis Center

Nestled within the 55-acre Patch Reef Park, this center offers 17 well-maintained courts and a full-service pro shop. The facility provides programs for all ages, including pickleball clinics, junior tennis programs, and adult clinics for different skill levels. Memberships are available for both residents and non-residents, with options for singles, families, and juniors.



5. Woodfield Country Club

Woodfield's tennis program is celebrated for its robust year-round offerings, including clinics, teams, and leagues for all ages and skill levels. The club's 20-court complex features 18 Hydro-Grid courts, one Italian Red Clay court, and one Decoturf Hard court, all illuminated for nighttime play. A 9,200-square-foot Tennis Pro Shop complements the facilities, providing top brands in tennis apparel and equipment.



6. The Oaks at Boca Raton

The Oaks Tennis Facility is an award-winning complex comprising 12 courts, including 10 Har-Tru, one Red Clay, and one Deco-Turf Hard court. The facility offers a full-service pro shop and a staff of USPTA-certified professionals providing private lessons, group lessons, and open clinics for all levels. The Oaks also hosts numerous special events, including junior tournaments and adult mixers.


7. The Polo Club of Boca Raton

With 22 immaculately groomed Har-Tru tennis courts and eight pickleball courts, The Polo Club has hosted internationally recognized competitions and celebrity charity events. The club offers private and group lessons, clinics, team tournaments, and various social events, catering to players of all levels.


8. Boca Bridges Racquet Club

This facility features six clay tennis courts, one asphalt tennis court, and four lighted pickleball courts with shaded courtside pavilions. The club offers lessons, exhibitions, round-robins, and social events for tennis and pickleball enthusiasts of all ages.

9. Boca Lago Country Club

Home to one of the largest tennis facilities in Palm Beach County, Boca Lago Country Club offers 16 Har-Tru and four hard courts. The club is associated with the One Tennis Academy, providing personalized attention from world-class coaches for both young and adult players. Private and group instruction, as well as clinics, are available.



10. Evert Tennis Academy

Founded by professional tennis player Chris Evert and her brother John Evert, the Evert Tennis Academy is a training center for developing collegiate and professional tennis players. The academy offers part-time training options, as well as academic and housing options for full-time students, with affiliations to nearby educational institutions.

Boca Raton's diverse array of tennis clubs ensures that enthusiasts can find the perfect venue to enjoy the sport, improve their skills, and engage with a vibrant community of players.
