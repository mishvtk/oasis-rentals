Airport access guide: best rental areas for people flying through FLL, PBI, or MCO

Choosing the right rental location in Florida often comes down to one key factor: airport access. Whether you’re flying frequently, hosting visitors, or planning weekend trips, living near a well-connected airport can save hours of travel time and reduce stress. Florida’s three major airports—Fort Lauderdale–Hollywood International Airport (FLL), Palm Beach International Airport (PBI), and Orlando International Airport (MCO)—each serve different regions and lifestyles.

This guide breaks down the best rental areas near each airport and who they’re ideal for.



Fort Lauderdale (FLL): Best for Beaches, Cruises, and South Florida Access

Fort Lauderdale–Hollywood International Airport (FLL) is one of South Florida’s most convenient and well-connected airports, making it a top choice for travelers who want quick access to beaches, cruise ports, and major metro areas. Located just a few miles from downtown Fort Lauderdale, the airport offers easy connections via major highways like Interstate 95 (I-95) and Interstate 595 (I-595), allowing renters to move efficiently throughout the region.

FLL is especially popular among domestic travelers, cruise passengers departing from Port Everglades, and those planning to split time between Fort Lauderdale and nearby Miami. Its strategic location makes it ideal for both short-term stays and longer seasonal rentals.

Best Rental Areas Near FLL

Fort Lauderdale (Downtown & Las Olas) The heart of Fort Lauderdale offers a vibrant, walkable lifestyle centered around Las Olas Boulevard. Just 10–15 minutes from the airport, this area is known for its mix of upscale apartments, restaurants, nightlife, and proximity to the beach. It’s especially attractive for professionals, digital nomads, and short-term renters who want convenience and an active social scene.

Hollywood, FL Hollywood sits just 5–10 minutes south of FLL and is often more affordable than central Fort Lauderdale. It provides easy access to Hollywood Beach Broadwalk, a lively beachfront area with shops, cafes, and scenic ocean views. This makes it a strong option for renters seeking value without sacrificing coastal access.

Dania Beach Located immediately adjacent to the airport, Dania Beach is one of the closest residential options—often less than 5 minutes from FLL. It offers a quieter, more laid-back environment compared to its neighbors, making it ideal for frequent flyers or travelers who prioritize convenience and minimal commute times.

Plantation / Davie Further inland, Plantation and Davie provide a suburban alternative about 15–25 minutes from the airport. These areas typically feature larger homes, more green space, and lower rental prices compared to coastal zones. They are well-suited for families, long-term renters, or anyone seeking a quieter residential setting with easy highway access.

Who Should Choose FLL

FLL is a strong fit for:

  • Frequent domestic travelers who want a less congested alternative to Miami airports

  • Cruise passengers departing from nearby Port Everglades

  • Renters who want a balance of beach access and city amenities

  • Seasonal visitors looking for flexibility between Fort Lauderdale and Miami

Why FLL Stands Out

Compared to other South Florida airports, FLL offers a unique combination of proximity, accessibility, and lifestyle appeal. You can land and be at the beach, your rental, or a cruise terminal in minutes. Whether you prefer an urban waterfront lifestyle, a quiet residential neighborhood, or a budget-friendly coastal stay, the areas surrounding FLL provide options for a wide range of renters.



West Palm Beach (PBI): Best for Convenience and Low-Stress Travel

Palm Beach International Airport (PBI) is widely known for offering one of the most convenient and stress-free travel experiences in South Florida. Smaller and easier to navigate than larger regional airports, PBI allows travelers to move quickly from curb to gate, often with shorter security lines and less congestion.

Located just west of downtown West Palm Beach, the airport provides direct access to major roadways like Interstate 95 (I-95), making it simple to reach surrounding communities throughout Palm Beach County. This accessibility, combined with its calmer atmosphere, makes PBI especially appealing for families, retirees, and anyone prioritizing efficiency over the hustle of larger airports.

Best Rental Areas Near PBI

West Palm Beach Staying in West Palm Beach puts you just 5–10 minutes from the airport, offering a blend of urban and suburban living. Renters can enjoy proximity to waterfront areas, shopping districts, and dining options, particularly around Clematis Street and CityPlace. This area is ideal for those who want convenience without sacrificing lifestyle amenities.

Palm Beach Gardens About 20–25 minutes north, Palm Beach Gardens is known for its upscale communities, golf courses, and well-maintained neighborhoods. It attracts retirees and professionals seeking a quieter, more refined environment while still being within easy reach of PBI.

Wellington Located 20–25 minutes west, Wellington is a family-friendly community प्रसिद्ध for its equestrian culture and spacious residential developments. With newer homes, parks, and strong community planning, it’s a popular choice for long-term renters and families looking for comfort and space.

Boynton Beach / Lake Worth To the south, Boynton Beach and Lake Worth Beach offer more affordable coastal living options within 15–25 minutes of the airport. These areas provide a balanced lifestyle—access to beaches, local dining, and entertainment—while typically maintaining lower rental costs than northern Palm Beach County.

Who Should Choose PBI

PBI is an excellent option for:

  • Travelers who value speed, simplicity, and a low-stress airport experience

  • Families or retirees seeking quieter, more residential neighborhoods

  • Renters planning to stay primarily within Palm Beach County

  • Seasonal residents who prefer easy arrivals and departures without heavy traffic

Why PBI Stands Out

Unlike larger airports in South Florida, PBI emphasizes efficiency and ease. Travelers often experience shorter wait times, easier parking, and faster baggage handling. Its central location within Palm Beach County also means less time commuting to your rental and more time enjoying your stay—whether that’s relaxing by the water, golfing, or exploring local communities.

For renters who prioritize convenience, спокойствие, and accessibility, PBI offers a practical and comfortable gateway to South Florida living.



Orlando (MCO): Best for Theme Parks, Tourism, and Central Florida

Orlando International Airport (MCO) is the busiest airport in Florida and one of the largest in the United States, serving tens of millions of passengers each year. With extensive domestic and international routes, MCO functions as the primary gateway to Central Florida and its world-famous attractions—including Walt Disney World, Universal Orlando Resort, and SeaWorld Orlando.

Strategically located southeast of downtown Orlando, the airport offers strong connectivity via major highways, making it easy for travelers and renters to access a wide range of neighborhoods across Central Florida. Its size and flight availability make it especially attractive for tourists, remote workers, and seasonal residents who value flexibility.

Best Rental Areas Near MCO

Lake Nona Just 10 minutes from the airport, Lake Nona is a fast-growing, master-planned community known for its modern design and focus on innovation. It’s home to Lake Nona Medical City, making it especially popular with healthcare professionals and remote workers. With new apartments, walkable spaces, and tech-forward amenities, it offers a contemporary lifestyle close to MCO.

Downtown Orlando Located about 15–20 minutes away, downtown Orlando provides an urban living experience with high-rise apartments, nightlife, and business hubs. Areas around Lake Eola and the central business district attract young professionals who want proximity to offices, entertainment, and cultural venues.

Kissimmee Approximately 20–30 minutes from MCO, Kissimmee is a popular choice for affordable housing and proximity to theme parks. Its location near Walt Disney World makes it ideal for vacation rentals, families, and seasonal visitors who want easy access to attractions without paying premium Orlando prices.

Winter Park About 25–30 minutes north of the airport, Winter Park offers a more upscale and tranquil environment. Known for its tree-lined streets, boutique shopping, and scenic parks, Winter Park appeals to renters looking for charm, culture, and a quieter residential feel while still being connected to the city.

Who Should Choose MCO

MCO is a great fit for:

  • Tourists and seasonal renters visiting Central Florida attractions

  • Remote workers who need frequent and flexible flight options

  • Families planning extended stays near theme parks

  • International travelers seeking direct access to Orlando

Why MCO Stands Out

MCO’s scale and connectivity set it apart from other Florida airports. With hundreds of daily flights and global connections, it offers unmatched travel flexibility. At the same time, its proximity to diverse rental areas—from modern planned communities to tourist-friendly suburbs—makes it easy to find housing that fits different lifestyles and budgets.

Whether you’re visiting for theme parks, working remotely, or planning a seasonal stay, MCO provides a central and highly accessible base for exploring everything Central Florida has to offer.

Choosing the Right Florida Rental Starts with the Right Airport

Choosing where to rent in Florida isn’t just about the home itself—it’s about how easily you can move in and out of the region. Your nearest airport can shape your daily convenience, travel flexibility, and even your overall lifestyle during your stay.

Florida’s three major rental hubs—Fort Lauderdale–Hollywood International Airport (FLL), Palm Beach International Airport (PBI), and Orlando International Airport (MCO)—each offer a different experience, and the right choice depends on how you live and travel.

FLL: Energy, Beaches, and Connectivity

If you’re drawn to vibrant coastal living with easy access to both city life and the ocean, FLL stands out. Located near Fort Lauderdale and within reach of Miami, it offers a balance of beach culture, nightlife, and strong domestic flight options.

This makes FLL ideal for renters who:

  • Travel frequently within the U.S.

  • Want to be close to beaches and entertainment

  • Value access to cruise ports like Port Everglades

PBI: Simplicity, Comfort, and Low-Stress Living

For those who prioritize ease and efficiency, PBI offers a calmer alternative. Serving West Palm Beach and surrounding communities, it’s known for shorter lines, lighter crowds, and a more relaxed pace.

PBI is a great fit for renters who:

  • Prefer a quieter, less congested travel experience

  • Live primarily within Palm Beach County

  • Value convenience over a wide range of flight options

It’s especially appealing to families, retirees, and seasonal residents who want a smoother arrival and departure process.

MCO: Flexibility, Tourism, and Central Access

As Florida’s busiest airport, MCO provides unmatched flight availability and global connectivity. Located in Orlando, it’s the gateway to Central Florida’s tourism economy, including destinations like Walt Disney World.

MCO is best suited for renters who:

  • Need maximum flight flexibility, including international travel

  • Are visiting or working near theme parks and attractions

  • Plan extended or seasonal stays in Central Florida

What Matters Most When Choosing

The best rental location ultimately depends on your personal priorities. Consider:

  • How often you fly: Frequent travelers may benefit from larger airports like MCO or well-connected hubs like FLL.

  • Your daily routine: Proximity to work, schools, or attractions can outweigh airport access.

  • Lifestyle preferences: Beachfront energy, suburban quiet, or tourist-centered living all offer different experiences.

  • Budget considerations: Rental prices vary widely depending on proximity to coastlines, city centers, and attractions.

Florida offers a diverse range of living environments, and your choice of airport plays a bigger role than many renters expect. Whether you prioritize convenience, cost, or lifestyle, aligning your rental location with your travel habits can make your stay significantly smoother and more enjoyable.
