Seminole Woods, Palm Coast: A renter's guide

Seminole Woods suits renters who want a residential setting in Palm Coast, plan to drive for most errands, and value practical access to a neighborhood park, Town Center, and the State Road 100 corridor. Renters looking for a walkable restaurant district, frequent public transportation, or a home directly beside the beach may be happier elsewhere.

Whether the neighborhood works for your household comes down to its daily driving pattern, housing stock, recreation options, and ZIP 32164 rent level.

Who Seminole Woods fits best

A residential routine built around a car

Seminole Woods occupies the southern side of Palm Coast, where Seminole Woods Boulevard and Sesame Boulevard are familiar reference points. The streets within the neighborhood are primarily residential. Your lease may put you close to a park, while groceries, medical appointments, restaurants, and most workplaces will generally require a drive.

This arrangement works well for renters who prefer a house with a driveway, garage, or yard over a downtown apartment. It is less convenient for someone who wants to walk from home to coffee, dinner, and work. Palm Coast has paths and recreational trails, though they do not form a complete pedestrian network for daily errands.

Who should compare another area

Look closer to Town Center if you want the shortest practical connection to shopping, City Hall, community events, and the hospital area. Compare northern Palm Coast if work or regular appointments take you toward St. Augustine. If living beside the Atlantic matters more than having an inland residential address, compare Flagler Beach and account for its different housing mix and coastal conditions.

For a broader view before choosing a section, browse the Palm Coast rental guides. Then evaluate the actual address instead of relying on the neighborhood name alone.

July 2026 rent context for ZIP 32164

What the Zillow figure measures

The Zillow Observed Rent Index placed the typical rent for single-family homes and condominiums in ZIP 32164 at $1,892.60 in July 2026. That was up 0.06% from June and 0.50% from July 2025.

This is a ZIP-level index. It is neither Seminole Woods' asking rent nor a promise that a particular home should lease for that amount. Bedrooms, square footage, age, fencing, garage space, included lawn service, pet terms, and condition can all move an individual asking rent away from the ZIP benchmark.

How to use the benchmark during a search

Compare homes by the full monthly obligation, not the advertised rent alone. Put lawn care, pest control, pet rent, utility responsibility, renters insurance, and any recurring resident fee on the same worksheet. A home priced below the ZIP benchmark can still be the more expensive choice if the tenant must pay for several services.

Compare like with like as well. A duplex unit and a detached house may have the same bedroom count but differ in yard responsibility, sound separation, parking, and utility setup. Current options across Palm Coast can be reviewed on the rental properties page; always confirm availability and prices before relying on a listing.

Housing and street layout

What renters are likely to tour

Renters are likely to encounter detached houses and duplex-style rentals, including newer construction on streets where neighboring lots may still be developing. Floor plans commonly have practical suburban features such as attached garages, open living areas, covered patios, and yards. None of those features should be assumed from the section name.

A nearby ZIP 32164 example is this three-bedroom Palm Coast rental. It shows the kind of space and layout renters may compare, but it is not evidence of a Seminole Woods market price or current neighborhood availability.

The block matters as much as the section

Drive the exact street after work and again on a weekend. Listen for traffic from Seminole Woods Boulevard, Belle Terre Parkway, State Road 100, or I-95 where relevant. Check the street lighting, driveway width, practicality of guest parking, nearby construction, and how water moves along the swales after rain.

A quiet showing in the middle of the day can hide school-run or commuting traffic. Before applying, repeat the route at the hour you would normally leave home. That test will tell you more than a generic online commute estimate.

Parks and recreation close to daily life

Seminole Woods Neighborhood Park

Seminole Woods Neighborhood Park is the neighborhood's clearest everyday amenity. The city lists a playground, lighted tennis court, basketball court, multipurpose field, covered picnic space, restrooms, parking, trail access, and a half-mile perimeter walking trail. Posted hours are 7 a.m. to 10 p.m. daily.

The park works for a quick walk, pickup game, playground visit, or casual picnic without making recreation a beach-day project. Proximity varies considerably within the section, so map the rental address instead of assuming the park is walkable from every Seminole Woods home.

Southern Recreation Center and Lehigh Trail

On Belle Terre Parkway, the Southern Recreation Center provides a more structured recreation option. The city lists 15 clay tennis courts, 12 pickleball courts, including covered and uncovered courts, multipurpose rooms, locker rooms, food service, and access beside the Lehigh Trailhead.

Court use, lessons, passes, and reservation rules can change, so check the facility page before planning a weekly routine around a particular program. It is also worth remembering that "nearby" in southern Palm Coast usually means a short drive and may not mean a sidewalk trip.

Town Center's open space

Central Park in Town Center has open event space, restrooms, a fountain, trail access, and a half-mile brick walking path. The city identifies it as a venue for community events, so check the municipal calendar when planning a visit.

Commutes and regional access

Town Center and I-95

Seminole Woods sits south of Town Center and west of the coastal communities. The usual local route reaches State Road 100 for Town Center, the hospital area, shopping, or the I-95 interchange. From there, I-95 is the north-south connection toward Daytona Beach or St. Augustine.

The route is straightforward but depends on a few major corridors. The city has identified the State Road 100 segment from I-95 to Memorial Medical Parkway as over capacity, so a mileage estimate should not be treated as a dependable rush-hour prediction.

Flagler Beach, Daytona Beach, and St. Augustine

For Flagler Beach, the practical route generally runs east on State Road 100 toward the Intracoastal Waterway and State Road A1A. Daytona Beach lies south via I-95, while St. Augustine lies north. A Seminole Woods address can work for occasional regional trips, but a daily commute calls for a live test in both directions.

If you must complete a route at a fixed hour several days each week, drive it before signing. Check an ordinary weekday morning and afternoon, include the time needed to reach the interstate, and notice whether school traffic or a left turn from the rental's street changes the result.

Employment corridors

Town Center and the State Road 100 corridor contain several useful destinations. The city's State Road 100 Community Redevelopment Agency page identifies the area as a mix of business and residential uses and specifically notes Palm Coast Landings, AdventHealth Palm Coast, City Hall, office space, and entertainment.

For jobs outside Palm Coast, the interstate is the main regional connector. Seminole Woods may suit a hybrid worker who occasionally travels to Daytona Beach or St. Augustine. Someone commuting every weekday should judge the house by the full door-to-door trip, not its apparent proximity to I-95 on a map.

Shopping, healthcare, and dining

What is concentrated near State Road 100

Town Center is the most practical starting point for larger shopping trips and several services. The city identifies Palm Coast Landings and its Super Target, AdventHealth Palm Coast, City Hall, and Epic Theatres among the district's established destinations. Other businesses change over time, so confirm the exact store, pharmacy, clinic, or restaurant you rely on.

Seminole Woods is primarily a residential base, not an all-in-one commercial district. Combining errands can save time. Groceries, prescriptions, medical visits, and dinner can often be handled during one State Road 100 or Town Center trip.

Build a personal essentials map

Before committing, enter the prospective address in your preferred map application and save routes to work, your grocery store, pharmacy, urgent care, veterinarian, and the beach access you would actually use. Check business hours and whether your medical providers accept your insurance. A pin marked "healthcare" is not useful if it is the wrong specialty or network.

  • Confirm the closest acceptable grocery store, not merely the closest market.
  • Check after-hours pharmacy and urgent-care options.
  • Test restaurant delivery to the exact address.
  • Ask where oversized deliveries can be left without blocking the driveway.
  • Confirm trash and recycling procedures with the manager and city.

School-zone due diligence

Never rely on a neighborhood label

Families should not assume that every Seminole Woods address feeds the same schools. Flagler Schools states that assignments are determined by the parent or legal guardian's address and provides a SchoolSite Locator that can search an exact address.

Use the complete street address, including the unit designation for a duplex. Save the result, then contact Flagler Schools if enrollment timing, transportation, walk zones, special programs, or a boundary near the property could affect the move.

Verify again before signing

School boundaries and programs can change. Do not treat a listing agent, owner, neighbor, or old advertisement as the final authority. Ask the district to confirm the assigned schools for the lease start date, and decide according to your household's needs rather than an unsupported claim that one school is "best."

If transportation matters, verify bus eligibility and stop information separately. The locator provides planning information, while the district can confirm the exact bus arrangement before you count on it for the morning commute.

Planning and construction checks

Start with the city's live tools

Palm Coast changes street by street as vacant parcels develop and infrastructure work moves forward. The city's Planning and Zoning division reviews development plans and provides a development-project search. Its Capital Projects Dashboard is another place to check for public work that may affect routes or nearby land.

Search around the rental, the route to State Road 100, and the roads you will use for work or school. A project elsewhere in Palm Coast may have no bearing on your routine, while a smaller drainage or road project beside your daily turn can have an immediate effect.

Watch the key intersections

The city reports that it is pursuing an intersection analysis at Belle Terre Parkway and Seminole Woods Boulevard along State Road 100, and that Flagler County provided approximately $380,000 for improvements at Seminole Woods Boulevard and State Road 100. Check the current project status, since this does not guarantee timing or future congestion.

The Citation Boulevard extension now links Belle Terre Boulevard and Seminole Woods Boulevard, according to the city's project announcement. When touring, compare the route suggested by your navigation app with the route that feels practical on local streets during busy hours.

A Seminole Woods rental-tour checklist

Inside the home

  • Run the air conditioner and note airflow in every bedroom.
  • Look under sinks and around windows for staining, active moisture, or recent repairs.
  • Test water pressure, hot water, appliances, locks, garage controls, and exterior lights.
  • Ask which internet providers serve the exact address. Verify the plan and installation date directly with the provider.
  • Confirm whether the home shares a wall, meter, mailbox, driveway, or irrigation system.

Yard, drainage, and lease responsibilities

  • Walk the swales and low areas. Ask how the property drains after a heavy Florida rain.
  • Identify who handles mowing, edging, irrigation, weeds, shrubs, and pest control.
  • Confirm fence ownership and pet rules in writing.
  • Ask about watering schedules and what happens if an irrigation component fails.
  • Photograph existing exterior wear during the documented move-in inspection.

Storm preparation

September falls within Atlantic hurricane season, so ask storm questions before there is a watch or warning. Confirm who supplies and installs shutters, where panels or fasteners are stored, how emergency notices are delivered, and what the lease requires if an evacuation order is issued.

Renters insurance ordinarily protects tenant belongings subject to the policy, not the building itself. Ask the insurer specifically about wind, water intrusion, temporary living expenses, deductibles, and flood coverage. Keep a portable inventory of medications, documents, pet supplies, chargers, and the property manager's emergency contact information.

FAQ

Is Seminole Woods a good fit for renters without a car?

Usually not. The area is primarily residential, and most grocery trips, medical appointments, restaurant visits, beach outings, and regional commutes are more practical by car.

What was the typical rent in ZIP 32164 in July 2026?

Zillow's Observed Rent Index reported $1,892.60 for single-family homes and condominiums in ZIP 32164. That was a ZIP-wide benchmark, not a quoted rent for every Seminole Woods home.

What recreation is available near Seminole Woods?

Seminole Woods Neighborhood Park has a playground, sports courts, a multipurpose field, picnic space, restrooms, and a half-mile walking trail. The Southern Recreation Center adds tennis, pickleball, programming space, and Lehigh Trail access.

Which schools serve a Seminole Woods rental?

School assignments depend on the exact home address. Enter the complete address in Flagler Schools' SchoolSite Locator and confirm the result with the district before signing a lease.

What should I inspect during a Seminole Woods rental tour?

Check internet service, air-conditioning performance, drainage and swales, lawn and irrigation duties, pest-control responsibility, parking, nearby construction, and the lease's storm-preparation procedures.
