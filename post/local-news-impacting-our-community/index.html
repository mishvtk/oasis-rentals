Local News Impacting Our Community

Palm Coast is buzzing with significant developments this week, each of which could influence our real estate market and community landscape.



Local Politics:

The mayoral race has narrowed down to Mike Norris and Cornelia Manfre, leading to a runoff in November. As these candidates bring new perspectives to the table, their policies may shape the future of Palm Coast, potentially affecting everything from local property taxes to infrastructure investments


Criminal Activity:

A dramatic incident unfolded with the arrest of Johnathan Costa after a high-speed chase in a stolen Ferrari. While such events are rare, they highlight the importance of community safety and law enforcement’s vigilance, factors that can influence local real estate desirability.


Community Events:

The 25th Anniversary Celebration of Palm Coast on October 26 is more than just a party—it's a reflection of our city's growth and community spirit. Such events reinforce the value of our neighborhoods, making Palm Coast an even more attractive place to call home.

The opening of a new BJ's Wholesale Club and its accompanying shopping plaza signifies economic growth and expanding amenities for Palm Coast residents. This development, along with ongoing annexations, points to a vibrant and growing community—key factors for real estate investors and homebuyers.

While Palm Coast remains safe, nearby incidents remind us of the importance of community vigilance. Public safety is always a top concern for residents and potential buyers alike.
