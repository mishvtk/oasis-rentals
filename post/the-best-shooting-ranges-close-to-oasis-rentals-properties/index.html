The Best Shooting Ranges Close to Oasis Rentals Properties

Whether you’re a seasoned marksman or just starting to explore the world of firearms, South Florida offers excellent shooting ranges to meet your needs. Near Oasis Rentals properties in Palm Coast, Deerfield Beach, and Boca Raton, you’ll find state-of-the-art facilities catering to a variety of shooting styles, from handguns to rifles and even archery. These ranges are perfect for honing your skills, practicing safe firearm use, or simply enjoying a unique recreational experience.


1. Hot Shot Shooting Range (Deerfield Beach)

Located just minutes from Deerfield Beach, Hot Shot Shooting Range offers an indoor facility with well-maintained lanes and professional staff. The range accommodates handguns, rifles, and shotguns, and also provides firearm rentals and training courses for beginners.

  • Special Features: Safety-focused training classes and a retail store with a wide selection of firearms and accessories.



2. Nexus Shooting (Davie - Near Deerfield Beach)

For an advanced shooting experience, Nexus Shooting is worth the short drive from Deerfield Beach. This cutting-edge range features a dynamic targeting system and live-fire simulators for an immersive shooting session. Whether you’re a recreational shooter or training for defense, Nexus Shooting offers a high-tech experience.

  • Special Features: Dynamic targeting system and live-fire virtual simulators.

3. The Best Palm Coast Indoor Shooting Range (Palm Coast) Close to Oasis rentals Properies

For residents or visitors staying in Palm Coast, this indoor shooting range is a convenient and safe environment for firearm enthusiasts. The facility offers lanes for pistols and rifles, along with firearm rentals, ammunition sales, and concealed carry classes.

  • Special Features: Friendly staff and affordable membership options for frequent visitors.

4. Gun World of South Florida (Deerfield Beach)

Gun World is a popular choice among Deerfield Beach locals for its well-lit indoor range and extensive retail space. With 15 lanes available, shooters of all skill levels can enjoy a comfortable and professional experience. They also offer firearm safety courses and training programs.

  • Special Features: Large retail section and private shooting lessons.

5. Big Gun Range (Homestead - Near Boca Raton)

A short drive from Boca Raton, Big Gun Range is an outdoor facility offering shooting lanes for handguns, rifles, and shotguns. This range provides a unique experience with its open-air setting, perfect for those who prefer outdoor shooting.

  • Special Features: Outdoor setting and a variety of targets for long-range shooting.



6. Okeechobee Shooting Sports (Palm Coast)

If you’re staying near Palm Coast and enjoy a full day of shooting sports, Okeechobee Shooting Sports is worth the trip. This sprawling outdoor range features lanes for rifles, pistols, and shotguns, as well as a dedicated archery area. Their events and competitions attract enthusiasts from across Florida.

  • Special Features: Long-distance rifle ranges and professional competitions.



Tips for a Safe and Enjoyable Shooting Experience

  1. Know the Rules: Each range has specific safety guidelines. Take time to familiarize yourself with them before starting.

  2. Wear Proper Gear: Use eye and ear protection at all times while on the range.

  3. Bring Necessary Documentation: Some ranges require identification or proof of firearms ownership.

  4. Take a Class: If you’re a beginner, consider enrolling in a safety course or guided training session.

  5. Book Ahead: Popular ranges can get busy, so reserving a lane in advance is recommended.
