Top Hospitals Near Deerfield Beach

Hospitals in and around Deerfield Beach, Florida, offer residents access to high-quality healthcare, ensuring they are well-served for both routine and emergency medical needs. Below is a list of prominent hospitals near Deerfield Beach, along with the key services they offer.


1. Broward Health North

Broward Health North is a full-service medical facility that serves the Deerfield Beach community. As a Level II Trauma Center, it provides advanced emergency care, including specialized services for critical injuries. The hospital also offers comprehensive care in cardiology, neurology, orthopedic surgery, and cancer treatment.

Location: 201 E. Sample Road, Deerfield Beach, FL 33064

Key Services: Trauma care, cardiovascular services, stroke care, rehabilitation, cancer treatment, and surgical services.


2. West Boca Medical Center

West Boca Medical Center is known for its wide range of healthcare services, especially its maternity and pediatric care. The facility also provides specialized treatments for cardiac conditions and features a state-of-the-art surgical center.

Location: 21644 FL-7, Boca Raton, FL 33428 (approximately 15 minutes from Deerfield Beach)

Key Services: Maternity and women’s health, pediatric care, cardiology, robotic surgery, and emergency services.


3. Holy Cross Health

Holy Cross Health is another top hospital near Deerfield Beach. It offers high-level care for cancer, cardiovascular diseases, and orthopedic needs. With a focus on technology and innovation, Holy Cross provides minimally invasive surgeries and comprehensive rehabilitation services.

Location: 4725 N Federal Hwy, Fort Lauderdale, FL 33308 (approximately 20 minutes south of Deerfield Beach)

Key Services: Cancer treatment, orthopedic and joint care, cardiology, women's health, and outpatient services.


4. Boca Raton Regional Hospital

Part of Baptist Health South Florida, Boca Raton Regional Hospital is recognized for its excellence in cancer care, cardiovascular services, and women’s health. It is equipped with advanced technology, including robotic surgery, and offers specialized care in various medical fields.

Location: 800 Meadows Road, Boca Raton, FL 33486 (about 10 minutes from Deerfield Beach)

Key Services: Cancer care, cardiology, neurology, women’s health services, and robotic surgery.


5. Northwest Medical Center

Northwest Medical Center is a healthcare provider known for its focus on spine care, cardiac services, and emergency medicine. The hospital also features a specialized women’s center, offering maternity and neonatal care.

Location: 2801 N State Rd 7, Margate, FL 33063 (about 20 minutes from Deerfield Beach)

Key Services: Spine and orthopedic care, cardiac services, emergency care, maternity services, and surgical treatments.


Deerfield Beach residents are fortunate to be within easy reach of several top-rated hospitals and medical centers. Whether you need routine medical care, specialized treatment, or emergency services, the healthcare options in this area are both accessible and comprehensive.
