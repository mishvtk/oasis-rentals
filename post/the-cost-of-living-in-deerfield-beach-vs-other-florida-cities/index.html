The Cost of Living in Deerfield Beach vs. Other Florida Cities

Deerfield Beach, located in South Florida, is known for its pristine beaches, thriving cultural scene, and relaxed coastal lifestyle. But how does the cost of living here compare to other cities across Florida? Whether you're considering relocating to Deerfield Beach or just curious about how it stacks up against other popular areas, understanding the local cost of living is essential for making an informed decision.

In this article, we’ll explore how the cost of living in Deerfield Beach compares to other Florida cities like Miami, Orlando, Tampa, and Palm Coast, breaking down key factors such as housing, transportation, groceries, and entertainment.


Housing Costs of Living in Deerfield Beach

Housing is often the largest component of the cost of living, and prices vary widely depending on the city. In Deerfield Beach, housing costs are relatively affordable compared to nearby coastal cities like Miami but slightly higher than more inland areas such as Orlando.

  • Deerfield Beach: The median home price in Deerfield Beach hovers around $350,000 with average rent for a one-bedroom apartment ranging from $1,600 to $2,000 per month, depending on the neighborhood .

  • Miami: In Miami, one of the most expensive cities in Florida, the median home price exceeds $550,000, and rent for a one-bedroom apartment can range from $2,200 to $3,000 per month.

  • Orlando: Known for its relatively affordable housing market, Orlando offers a median home price of approximately $400,000, and rent for a one-bedroom averages around $1,500 to $1,800.

  • Tampa: Tampa’s housing costs are slightly higher than Orlando, with the median home price around $450,000, and typical one-bedroom rents ranging from $1,800 to $2,200 per month.

  • Palm Coast: A smaller city, Palm Coast offers more affordable housing with a median home price of around $325,000 and rents for one-bedroom apartments ranging between $1,400 and $1,700.

Overall, Deerfield Beach offers more affordable housing options compared to major metro areas like Miami and Tampa, while still providing the benefits of living in a vibrant coastal community.


Transportation Costs

Transportation is another significant factor in the cost of living. In Deerfield Beach, most residents rely on cars for their daily commute, and fuel costs are on par with other cities in South Florida. However, access to public transportation is more limited compared to larger cities like Miami or Orlando.

  • Deerfield Beach: With an average commute time of 25 minutes, the cost of transportation (including gas and car maintenance) averages about $3,000 annually per household. Public transit options are limited to Broward County Transit buses, which cost $2 per ride or $70 for a monthly pass.

  • Miami: Miami offers more extensive public transit options, including the Metrorail and Metrobus systems. While public transit is more accessible, the cost of car ownership and insurance in Miami is significantly higher due to traffic congestion.

  • Orlando: Like Deerfield Beach, most people in Orlando drive, but the city offers a growing SunRail system for commuters. Transportation costs are similar, but parking can be expensive in downtown areas.

  • Tampa: Tampa’s transportation system is similar to Deerfield Beach, with most residents relying on cars. However, public transit options such as the HART bus system offer more routes and lower costs.

  • Palm Coast: Palm Coast residents also rely heavily on cars, with minimal public transit options, but transportation costs are relatively low due to shorter commutes and lower congestion.

In summary, transportation costs in Deerfield Beach are comparable to other mid-sized Florida cities, though public transit is more limited.


Groceries and Dining Out

Groceries and dining costs are crucial parts of daily life, and they can differ significantly across Florida’s cities. In Deerfield Beach, grocery costs are about average for South Florida, but dining out can be more affordable compared to pricier cities like Miami.

  • Deerfield Beach: A typical grocery bill for a family in Deerfield Beach will cost around $350 to $400 per month. Dining out is affordable, with the average meal at a mid-range restaurant costing $15 to $20 per person.

  • Miami: Grocery costs in Miami tend to be slightly higher, with a family spending around $400 to $450 per month. Dining out in Miami is also more expensive, averaging $20 to $30 per person at a mid-range restaurant.

  • Orlando: Grocery prices in Orlando are similar to Deerfield Beach, but dining out is slightly more affordable, with meals costing around $12 to $18 per person.

  • Tampa: Tampa has slightly higher grocery costs, with families spending approximately $400 per month, but dining out remains fairly affordable at around $15 to $22 per meal.

  • Palm Coast: Palm Coast boasts some of the lowest grocery costs, with families spending around $300 to $350 per month. Dining out is similarly affordable, with meals at local restaurants typically costing $12 to $15 per person.

Overall, Deerfield Beach’s grocery and dining costs are similar to other mid-sized Florida cities but are significantly lower than major metro areas like Miami.


Utilities and Healthcare Costs

Utility and healthcare costs are often overlooked but are key factors in determining the overall cost of living. Deerfield Beach’s utility costs are consistent with other South Florida cities, where air conditioning drives up electricity bills, but healthcare services tend to be slightly more affordable.

  • Deerfield Beach: Monthly utilities, including electricity, water, and garbage collection, average around $150 to $200, especially in the summer when air conditioning use peaks. Healthcare costs in Deerfield Beach are moderate, with a standard doctor’s visit averaging $120 without insurance.

  • Miami: Utilities in Miami can run higher, averaging $200 to $250 per month due to larger homes and higher demand for air conditioning. Healthcare is also more expensive, with costs similar to Deerfield Beach but slightly inflated due to Miami’s size.

  • Orlando: Utilities in Orlando are comparable to Deerfield Beach, averaging around $150 to $200 per month. Healthcare costs are also in the mid-range, with doctor visits averaging $100 to $120.

  • Tampa: Utility costs in Tampa are similar, with average monthly bills ranging from $150 to $200. Healthcare is moderately priced, with doctor’s visits typically costing $100 to $120.

  • Palm Coast: Utility costs are slightly lower in Palm Coast, averaging around $120 to $180 per month. Healthcare costs here are also among the most affordable, with doctor visits costing approximately $100.

In general, Deerfield Beach offers competitive utility and healthcare costs, comparable to other mid-sized Florida cities but lower than the larger metro areas.


Entertainment and Recreation

Deerfield Beach is known for its outdoor lifestyle, and recreational activities here are relatively affordable compared to cities like Miami and Tampa. With its beautiful beaches, parks, and cultural events, residents can enjoy a rich lifestyle without breaking the bank.

  • Deerfield Beach: Access to public beaches is free, and outdoor activities like hiking and biking are available year-round. Movie tickets cost about $12, and fitness memberships average $30 to $50 per month.

  • Miami: Miami offers a wide range of entertainment options, from high-end clubs to cultural attractions, but costs can add up. Fitness memberships in Miami often cost more, averaging $50 to $70 per month.

  • Orlando: Orlando, with its world-class theme parks, offers unique recreational activities but at a higher cost. For example, a day at Disney World or Universal Studios can easily cost $100 per person.

  • Tampa: Tampa offers a mix of affordable and higher-end entertainment options, with fitness memberships costing $40 to $60 per month and movie tickets averaging $13.

  • Palm Coast: Palm Coast offers outdoor activities like kayaking and beach visits at low or no cost, with fitness memberships averaging $30 to $40 per month.

Deerfield Beach stands out for offering a balanced mix of affordable recreation options, making it an appealing city for families and outdoor enthusiasts.


When comparing the cost of living in Deerfield Beach to other Florida cities, it offers a middle ground in affordability. While it is more affordable than larger metro areas like Miami and Tampa, it remains slightly more expensive than inland cities like Orlando and Palm Coast. Deerfield Beach is perfect for those seeking a coastal lifestyle with access to great amenities, all at a more reasonable cost compared to its larger neighbors.

Ultimately, Deerfield Beach provides an attractive option for families, professionals, and retirees looking for a vibrant beach community without the high costs associated with other South Florida cities.
