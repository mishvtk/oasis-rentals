The Cost of Living in Deerfield Beach vs. Other Florida Cities

Deerfield Beach, nestled on Florida’s southeastern coast, offers residents a relaxed lifestyle with beautiful beaches, outdoor activities, and easy access to the greater Miami-Fort Lauderdale metro area. For renters and prospective residents, comparing Deerfield Beach's cost of living to other popular Florida cities, such as Miami, Orlando, and Boca Raton, can provide valuable insights. Here’s a detailed look at what to expect in terms of housing, utilities, food, transportation, and entertainment costs in Deerfield Beach versus other cities.


1. Housing Costs of Living in Deerfield Beach vs. Other Florida Cities

Housing is typically the most significant expense, and in Deerfield Beach, it remains relatively affordable compared to other coastal cities in Florida. The median rent for a one-bedroom apartment in Deerfield Beach is approximately $1,700 per month, lower than in Boca Raton, where similar apartments average around $2,200, and significantly lower than Miami, where one-bedroom rents often exceed $2,500 (Zumper, 2023). Compared to Orlando, where the median one-bedroom rent is around $1,800, Deerfield Beach offers a competitive option without the hustle and bustle of a major city.



Property values and real estate taxes in Deerfield Beach are also generally more affordable than in neighboring cities. As a result, property management companies, like Oasis Rentals, can offer competitive rental rates, particularly in comparison to high-demand areas like Boca Raton and Miami. This affordability makes Deerfield Beach attractive for those looking for a more budget-friendly coastal lifestyle.


2. Utilities and Maintenance

Utility costs in Deerfield Beach vs. other Florida cities are comparable, with minor regional variations. According to Numbeo (2023), average monthly utility bills (including electricity, heating, cooling, water, and garbage) for a standard apartment in Deerfield Beach come to around $160, similar to utility costs in Boca Raton and Miami.

Florida’s climate demands regular air conditioning use, making electricity one of the more significant utility expenses. However, Deerfield Beach benefits from consistent sea breezes, which can occasionally reduce the need for air conditioning compared to inland cities like Orlando, where air conditioning usage is typically higher year-round.


3. Food and Dining

Grocery prices in Deerfield Beach align with the Florida average, with minor fluctuations based on local store pricing. Residents can expect grocery costs similar to those in Orlando, where a month’s worth of groceries for a single person is around $350, according to LivingCost.org (2023). Dining out in Deerfield Beach is generally more affordable than in tourist-heavy areas like Miami. A mid-range restaurant meal for two in Deerfield Beach averages around $60, while the same meal in Miami can reach $80 or more (Numbeo, 2023).

This affordability in food and dining makes Deerfield Beach an appealing option for renters who want to enjoy local cuisine and dining out without the elevated prices typical of larger cities. Residents can also enjoy the city’s local farmer’s markets and seafood restaurants, which provide fresh, local produce and seafood at reasonable prices.



4. Transportation and Commuting Costs

For those commuting to nearby cities, Deerfield Beach offers an ideal location with easy access to major highways and public transportation. The city is well-connected via Interstate 95, allowing residents to commute to neighboring cities like Fort Lauderdale or Miami efficiently. Gas prices in Deerfield Beach are comparable to the state average, at approximately $3.50 per gallon (AAA, 2023). Public transportation is available through Broward County Transit, with monthly passes costing around $70, which is consistent with public transit costs in Boca Raton and other nearby cities.

Compared to Miami, where transportation expenses tend to be higher due to increased traffic and limited parking availability, Deerfield Beach’s smaller size and efficient road network make commuting less costly and stressful. Residents also benefit from lower parking fees and fewer toll roads than in Miami.



5. Recreation and Entertainment

Deerfield Beach offers a variety of recreational options that cater to different interests, many of which are affordable or free. The city’s public beaches are a major draw, offering residents free access to some of Florida’s most beautiful shorelines. Other attractions, like the Deerfield Beach Pier and Quiet Waters Park, offer affordable entrance fees, making it easy for residents to enjoy the outdoors without breaking the bank.

In comparison, Miami’s entertainment options tend to be more expensive, with many popular activities, restaurants, and nightclubs priced at a premium. Orlando, with its numerous theme parks and attractions, is known for higher entertainment costs as well, especially for families. For renters in Deerfield Beach, the abundance of affordable recreation—from beach outings to local festivals—offers a high quality of life without the premium pricing of nearby tourist hotspots.


6. Healthcare Costs

Healthcare in Deerfield Beach is generally in line with Florida’s average, though slightly lower than in larger metropolitan areas. With accessible healthcare facilities and hospitals nearby, Deerfield Beach residents can expect moderate medical expenses compared to Miami, where costs are typically higher due to demand and a larger population.

Additionally, the availability of health services and lower local taxes contribute to making Deerfield Beach an attractive option for retirees and young families seeking affordable healthcare without sacrificing quality.



When compared to other Florida cities, Deerfield Beach stands out as a cost-effective yet vibrant place to live. While cities like Miami and Boca Raton offer a more cosmopolitan experience, they also come with higher living costs. Orlando, though more affordable than Miami, lacks the coastal charm and lifestyle Deerfield Beach provides. Deerfield Beach’s affordable housing, accessible transportation, and recreational opportunities make it an excellent choice for renters who want to enjoy the best of Florida living without the high costs associated with larger urban centers.

For prospective renters exploring Florida’s diverse cities, Deerfield Beach combines affordability with access to beaches, outdoor activities, and a close-knit community atmosphere.
